# Run-On Skits Handout

**Roaring Laughter Adventure – Requirement 5**

A **run-on** is a very short skit where Scouts "run on" to the stage, deliver a quick joke or punchline, and "run off." They're perfect for pack meetings and campfires!

---

### The Viper
- **Scout 1**: *(Runs on screaming)* "The Viper is coming! The Viper is coming!"
- **Scout 2**: *(Runs on screaming)* "The Viper is coming! Hide!"
- **Scout 3**: *(Walks on calmly with a squeegee and bucket)* "I'm the Vindow Viper. I'm here to vipe your vindows."

### Invisible Bench
- **Scout 1**: *(Sits in mid-air, pretending to sit on a bench)*
- **Scout 2**: *(Walks up)* "What are you doing?"
- **Scout 1**: "Sitting on this invisible bench."
- **Scout 2**: "Can I sit too?"
- **Scout 1**: "Sure!" *(Scout 2 sits)*
- **Scout 3**: *(Walks up)* "What are YOU doing?"
- **Scout 2**: "Sitting on this invisible bench."
- **Scout 3**: "Can I sit too?" *(Scout 3 sits)*
- **Scout 4**: *(Walks up)* "Hey! What are you all doing?"
- **All 3**: "Sitting on this invisible bench!"
- **Scout 4**: "What invisible bench? I moved it over there!" *(Points away)*
- *(All 3 fall down)*

### Is It Time Yet?
- **Scout 1**: *(Sitting down, looking bored)* "Is it time yet?"
- **Scout 2**: *(Checks watch)* "No, not yet."
- *(Wait 5 seconds)*
- **Scout 1**: "Is it time yet?"
- **Scout 2**: "No, not yet."
- *(Wait 5 seconds)*
- **Scout 1**: "Is it time yet?"
- **Scout 2**: "Yes! It's time!"
- **Both**: *(Stand up and yell)* "It's time to run off!" *(Run off stage)*

### The Lost Quarter
- **Scout 1**: *(Crawling on the ground, searching)*
- **Scout 2**: *(Walks up)* "What are you looking for?"
- **Scout 1**: "I lost a quarter over by that tree."
- **Scout 2**: "Then why are you looking over here?"
- **Scout 1**: "The light is better here!"

### The Hike
- **Scout 1**: *(Walks across stage slowly, exhausted)*
- **Scout 2**: *(Following)* "Are we there yet?"
- **Scout 1**: "No."
- **Scout 2**: "Are we there yet?"
- **Scout 1**: "No."
- **Scout 2**: "Are we there yet?"
- **Scout 1**: "YES! We're here!"
- **Scout 2**: "Great! ...Where are we?"
- **Scout 1**: "I have no idea." *(Both walk off confused)*

### The Bug Juice
- **Scout 1**: *(Drinking from a cup)* "Mmm, this bug juice is great!"
- **Scout 2**: "Can I have some?"
- **Scout 1**: "Sure!" *(Hands over cup)*
- **Scout 2**: *(Drinks, then spits it out)* "This tastes terrible! What flavor is it?"
- **Scout 1**: "Bug flavor. What did you think bug juice was?"

### Waiter! Waiter!
- **Scout 1**: "Waiter! Waiter! There's a fly in my soup!"
- **Scout 2** (Waiter): "Don't worry, the spider in your salad will get it."
- *(Scout 1 faints)*

### Waiter! Waiter! 2
- **Scout 1**: "Waiter! Waiter! What's this fly doing in my soup?"
- **Scout 2**: *(Looks closely)* "Looks like the backstroke!"

### Doctor! Doctor!
- **Scout 1**: *(Runs on)* "Doctor! Doctor! I feel like a pair of curtains!"
- **Scout 2** (Doctor): "Pull yourself together!"
- *(Scout 1 runs off, Scout 3 runs on)*
- **Scout 3**: "Doctor! Doctor! I think I'm a bell!"
- **Scout 2**: "Take these pills, and if they don't work, give me a ring!"
- *(Scout 3 runs off, Scout 4 runs on)*
- **Scout 4**: "Doctor! Doctor! Everyone ignores me!"
- **Scout 2**: *(Looks right past Scout 4)* "NEXT!"

### Broken Arm
- **Scout 1**: *(Holding arm)* "Doctor, I broke my arm in two places!"
- **Scout 2** (Doctor): "Well, stop going to those places!"

### The Rope
- **Scout 1**: *(Pulling on an invisible rope)*
- **Scout 2**: *(Walks up)* "What are you doing?"
- **Scout 1**: "Pulling this rope!"
- **Scout 2**: "Why?"
- **Scout 1**: "Because if I push it, nothing happens!"

### Smoke Signals
- **Scout 1**: *(Waving blanket over imaginary fire)*
- **Scout 2**: "What message are you sending?"
- **Scout 1**: "Help! Our camp is on fire!"
- **Scout 2**: "But our camp isn't on fire."
- **Scout 1**: *(Looks down)* "It is now!"
- *(Both run off)*

### The Canteen
- **Scout 1**: *(Crawling across stage)* "Water... water..."
- **Scout 2**: *(Walks up with canteen)* "Here, have some water!"
- **Scout 1**: *(Takes canteen, pours it on head)* "Ahhhh, much better."
- **Scout 2**: "You were supposed to drink it!"
- **Scout 1**: "Oh. Got any more?"
