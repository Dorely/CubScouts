# Completion Checklist

- Headings: exactly one H1; logical `##/###` hierarchy; reuse standard section names.
- Reference alignment: check `reference/required/` or `reference/elective/` for correct requirement names and numbering.
- Lists: use `-` bullets; concise items; consistent ordering with similar lessons.
- Links: prefer relative paths; quick scan for `http` links and obvious typos; verify repo-relative paths exist.
- Spelling/typos: skim edited sections for common mistakes.
- Consistency: match tone and structure of adjacent lessons.
- Whitespace: ensure trailing newline; avoid trailing spaces; preserve existing spacing outside edits.
- Scope: keep diffs minimal and focused on the requested change.
