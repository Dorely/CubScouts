# Lesson Style Guide

- Headings: ATX (`#`, `##`, `###`), one `#` H1 per file.
- File naming: Title_Case with underscores, e.g., `Bear_Habitat_Lesson_Plan.md`.
- Lists: `-` for bullets; concise phrases; consistent order.
- Links: use relative paths within repo when possible; full URLs only if external.
- Tone: clear, actionable, age-appropriate for Cub Scouts; match existing lesson voice.

## Recommended Lesson Headings
- `# <Adventure Name> Lesson Plan`
- `**Estimated Time to Complete: ...**` (one or two meetings)
- `## ✅ Requirements Checklist`
  - `### Meeting 1 Requirements:`
  - `### Meeting 2 Requirements:` (as needed)
- `---` (divider)
- `## 🛒 Shopping & Materials List`
  - `### Items to Purchase:` (with cost ranges)
  - `### Items You May Already Have:`
  - `### **Total Estimated Cost: $X–Y**`
  - `**💡 Cost-Saving Tips:**` (bulleted)
- `---`
- `## Meeting 1: <Theme> (60 minutes)`
  - `### Opening (5 minutes)`
  - `### Activities`
    - `#### Activity 1: <Title> (N minutes)`
      - Include which requirements are covered and concise steps
    - Additional activities as needed
  - `### Closing (5 minutes)`
- `---`
- `## Meeting 2: <Theme> (60 minutes)` (if applicable)
  - Same sub-structure as Meeting 1
- `## Materials Needed`
- `## Safety Notes`
- `## Take-Home Elements` (optional)
- `## Extension Opportunities` (optional)
- `## Special Notes` (optional)

## Reference Alignment
- Before editing lessons, review related items in `reference/required/` or `reference/elective/` to align terminology and requirements.
- Reuse requirement numbering and phrasing from the reference files.

## Formatting Basics
- Maintain logical `##/###` hierarchy; avoid inventing new headings unnecessarily.
- Preserve existing spacing; avoid reflowing unrelated text.
- Ensure a trailing newline; avoid trailing spaces.
