## Lesson Objectives
By the end of this meeting, scouts will:
- Sample foods from 3 different food groups
- Create a short workout exploring different exercises
- Participate in a 10-minute relaxing activity
- Understand personal fitness includes body and mind
- Learn about eating right, exercising, and getting rest

## Materials Needed
- Healthy snacks from all 5 food groups (protein, vegetables, fruits, dairy, grains)
- Fitness tracking sheets for home use
- Stopwatch or timer
- Yoga mats or towels for stretching
- Soft background music for relaxation
- Water bottles for hydration
- Bear Strong adventure requirements checklist

## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome:** "Today we become strong like bears!"
- **Bear Facts:** Bears can run 30 mph, swim well, and climb trees - they're incredibly fit!
- **Goal Setting:** "We'll learn how to keep our bodies strong and healthy"
- **Opening:** Scout Oath emphasizing "physically strong"

### Activity 1: Nutrition Exploration (12 minutes)
**Requirement:** Sample food from three of the following food groups: protein, vegetables, fruits, dairy, and grains

**Food Group Stations:**
1. **Protein Station:** Turkey roll-ups, cheese cubes, nuts (check allergies)
2. **Vegetables Station:** Carrots, bell peppers, cherry tomatoes
3. **Fruits Station:** Apple slices, grapes, berries
4. **Dairy Station:** String cheese, yogurt cups, milk
5. **Grains Station:** Whole grain crackers, granola pieces

**Activity:**
- Scouts rotate through stations
- Taste foods from at least 3 different groups
- Discuss: "Why do bears (and scouts) need different types of food?"
- Learn: Each food group helps our body in different ways
- Track on individual sheets which groups they sampled

### Activity 2: Create Your Bear Workout (25 minutes)
**Requirement:** Create a short workout to explore different exercises

**Warm-Up Stretches (5 minutes):**
- Bear stretch: Down on all fours, stretch like waking up
- Tree reaching: Stretch arms up high like a bear reaching for honey
- Fish catching: Side stretches like a bear reaching into a stream
- Hibernation curls: Gentle spine stretches

**Movement Circuit - 4 Stations (15 minutes total):**

**Station 1: Bear Movements (4 minutes)**
- Bear crawls forward and backward
- Bear walks (hands and feet only)
- "Climbing trees" - reach high while walking

**Station 2: Salmon Fishing (4 minutes)**
- Jumping to "catch salmon" (jumping jacks)
- Quick footwork like fishing in a stream
- Balance challenges on one foot

**Station 3: Forest Navigation (4 minutes)**
- Weaving through "trees" (cones)
- Quick direction changes
- Low crawling under "branches"

**Station 4: Bear Strength (3 minutes)**
- Modified push-ups (knees down okay)
- "Digging" motions for arm strength
- Core strengthening with bear positions

**Cool-Down Stretches (5 minutes):**
- Return to gentle stretching
- Deep breathing exercises
- Prepare for personal exercise time

### Activity 3: Test Your Bear Workout (15 minutes)
**Activity:** Demonstrate the short workouts created by scouts

**Workout Demonstration:**
Each scout (or small group) demonstrates their created workout:

**Exercise Variety Includes:**
- **Cardio exercises:** jumping jacks, bear crawls, running in place
- **Strength exercises:** modified push-ups, squats, planks
- **Flexibility exercises:** stretching, yoga poses, balance challenges
- **Fun variations:** animal movements, dance moves, games

**Group Participation:**
- Scouts take turns leading their workout
- Everyone tries each workout for 2-3 minutes
- Celebrate creativity and different exercise types
- Discuss which exercises work different parts of the body

### Activity 4: Relaxation Activity (10 minutes)
**Requirement:** Do a relaxing activity for 10 minutes

**Guided Bear Relaxation:**
- Scouts lie down on mats or sit comfortably
- Soft background music if available
- Guide through progressive relaxation:

"Close your eyes and imagine you're a bear finding the perfect spot to rest in the forest. Feel the soft ground beneath you. Take slow, deep breaths like a sleeping bear - in for 4 counts, out for 4 counts.

Starting with your toes, let each part of your body relax like a bear settling down for a peaceful rest. Your legs are heavy and calm. Your arms are loose and comfortable. Your whole body feels peaceful and strong.

Listen to the sounds of the forest around you. Feel proud of how strong and healthy you've made your body today. You are a strong, capable bear scout."

**Gentle Wake-Up:**
- Slowly wiggle fingers and toes
- Gentle stretches
- Sit up slowly when ready

### Closing Circle (3 minutes)

**Reflection Questions:**
- "Which food group was your favorite to try?"
- "What exercise made you feel strongest?"
- "How do you feel after our relaxation time?"

**Take-Home Materials:**
- Fitness tracking logs for family use
- Healthy snack ideas list
- Physical activity suggestions for home

**Next Week Preview:**
- "Next week we'll finish Bear Strong by talking about healthy habits and completing our fitness logs!"

**Closing:**
- Scout sign
- "A Bear Scout is physically strong!"
- Remind: drink water and get good rest tonight

## Notes for Den Leader

### Safety Considerations:
- Check for food allergies before food sampling
- Ensure adequate water breaks during activities
- Monitor exertion levels - emphasize personal best, not competition
- Have first aid kit available
- Modify exercises for any physical limitations

### Adaptations:
- **Indoor/Outdoor:** All activities work in either setting
- **Different Abilities:** Emphasize effort over performance
- **Small Spaces:** Reduce movement area but maintain activity variety

### Food Allergy Management:
- Survey families beforehand about allergies
- Have alternative foods available
- Consider bringing pictures of foods if actual foods aren't safe
- Ensure proper hand washing between food stations

### Assessment Notes:
- ✅ Sampled from 3+ food groups
- ✅ Created and tested short workout exploring different exercises
- ✅ Engaged in 10-minute relaxation activity
