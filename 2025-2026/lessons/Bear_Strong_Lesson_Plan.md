# Bear Strong Adventure Lesson Plan

**Estimated Time to Complete: 2 den meetings (60 minutes each)**

## ✅ Requirements Checklist

### Meeting 1 Requirements:
- [ ] **Requirement 1:** Sample food from three of the following food groups: protein, vegetables, fruits, dairy, and grains
- [ ] **Requirement 2:** Be active for 30 minutes with your den or at least one other person that includes both stretching and moving

### Meeting 2 Requirements:
- [ ] **Requirement 3:** Be active for 15 minutes doing personal exercises that include cardio, muscular strength, and flexibility
- [ ] **Requirement 4:** Do a relaxing activity for 10 minutes

### Take-Home Requirement:
- [ ] **Requirement 5:** Review your Scouting America Annual Health and Medical record with your parent or guardian. Discuss your ability to participate in den and pack activities

---

## 🛒 Shopping & Materials List

### Items to Purchase:
- **Healthy snacks for food group sampling** - $20-30
  - Protein: cheese cubes, nuts, hard-boiled eggs
  - Vegetables: baby carrots, bell pepper strips, cherry tomatoes
  - Fruits: apple slices, grapes, berries
  - Dairy: individual yogurt cups, string cheese
  - Grains: whole grain crackers, pretzels
- **Frisbee or balls** (if not available) - $8-15 (Dollar Tree, Walmart, Target)
- **Exercise station signs** (cardstock/posterboard) - $5-8
- **Yoga mats or towels** (if not available) - $15-25 (Walmart, Target, Amazon)
- **Paper and crayons** for relaxation activity - $5-8
- **Water bottles** (disposable or ask families to bring) - $5-10

### Items You May Already Have:
- Calming music and speaker/phone
- Stopwatch or timer (phone app works)
- Markers for signs
- First aid kit

### **Total Estimated Cost: $40-60**

**💡 Cost-Saving Tips:**
- Ask families to contribute snacks by food group
- Use towels instead of yoga mats
- Use phone timer and music apps
- Make signs on regular paper

## Meeting 1: Nutrition and Active Fun (60 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Opening ceremony
- Discuss what it means to be "physically strong and mentally awake"

### Activities

#### Activity 1: Food Group Sampling (20 minutes)
**Requirement 1: Sample food from three food groups**
- Set up stations with foods from different groups:
  - Protein: cheese cubes, nuts, hard-boiled eggs
  - Vegetables: carrots, bell peppers, cherry tomatoes
  - Fruits: apple slices, grapes, berries
  - Dairy: yogurt, cheese
  - Grains: whole grain crackers, pretzels
- Cubs rotate through stations, sampling at least three groups
- Discuss which foods give us energy and help us grow strong

#### Activity 2: Group Active Time (30 minutes)
**Requirement 2: Be active for 30 minutes with stretching and moving**
- 5 minutes: Stretching routine (arm circles, toe touches, leg swings)
- 20 minutes: Ultimate frisbee or active games
- 5 minutes: Cool-down stretches
- Track activity on chart

### Closing (5 minutes)
- Discuss how we feel after being active
- Preview next meeting

---

## Meeting 2: Personal Fitness and Wellness (60 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Quick energizer game
- Review healthy habits from last meeting

### Activities

#### Activity 1: Personal Exercise Circuit (20 minutes)
**Requirement 3: Personal exercises including cardio, strength, and flexibility**
- Set up 3 stations (6-7 minutes each):
  - **Cardio Station**: Jumping jacks, running in place, dance moves
  - **Strength Station**: Modified push-ups, wall sits, bear crawls
  - **Flexibility Station**: Yoga poses, stretching, balance challenges
- Cubs rotate through all stations
- Each scout tracks their exercises

#### Activity 2: Relaxation Activity (15 minutes)
**Requirement 4: Relaxing activity for 10 minutes**
- Choose from:
  - Guided meditation/breathing exercises
  - Listening to calming music while drawing
  - Progressive muscle relaxation
  - Quiet reading time
- Discuss how relaxation helps our bodies and minds

#### Activity 3: Health Record Discussion (15 minutes)
**Requirement 5: Review health records**
- General discussion about staying healthy
- Talk about the importance of medical checkups
- Remind scouts to complete this requirement at home with parents
- Activity: Create personal wellness goals

### Closing (5 minutes)
- Share one new thing learned about staying healthy
- Closing ceremony

## Materials Needed
- Variety of healthy snacks from different food groups
- Frisbee or balls for active games
- Exercise station signs
- Yoga mats or towels
- Calming music and speaker
- Paper and crayons for relaxation activity
- Stopwatch or timer

## Safety Notes
- Review food allergies before snack activity
- Ensure EpiPen availability if needed
- Ask about dietary restrictions/religious concerns
- Modify exercises for different ability levels
- Provide water breaks during activities
- Supervise all physical activities closely

## Take-Home Requirements
- Requirement 5 must be completed at home with parent/guardian review of health records
