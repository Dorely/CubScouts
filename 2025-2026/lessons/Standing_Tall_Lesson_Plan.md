# Standing Tall Adventure Lesson Plan

**Estimated Time to Complete: 2 den meetings (60 minutes each)**

*Note: Requirements 1, 2, and 3 should be completed at home with parents. This lesson plan focuses on den-appropriate activities and Requirement 4.*

## Meeting 1: Personal Safety Gear and Digital Safety Discussion (60 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Opening ceremony
- Discuss what it means to "stand tall" and be safe

### Activities

#### Activity 1: Personal Safety Gear Exploration (35 minutes)
**Requirement 4: Identify and demonstrate safety gear**
- **Head Protection**: Helmets for biking, skating, sports
  - Show different types of helmets
  - Discuss when each is used
  - Practice proper fitting
  
- **Eye Protection**: Safety glasses, sunglasses, goggles
  - Demonstrate for different activities
  - Try on different types
  
- **Mouth Protection**: Mouthguards for sports
  - Show examples and explain use
  
- **Hand Protection**: Gloves for gardening, work, sports
  - Different gloves for different purposes
  - Practice putting on and taking off
  
- **Foot Protection**: Safety shoes, proper footwear for activities
  - Athletic shoes, boots, cleats
  - Match shoes to activities

#### Activity 2: Safety Gear Demonstration Activity (15 minutes)
- **Kitchen Safety Station**: Show safety gear for cooking
  - Oven mitts, aprons, closed-toe shoes
  - Practice safe kitchen behaviors

- **Science Experiment Safety**: Demonstrate lab safety
  - Safety goggles, gloves, aprons
  - Set up mock experiment with proper gear

- **Sports Safety**: Show gear for various sports
  - Helmet, knee pads, proper shoes for skating/rollerblading
  - Demonstrate proper use

### Closing (5 minutes)
- Review safety gear for different activities
- Remind about home requirements

---

## Meeting 2: Safety Practice and Personal Space (60 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Quick review of safety gear from last meeting
- Introduce personal space concept

### Activities

#### Activity 1: Personal Space Bubble Activity (25 minutes)
**Requirement 2: Personal Space Bubble (preparation for home completion)**
- **General Discussion**: Talk about different types of personal space
  - Intimate space (family hugs)
  - Personal space (close friends)
  - Social space (classmates, acquaintances)
  - Public space (strangers, crowds)
  
- **Activity**: Use hula hoops or rope circles to demonstrate different space zones
- **Practice**: Role-play appropriate interactions for different relationships
- **Note**: Remind scouts to complete full worksheet at home with parents

#### Activity 2: Digital Safety Discussion (20 minutes)
**Requirement 3: Family digital device policy (preparation)**
- **Age-appropriate discussion** about screen time and online safety
- **Group activity**: Brainstorm good digital habits
  - Time limits for devices
  - Appropriate content
  - Not sharing personal information
  - Asking permission before downloading
- **Take-home**: Encourage families to create their own device rules

#### Activity 3: Safety Scenarios Practice (5 minutes)
- Quick scenarios to practice safety thinking:
  - "What safety gear would you need to ride a bike?"
  - "How would you stay safe while cooking with a grown-up?"
  - "What should you do if someone makes you uncomfortable?"

### Closing (5 minutes)
- Review key safety concepts
- Emphasize that it's always okay to ask for help
- Closing ceremony
