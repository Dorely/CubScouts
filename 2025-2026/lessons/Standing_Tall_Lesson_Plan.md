## Denner-led Games (pick 1–2; leaders rotate by round)

- Four Corners (8 minutes)
  - Setup: Mark four corners as 1–4; one Game Leader stands eyes closed in the center counting to 5–10.
  - Objective: Choose safe corners to avoid being called out.
  - How to play: On “move,” Cubs quietly choose a corner. Leader calls a number without looking; all in that corner are out for that round (spectate). Remaining Cubs play new rounds.
  - Rotate leaders: Last remaining Cub becomes the next Game Leader.
  - End: When winner is crowned or time expires; reset with everyone in.

- Blob Tag (8 minutes)
  - Setup: Open space; 1–2 taggers start as a “blob.”
  - Objective: Avoid being tagged and joining the blob.
  - How to play: Tagged Cubs link elbows to the blob; only blob edges may tag. When blob grows to 4–6, split into two blobs.
  - Rotate leaders: New round with different starting taggers as Game Leaders.
  - End: When everyone has joined a blob or time.
  - Safety: Walking tag only; no yanking arms; respect boundaries.
