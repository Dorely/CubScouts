# Roaring Laughter Adventure Lesson Plan

**Adventure Goal**: Learn that laughter is good for the soul and helps build friendships.
**Format**: 2 Meetings
**Location**: Home / Den Meeting

---

## Meeting 1: Laughter & Games (Tonight)
**Requirements Covered**: 1, 2, 3
**Preparation**: None (use the lists below)

### Activity 1: What Makes Us Laugh? (Requirement 1)
**Time**: 10 Minutes
**Instructions**:
1.  Sit in a circle.
2.  Ask each Scout to share 3 things that make them laugh.
3.  **Discussion Prompts**:
    *   "Do you like funny movies? Which ones?"
    *   "Do you like silly noises?"
    *   "Does it make you laugh when a pet does something silly?"
4.  **Optional Game**: "Don't Laugh" - One person tries to make the others laugh without touching them. The first person to laugh is "out" or becomes the next "joker."

### Activity 2: Tongue Twister Challenge (Requirement 2)
**Time**: 15 Minutes
**Instructions**:
1.  Explain that tongue twisters are phrases that are hard to say quickly.
2.  Have Scouts try to say these 3 times fast:

**Easy**:
*   "Red leather, yellow leather."
*   "Unique New York."
*   "Toy boat."
*   "Good blood, bad blood."
*   "Rubber baby buggy bumpers."
*   "Eleven benevolent elephants."

**Medium**:
*   "She sells seashells by the seashore."
*   "Fuzzy Wuzzy was a bear. Fuzzy Wuzzy had no hair. Fuzzy Wuzzy wasn't fuzzy, was he?"
*   "I scream, you scream, we all scream for ice cream."
*   "A proper copper coffee pot."
*   "Six slippery snails slid slowly seaward."
*   "Fred fed Ted bread and Ted fed Fred bread."

**Hard**:
*   "Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers."
*   "How much wood would a woodchuck chuck if a woodchuck could chuck wood?"
*   "The thirty-three thieves thought that they thrilled the throne throughout Thursday."
*   "Betty Botter bought some butter but the butter was bitter, so Betty Botter bought some better butter to make the bitter butter better."
*   "I saw Susie sitting in a shoeshine shop."
*   "Can you can a can as a canner can can a can?"

**Optional Game: Alphabet Sentence Game**
**Time**: 10 Minutes (if time permits)
**Instructions**:
1.  Scouts work together to create a sentence where each word starts with the next letter of the alphabet.
2.  Example: "**A**aron **b**ought **c**andy **d**uring **e**arly **f**all..."
3.  Go around the circle - each Scout adds the next word.
4.  See how far you can get through the alphabet!
5.  **Challenge**: Try to make the sentence make sense (or be as silly as possible!).

### Activity 3: Charades (Requirement 3)
**Time**: 20 Minutes
**Instructions**:
1.  Explain the rules: Act out a word or phrase without speaking.
2.  Use these easy words for Cub Scouts:

**Animals**:
*   Monkey
*   Elephant
*   Snake
*   Chicken
*   Dog chasing its tail

**Actions**:
*   Brushing teeth
*   Eating spaghetti
*   Riding a bike
*   Swimming
*   Playing baseball

**Objects**:
*   Tree
*   Airplane
*   Robot
*   Balloon

## Meeting 2: Jokes & Skits (December 10, 2025)
**Requirements Covered**: 4, 5
**Preparation**: Have a few jokes ready (see below), print run-on scripts if needed

### Activity 1: Funniest Joke Contest (Requirement 4)
**Time**: 20 Minutes
**Instructions**:
1.  Have a "contest" where everyone wins a category (e.g., "Silliest Joke," "Loudest Joke," "Most Confusing Joke," "Best Delivery").
2.  Encourage Scouts to find jokes at home or use the ones below.
3.  **Joke Bank** (if they need ideas):

**Animal Jokes**:
*   *Why did the chicken cross the playground?* To get to the other slide.
*   *What do you call a bear with no teeth?* A gummy bear.
*   *What do you call a sleeping dinosaur?* A dino-snore.
*   *Why do fish live in salt water?* Because pepper makes them sneeze.
*   *What do you call a dog magician?* A labracadabrador.

**Food Jokes**:
*   *Why couldn't the pony sing a lullaby?* He was a little horse.
*   *What has ears but cannot hear?* A cornfield.
*   *Why did the cookie go to the doctor?* Because it felt crumbly.
*   *What do you call cheese that isn't yours?* Nacho cheese.

**School Jokes**:
*   *Why was 6 afraid of 7?* Because 7, 8, 9!
*   *What's a pirate's favorite letter?* You'd think it's R, but it's the C!
*   *Why did the student eat his homework?* The teacher said it was a piece of cake.

**Knock-Knock Jokes**:
*   *Knock knock.* Who's there? *Boo.* Boo who? *Don't cry, it's just a joke!*
*   *Knock knock.* Who's there? *Interrupting cow.* Interrupting c— *MOOOOO!*
*   *Knock knock.* Who's there? *Tank.* Tank who? *You're welcome!*

### Activity 2: Run-Ons (Requirement 5)
**Time**: 20 Minutes
**Instructions**:
1.  Explain a "Run-on": A very short skit that "runs on" to the stage, delivers a quick punchline, and "runs off."
2.  Practice at least 2 of these run-ons (they'll perform them at a pack meeting or campfire):

**Run-On 1: The Viper**
*   **Scout 1**: (Runs on screaming) "The Viper is coming! The Viper is coming!"
*   **Scout 2**: (Runs on screaming) "The Viper is coming! Hide!"
*   **Scout 3**: (Walks on with a squeegee and bucket) "I'm the Vindow Viper. I'm here to vipe your vindows."

**Run-On 2: Is it Time?**
*   **Scout 1**: (Sitting down, looking bored) "Is it time yet?"
*   **Scout 2**: (Checks watch) "No, not yet."
*   (Wait 5 seconds)
*   **Scout 1**: "Is it time yet?"
*   **Scout 2**: "No, not yet."
*   (Wait 5 seconds)
*   **Scout 1**: "Is it time yet?"
*   **Scout 2**: "Yes! It's time!"
*   **Both**: (Stand up and yell) "It's time to run off!" (Run off stage)

**Run-On 3: Invisible Bench**
*   **Scout 1**: (Sits in mid-air, pretending to sit on a bench reading a book)
*   **Scout 2**: (Walks up) "What are you doing?"
*   **Scout 1**: "Sitting on this invisible bench."
*   **Scout 2**: "Can I sit too?"
*   **Scout 1**: "Sure!" (Scout 2 sits)
*   **Scout 3**: (Walks up) "What are YOU doing?"
*   **Scout 2**: "Sitting on this invisible bench."
*   **Scout 3**: "Can I sit too?"
*   **Scout 1 & 2**: "Sure!" (Scout 3 sits)
*   **Scout 4**: (Walks up) "Hey! What are you all doing?"
*   **All 3**: "Sitting on this invisible bench!"
*   **Scout 4**: "What invisible bench? I moved it over there!" (Points away)
*   (All 3 fall down)

**Run-On 4: The Gum**
*   **Scout 1**: (Walking and chewing gum loudly)
*   **Scout 2**: (Walks by) "You shouldn't chew gum like that."
*   **Scout 1**: "Why not?"
*   **Scout 2**: "You'll get cavities!"
*   **Scout 1**: "I don't care!" (Continues chewing)
*   **Scout 3**: (Walks by) "You'll lose all your teeth!"
*   **Scout 1**: "I don't care!" (Continues chewing)
*   **Scout 4**: (Walks by) "That gum lost its flavor 10 minutes ago!"
*   **Scout 1**: (Stops, looks horrified, spits it out and runs off)

### Tips for Leading This Meeting
*   **Encourage Originality**: Let Scouts find their own jokes or make up their own run-ons.
*   **Practice for Performance**: If possible, plan to perform the run-ons at the next pack meeting or campfire.
*   **Keep it Light**: The goal is to have fun and laugh together!
