# Bear Den Halloween Party Games

## Game 1: Monster Movement Freeze Dance (10 minutes)
**Leader:** Den Leader with rotating Cub "Monster Masters"
**Materials:** Halloween music, speaker
**How to Play:**
- Cubs dance like different monsters when music plays
- Monster Master calls out creatures: "Dance like zombies!" "Fly like bats!" "Prowl like werewolves!"
- When music stops, Cubs freeze in monster pose
- Last one moving becomes the next Monster Master
- Include Scouting monsters: "March like Scout zombies!" (Scout sign while walking)

## Game 2: Pumpkin Patch Relay (15 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs rotate as "Pumpkin Farmers"
**Materials:** Orange balloons (pumpkins), 2 baskets
**Setup:** 20-foot course with start/finish lines
**How to Play:**
- Teams of 4-5 Cubs line up at start
- Pumpkin Farmer gives challenges: carry "pumpkin" between knees, balance on head, roll with nose
- Cubs must get pumpkin to harvest basket and return
- Drop your pumpkin = start over from beginning
- Pumpkin Farmer rotates every round, chooses next challenge method

## Game 3: Ghostly Scavenger Hunt (12 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs take turns as "Ghost Guide"
**Materials:** White sheets/fabric pieces hidden around area
**How to Play:**
- Ghost Guide hides 8-10 white "ghost pieces" around party area
- Other Cubs search for ghosts in pairs (buddy system)
- When pair finds a ghost, they must perform a Scout skill together: Scout handshake, recite Scout motto, demonstrate Scout sign
- Ghost Guide verifies skill and gives pair a Halloween sticker
- Rotate Ghost Guide every 3-4 minutes

## Game 4: Werewolf Pack Leadership (10 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs alternate as "Alpha Werewolf"
**Materials:** None
**Setup:** Cubs form pack circle
**How to Play:**
- Alpha Werewolf leads pack in howling patterns: short-long-short, three quick howls, silent prowl
- Pack must follow Alpha's lead exactly
- Add Scout values: "Howl if you're trustworthy!" "Silent prowl if you're loyal!"
- Wrong followers become the next Alpha
- Include pack movements: prowling, hunting stance, sleeping in den

## Game 5: Witch's Brew Mixing Station (8 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs rotate as "Head Witch"
**Materials:** Large bowl, safe "ingredients" (colored water, glitter, foam balls)
**How to Play:**
- Head Witch calls for magical ingredients for Scout potion
- Other Cubs bring ingredients one at a time: "Bring courage!" (red water), "Add kindness!" (glitter)
- Each Cub must say why that ingredient helps Scouts before adding it
- Head Witch stirs and declares potion ready
- New Head Witch creates different Scout virtue potion

## Game 6: Skeleton Scout Skills (15 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs take turns as "Skeleton Leader"
**Materials:** None
**Setup:** Open space for movement
**How to Play:**
- Skeleton Leader calls out Scout skills that skeletons must perform with "no muscles"
- Cubs act out skills robotically: tie knots with stiff fingers, salute with rigid arms, march with locked knees
- Include camping skills: set up tent, start campfire, pack backpack
- Skeleton Leader judges best "boneless" performance and chooses next leader
- End with group skeleton Scout handshake chain

## Game 7: Vampire Bat Signal Relay (12 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs alternate as "Bat Commander"
**Materials:** Flashlight or phone light
**Setup:** Darken room/area if possible
**How to Play:**
- Bat Commander uses light signals to guide "bats" through obstacles
- Cubs must navigate course using only light signals: one flash = go, two flashes = stop, three flashes = turn
- Include Scout communication: use Scout signal with flashlight beam
- Successfully navigated bats become next Bat Commander
- Add teamwork: bats must help guide other bats using whispers

## Game 8: Mummy Wrap Leadership Challenge (10 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs rotate as "Mummy Master"
**Materials:** Toilet paper rolls (4-6 rolls)
**How to Play:**
- Mummy Master organizes Cubs into teams of 3: wrapper, mummy, timer
- Teams have 2 minutes to wrap their mummy (safely - face always visible)
- Mummy Master judges: neatness, speed, creativity, safety
- Winning team members become next rotation's Mummy Masters
- Include Scout themes: wrap mummy with neckerchief pattern, Scout salute while wrapped

## Game 9: Haunted House Courage Walk (15 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs take turns as "Courage Coach"
**Materials:** Blindfolds, safe obstacles to navigate
**Setup:** Simple obstacle course (chairs, pillows, etc.)
**How to Play:**
- Cubs navigate "haunted house" blindfolded with Courage Coach guidance
- Courage Coach gives verbal directions and encouragement
- Include Scout values: "Be brave like a Scout," "Trust your patrol buddy"
- Successfully guided Cubs become next Courage Coach
- End with group discussion: when have you shown courage in Scouting?

## Game 10: Halloween Charades Championship (12 minutes)
**Leader:** Cubs rotate as "Charades Champion"
**Materials:** Halloween/Scout themed word cards (or call out words)
**How to Play:**
- Charades Champion acts out Halloween + Scout combinations: "Camping Ghost," "Merit Badge Vampire," "Hiking Witch"
- Other Cubs guess both the Halloween creature AND the Scout activity
- Successful guessers become next Charades Champion
- Include den/pack activities: "Pinewood Derby Monster," "Popcorn Selling Zombie"
- Champion helps with hints if Cubs are stuck
