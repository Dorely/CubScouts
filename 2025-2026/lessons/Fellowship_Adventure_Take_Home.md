# Fellowship Adventure - Take Home Assignment

**This adventure is designed to be completed at home with your family over the course of 2-4 weeks.**

## ✅ Requirements Checklist

### Week 1:
- [ ] **Requirement 1:** With your parent or legal guardian talk about your family's faith traditions. Identify three holidays or celebrations that are part of your family's faith traditions. Make a craft, work of art, or a food item that is part of your favorite family's faith tradition, holiday or celebration

### Week 2:
- [ ] **Requirement 2:** With your family, attend a religious service OR other gathering that shows how your family expresses reverence

### Week 3:
- [ ] **Requirement 3:** Carry out an act of kindness

### Week 4:
- [ ] **Requirement 4:** With your parent or legal guardian identify a religion or faith that is different from your own. Determine two things that it has in common with your family's beliefs

**Alternative Completion:** This Adventure may be earned by completing the requirements above OR by completing a Religious Emblem of your family's choosing.

## Important Notes for Families

- **Alternative Completion**: This adventure may be earned by completing the requirements below OR by completing a Religious Emblem of your family's choosing

## Requirements and Activities

### Requirement 1: Family Faith Traditions
**Talk about your family's faith traditions. Identify three holidays or celebrations. Make a craft, work of art, or food item.**

**Timeline**: Week 1
**Activities to Choose From**:
- Have a family discussion about your faith traditions during dinner or family time
- Together, identify three holidays or celebrations that are important to your family
- Choose one of these projects:
  - **Thanksgiving treats**: Bake or cook something special for a family celebration
  - **Clay sculpture**: Create something meaningful to your faith tradition that can be used in celebrations
  - **String art heart**: Make decorative art representing love and faith
  - **Family tradition craft**: Create something specific to your family's customs

**Parent Notes**: 
- Guide your scout in understanding the meaning behind your family's traditions
- Help them see connections between celebrations and values
- Document with photos if desired

### Requirement 2: Religious Service or Gathering
**Attend a religious service OR other gathering that shows how your family expresses reverence.**

**Timeline**: Week 2
**Options**:
- Attend your family's regular place of worship
- Participate in a special celebration at your chartered organization
- Attend a Veterans Day or Memorial Day ceremony that includes reverent elements
- Participate in a family blessing or prayer time
- Join in community service that reflects your family's values

**Parent Notes**:
- Choose an activity that is meaningful and appropriate for your family
- Discuss beforehand what reverence means and how it's shown
- Talk afterward about what your scout observed and felt

### Requirement 3: Act of Kindness
**Carry out an act of kindness.**

**Timeline**: Week 3
**Ideas for Acts of Kindness**:
- Write positive notes about family members and put them in a "kindness jar"
- Talk to someone new at school who might need a friend
- Write thank you cards for teachers, coaches, or community helpers
- Help a neighbor with yard work or carrying groceries
- Donate toys or clothes to families in need
- Visit elderly family members or neighbors
- Help with household chores without being asked

**Parent Notes**:
- Help your scout plan an act of kindness that is meaningful
- Discuss how kindness reflects your family's values
- Encourage reflection on how it felt to help others

### Requirement 4: Learn About Different Faith
**Identify a religion or faith different from your own. Determine two things it has in common with your family's beliefs.**

**Timeline**: Week 4
**Approach**:
- Choose a respectful way to learn about another faith tradition
- Use library books, appropriate websites, or conversations with friends/neighbors
- Focus on positive aspects and common values
- Examples of common ground might include:
  - Caring for others and helping those in need
  - Importance of family and community
  - Values like honesty, kindness, and respect
  - Gratitude and appreciation
  - Taking care of the environment
  - Peace and understanding

**Parent Notes**:
- Guide this exploration with sensitivity and respect
- Emphasize similarities rather than differences
- Use this as an opportunity to discuss tolerance and understanding
- Consider connecting with families of different faiths if appropriate

## Documentation Ideas

**Family Faith Portfolio** (Optional):
- Create a simple scrapbook or folder documenting your scout's fellowship journey
- Include photos of family traditions, the craft made, acts of kindness performed
- Write short reflections about each requirement
- Add drawings or writings about what faith means to your family

## Reflection Questions for Families

After completing all requirements, consider discussing:
- What did you learn about our family's faith traditions?
- How did it feel to do kind acts for others?
- What surprised you about the different faith you learned about?
- How do our beliefs help guide how we treat others?
- What are you most grateful for in our family?

## Timeline Summary

- **Week 1**: Family faith discussion and craft creation
- **Week 2**: Attend religious service or reverent gathering
- **Week 3**: Perform act of kindness
- **Week 4**: Learn about different faith and find common ground

When your scout has completed all requirements:
- Check off each completed requirement in their handbook
- Share highlights with the den leader
- Consider sharing appropriate photos or stories with the den (respecting privacy)
- Celebrate this meaningful achievement as a family
