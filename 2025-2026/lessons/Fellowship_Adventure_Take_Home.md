# Fellowship Adventure - Take Home Assignment

**This adventure is designed to be completed at home with your family at your own pace.**

## ✅ Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Requirement 1:** With your parent or legal guardian talk about your family's faith traditions. 
  - Identify three holidays or celebrations that are part of your family's faith traditions. 
  - Make a craft, work of art, or a food item that is part of your favorite family's faith tradition, holiday or celebration
- [ ] **Requirement 2:** With your family, attend a religious service OR other gathering that shows how your family expresses reverence
- [ ] **Requirement 3:** Carry out an act of kindness
- [ ] **Requirement 4:** With your parent or legal guardian identify a religion or faith that is different from your own. Determine two things that it has in common with your family's beliefs

## Alternative Completion Method

This Adventure may be earned by completing the requirements above OR by completing a Religious Emblem of the Cub Scout's family's choosing.

## Reporting Completion

When your scout has completed all requirements:

- Parents may mark completion in the Scoutbook app or report to the den leader to mark it done

## Requirements and Activities

### Requirement 1: Family Faith Traditions
**Talk about your family's faith traditions. Identify three holidays or celebrations. Make a craft, work of art, or food item.**

**Activities to Choose From**:
- Have a family discussion about your faith traditions during dinner or family time
- Together, identify three holidays or celebrations that are important to your family
- Craft or Food Ideas:
  - **Thanksgiving Cards**: Decorate some thank you cards to give to people you are thankful for.
  - **Christmas Treats**: Bake some Christmas cookies or another treat
  - **Easter Eggs**: Decorate some Easter eggs
  - **Clay Faith Symbol**: Create a simple clay sculpture connected to a family faith tradition
  - **String Art Heart**: Make a string art heart as a reminder of love and kindness

### Requirement 2: Religious Service or Gathering
**Attend a religious service OR other gathering that shows how your family expresses reverence.**

**Options**:
- Attend your family's regular place of worship
- Participate in a family blessing or prayer time
- Join in community service that reflects your family's values
- Attend a celebration at your chartered partner based on a faith tradition
- Participate in a Veterans Day celebration

### Requirement 3: Act of Kindness
**Carry out an act of kindness.**

**Ideas for Acts of Kindness**:
- Talk to someone new at school who might need a friend
- Write thank you cards for teachers, coaches, or community helpers
- Help an elderly neighbor with yard work or carrying groceries
- Donate toys or clothes to families in need
- Start a "kindness jar" by writing kind words about a family member and adding them to a jar

### Requirement 4: Learn About Different Faiths
**Identify a religion or faith different from your own. Determine two things it has in common with your family's beliefs.**

**Approach**:
- Choose a respectful way to learn about another faith tradition
- Use library books, appropriate websites, or conversations with friends/neighbors
- Focus on positive aspects and common values
- Examples of common ground might include:
  - Caring for others and helping those in need
  - Importance of family and community
  - Values like honesty, kindness, and respect
  - Gratitude and appreciation
