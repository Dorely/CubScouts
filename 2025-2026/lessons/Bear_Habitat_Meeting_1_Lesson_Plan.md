## Denner-led Games (7–9 Scouts, age 8)
Pick 1–2 for Meeting 1 or campout downtime. Keep rounds short (3–5 minutes) and rotate roles.

1) Six Essentials Relay (10 minutes)
- Materials: empty daypack, labeled Six Essentials items, two cones
- How to play: Two teams race to pack the Six Essentials correctly, run to cone and back, then unpack in order
- Denner tips: Check items and order; add a “mystery extra” to discuss whether it belongs

2) Trash Timeline Sort (10 minutes)
- Materials: cards with common litter items and their decomposition times, tape or floor line
- How to play: Scouts place item cards along a “time line” from days to centuries; discuss surprises
- Denner tips: Ask, “What’s our plan to prevent this at campout?”

3) Wildlife Signs Bingo (10–12 minutes)
- Materials: simple 3×3 bingo cards with wildlife signs; tokens; a stack of picture prompts
- How to play: Denner shows a prompt; scouts cover matching sign; first bingo shares one LNT behavior for wildlife
- Denner tips: Encourage “look up, down, around” reminders

4) Leave No Trace Charades (8–10 minutes)
- Materials: slips with LNT scenarios covering all seven principles (Plan Ahead and Prepare; Choose the Right Path; Trash Your Trash; Leave What You Find; Be Careful with Fire; Respect Wildlife; Be Kind to Other Visitors)
- How to play: A scout acts out a scenario; others guess the Leave No Trace principle and name one behavior we’ll use at the campout
- Denner tips: Keep it moving; rotate actors; after each, tie back to Regional Adventure Weekend

	Example prompts:
	- Plan Ahead and Prepare: checking weather map; packing water and sun protection
	- Choose the Right Path: staying on trail; walking single file on narrow sections
	- Trash Your Trash: pocketing a wrapper; doing a micro-trash sweep
	- Leave What You Find: photographing a cool rock/flower instead of picking it
	- Be Careful with Fire: pointing to “High” fire danger sign; keeping distance from fire rings
	- Respect Wildlife: looking through imaginary binoculars from far away; quiet voices
	- Be Kind to Other Visitors: stepping aside to let others pass; friendly wave
