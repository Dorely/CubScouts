# 🐻 Tongue Twister Challenge
**Bear Den - Roaring Laughter Adventure**

---

## 📝 Instructions
Try to say each tongue twister **3 times fast**! The faster you go, the harder it gets!

---

## ⭐ Easy Tongue Twisters

1. **Red leather, yellow leather.**

2. **Unique New York.**

3. **Toy boat.**

4. **Good blood, bad blood.**

5. **Rubber baby buggy bumpers.**

6. **Eleven benevolent elephants.**

---

## ⭐⭐ Medium Tongue Twisters

1. **She sells seashells by the seashore.**

2. **Fuzzy Wuzzy was a bear. Fuzzy Wuzzy had no hair. Fuzzy Wuzzy wasn't fuzzy, was he?**

3. **I scream, you scream, we all scream for ice cream.**

4. **A proper copper coffee pot.**

5. **Six slippery snails slid slowly seaward.**

6. **Fred fed Ted bread and Ted fed Fred bread.**

---

## ⭐⭐⭐ Hard Tongue Twisters

1. **Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers.**

2. **How much wood would a woodchuck chuck if a woodchuck could chuck wood?**

3. **The thirty-three thieves thought that they thrilled the throne throughout Thursday.**

4. **Betty Botter bought some butter but the butter was bitter, so Betty Botter bought some better butter to make the bitter butter better.**

5. **I saw Susie sitting in a shoeshine shop.**

6. **Can you can a can as a canner can can a can?**
