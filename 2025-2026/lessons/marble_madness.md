# Marble Madness Adventure Lesson Plan

**Goal**: Complete the Marble Madness Elective Adventure.
**Duration**: 2 Days (Meetings)

## Reference
- **Official Source**: [Scouting America - Marble Madness](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/marble-madness/)

---

## Day 1: Build, Jargon & Mazes
**Focus**: Learning the language of marbles, constructing tracks, and solving mazes.

### 1. Marble Jargon (Requirement 2)
**Source**: [Marble Jargon Activity](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/marble-jargon/)
**Objective**: Learn five special words that are used when talking about marbles.

**Activity**:
Discuss and learn the meanings of at least five of the marble terms found in the **Marble Jargon List** at the bottom of this lesson. Use memory cards if available.

**Resources**:
- Printable Memory Cards: [Marble Jargon Memory Cards](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/cubscouts/adventure-resources/bear/Bear%20Marble%20Madness%202%20Marble%20Jargon.pdf)

### 2. Marble Racetrack (Requirement 4)
**Source**: [Marble Racetrack Activity](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/marble-racetrack/)
**Objective**: Build a marble racetrack with at least two lanes.

**Materials Needed**:
- Recycled materials: Paper towel/toilet paper tubes, paper plates, egg cartons, cardboard boxes.
- Creating materials: Tape (masking or painter's), scissors.
- Marbles (for testing).

**Instructions**:
1.  **Design**: Plan a track that allows two marbles to race side-by-side or on separate but equal paths.
2.  **Build**:
    - Use tubes for tunnels or chutes. Cut them in half lengthwise for open channels.
    - Use paper plates twisted or cut to make spirals or funnels.
    - Use cardboard boxes for support towers or ramps.
    - Use egg cartons or cups at the bottom to catch the marbles.
3.  **Test**: Run marbles down the track to see which lane is faster. Adjust slopes and curves as needed.
4.  **Race**: Have Scouts race their marbles against each other!

### 3. Marble Maze (Requirement 5)
**Source**: [Zig, Zag, Zoom Activity](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/zig-zag-zoom/)
**Objective**: Build a marble maze.

**Materials**:
- Shallow cardboard box lid or tray (one per Scout).
- Straws or pipe cleaners.
- Tape or glue.
- Marbles.

**Instructions**:
1.  Plan a path for the marble from a "Start" to a "Finish" point within the box lid.
2.  Tape or glue straws/pipe cleaners to the box floor to create walls and corridors.
3.  Add dead ends or obstacles to make it challenging.
4.  **Play**: Scouts trade mazes and try to solve them by tilting the box.

## Day 2: Gear & Games
**Focus**: Making a bag and playing classic games.

### 1. Make a Marble Bag (Requirement 1)
**Source**: [No-Sew Marble Bag Activity](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/no-sew-marble-bag/)
**Objective**: Make a personal drawstring bag to hold marbles.

**Option: No-Sew Marble Bag**
**Materials**:
- 10-inch square of felt or fleece (non-fraying fabric).
- 24-inch sturdy cord (paracord, shoelace, yarn).
- 9-inch paper plate (template).
- Scissors, rulers, pencils.

**Instructions**:
1.  Trace the 9-inch paper plate onto the fabric and cut out the circle.
2.  Mark 8 sets of slits around the edge (every 45 degrees).
3.  Cut small slits at these marks.
4.  Weave the cord through the slits.
5.  Tie knots at the ends of the cord and pull to close.

### 2. Marble Games (Requirement 3)
**Source**: [Ringer, Plums, and Dropsies Activity](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/ringer-plums-and-dropsies/)
**Objective**: Play three different marble games.

**Game 1: Ringer**
-   **Setup**: 10ft ring, 13 marbles in "X" in center.
-   **Play**: Shoot from outside ring. Keep marbles knocked out if shooter stays in. Turn ends if shooter goes out.

**Game 2: Plums**
-   **Setup**: Two parallel lines. Target marbles ("plums") on one line.
-   **Play**: Shoot from the other line. Keep any plums knocked off.

**Game 3: Dropsies**
-   **Setup**: 2ft square with 5 marbles inside.
-   **Play**: Stand outside, drop shooter from waist height. Knock marbles out.

## Marble Jargon List

-   **Aggie marble**: made of mineral agate
-   **Alley marble**: made of alabaster or marble
-   **Alley taw**: the offensive marble set aside for shooting
-   **Back to taw**: when a player needs to return to where he shot last
-   **Bombies**: dropping your marble onto another marble
-   **Boss**: a large playing marble
-   **Bull ring**: a large round ring for playing marbles
-   **Burns**: a call by a player which allows him to roll his marble again after his shooter has hit some object that deflected it from the desired direction
-   **Cat’s eye**: marbles have a swirl of color inside
-   **China alley**: a marble made from chinaware with rings painted in different colors
-   **Chuck**: a shooter that remains in the ring after being rolled towards marbles in the ring
-   **Clearies**: a call that gives permission to clear obstacles from the ground before the defensive marble
-   **Clip**: the act of hitting a marble
-   **Ccommie**: common marble, plainest looking marbles
-   **Dob**: a large marble
-   **Downsies**: a call demanding that the shooter keep his knuckles on the ground when shooting
-   **Drop**: an expert marble player
-   **Dubs**: when two or more marbles are knocked out of the ring with one shot; OR a call that gives the player right to take all marbles, or no dubs to revoke the right
-   **Earnest**: a game in which players keep the marbles they knock from the ring
-   **Edger**: a marble or taw that is near the edge of the ring or shooting line
-   **Fairsies**: rules of playing in which each player gets back his or her marbles at the end of the game
-   **Friendlies**: you give back the marbles you’ve won at the end of a game
-   **Fudging**: moving your hand forward while shooting
-   **Fumble**: to allow the marble to slip from the hand when shooting
-   **Hand span**: the width of your hand
-   **Histing**: lift your knuckle as you shoot
-   **Jumpsies**: shooting your marble in such a way that it jumps into the air and hits the target marble on landing without hitting the ground first
-   **Keepsies**: rules that any marbles that a player loses are not returned at the end of the game, but kept by whoever won them
-   **Knuckle down**: put one knuckle of your shooting hand on the ground as you shoot
-   **Lacky**: last in order of shooting
-   **Lagging**: in marbles you don’t flip a coin to see who goes first. You shoot or bowl a marble from the pitch line to another parallel line called the lag line
-   **Lag line**: the line you throw toward when lagging
-   **Mibs**: target marbles, the ones you shoot at
-   **Mibster**: a marble player
-   **Pitch line**: the line you stand behind to lag
-   **Pot**: collection of marbles that every player adds to for playing a game
-   **Poon**: to shoot at a marble from a long distance
-   **Shooter**: the marble you shoot with. It may be slightly larger than a regular marble. Some players have favorite or lucky shooters
-   **Shooting line**: the line behind which you stand and shoot in a game
-   **Slip**: when a marble slips out of a player’s hand accidentally
-   **Snooger**: term used in ringer to describe a target marble that is near the rim of the ring
-   **Shoot**: to roll, pitch, or move a marble in the direction of a target
-   **Shooter or taw**: the offensive marble
-   **Smug**: to grab all the marbles and run when some action might break up the game
-   **Target marble**: the marble you want to hit
-   **Taw marble**: between one-half inch and three-fourths of an inch in diameter
-   **Taw line**: line behind which you shoot
