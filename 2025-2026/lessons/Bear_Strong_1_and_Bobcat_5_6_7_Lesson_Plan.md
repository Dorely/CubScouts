# Bear Strong 1 & Bobcat 5–7 Lesson Plan

**Estimated Time to Complete: 1 den meeting (60 minutes)**

## ✅ Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Bear Strong — Requirement 1:** Sample food from three of the following food groups: protein, vegetables, fruits, dairy, and grains
- [ ] **Bobcat — Requirement 5:** Learn about the denner position and responsibilities
- [ ] **Bobcat — Requirement 7:** Share with your den a time when you demonstrated the Cub Scout motto "Do Your Best"

### Take-Home Assignments
- [ ] **Bobcat — Requirement 8 (Home):** With a parent or legal guardian, complete the activities in the booklet "How to Protect Your Children from Child Abuse: A Parent's Guide"
- [ ] **Fellowship Adventure (Home):** Complete at home with family — see `2025-2026/lessons/Fellowship_Adventure_Take_Home.md`

## Denner Duties Today (Quick Guide)
- Lead opening: flag/Oath/Law with den leader
- Assist: warmup game at start; cooldown/reflection at end
- Materials: help set up, pass out, and collect supplies/snacks
- Transitions: serve as line leader and model Scout behavior
- Roster: help track denner rotation and mark attendance
- Closing: help with flag ceremony and announcements; support cleanup

## Meeting 1: Den Leadership and Healthy Choices (60 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Welcome and brief overview of today’s goals
- Recite the Scout Oath and Law together
- Den Chief warmup game (choose one, ~2–3 minutes):
  - Traffic Light: leader calls Green/Yellow/Red; scouts move/walk/stop accordingly
  - Animal Walk Tag: one gentle tagger; others move as called (bear crawl, crab walk, hop)
  - Follow-the-Leader Walk: leader weaves a simple path; marching steps, pivots; no running

### Activities

#### Activity 1: Denner Role & Rotation (10 minutes)
**Covers Bobcat Requirement 5: Denner position and responsibilities**
- Discuss what a denner does (helps lead flag ceremony, helps pass out materials, sets a good example)
- Play quick chance-based selection (Numbered Stick Draw) and assign today’s denner immediately
- Record the full roster order on the rotation chart for upcoming meetings
- Post the denner responsibilities chart in your meeting space

#### Activity 3: "Do Your Best" Circle (10 minutes)
**Covers Bobcat Requirement 7: Share a time you did your best**
- Scouts take turns sharing a short story of when they did their best
- Den leader highlights themes like perseverance, kindness, and responsibility

#### Activity 4: Food Group Sampling Stations (20 minutes)
**Covers Bear Strong Requirement 1: Sample from three food groups**
- Set up simple tasting stations, aiming to include several groups:
  - Protein (e.g., cheese cubes, nuts, hard-boiled eggs)
  - Vegetables (e.g., carrots, bell peppers)
  - Fruits (e.g., apple slices, grapes)
  - Dairy (e.g., yogurt)
  - Grains (e.g., whole-grain crackers)
- Scouts try items from at least three different groups
- Quick discussion: Which foods fuel our bodies? What makes a snack well-balanced?

### Closing (5 minutes)
- Quick review: one thing learned or a favorite moment from today
- Remind that denner rotation continues next meeting with the next number on the chart
- Assign take-home items (Bobcat Req 8 and Fellowship Adventure) and remind families
- Den Chief cooldown game (choose one, ~2 minutes):
  - Silent Statues: soft music or humming; freeze on cue; last to freeze sits out briefly
  - Mirror Breathing: in pairs, one moves arms slowly while both breathe together, then switch
  - Pass the Squeeze: hand squeeze travels around the circle quietly with eyes closed

## Materials Needed
- Simple snacks from at least three food groups (see examples above)
- Plates/napkins, serving utensils, and hand sanitizer
- Denner rotation chart, numbered sticks, and a cup/hat (for the draw)
- Optional cones/tape to mark simple warmup paths
- Optional device for soft music during cooldown
- Timer/stopwatch

## Safety Notes
- Check for food allergies and dietary/religious restrictions before sampling
- Follow safe food handling; provide handwashing or sanitizer
- Have EpiPen access and trained adult if required
- Ensure adequate space and supervision during games
