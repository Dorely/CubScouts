#### **Materials Needed**
- Bobcat Bear badges for each scout
- Parent signature verification forms for Requirement 8
- Den code of conduct poster
- Relay game supplies

#### **Lesson Structure**

**Opening Circle (5 minutes)**
- **Welcome:** "Welcome back, future Bobcats!" First Denner helps lead.
- **Opening:** Scout sign and recite the Scout Oath together.

**Activity 1: Bobcat Requirements Review Game (20 minutes)**
- **Purpose:** Quickly and engagingly verify completion of all Bobcat requirements.
- **Review Game - "Bobcat Challenge":**
    - Create stations or have the den leader call out challenges.
    - **Challenge 1:** Recite the Scout Law as a den.
    - **Challenge 2:** Name the three points of the Scout Oath.
    - **Challenge 3:** A scout demonstrates the sign and handshake, explaining what they mean.
    - **Challenge 4:** Review the den's Code of Conduct and ask for an example of one rule.
    - **Challenge 5:** Ask, "What is one job a denner might do?"
    - **Challenge 6:** Ask, "What is our motto?" (Scouts shout: "Do Your Best!")
- **Parent Verification:** Collect signed verification forms for Requirement 8.

**Activity 2: Celebration Games (15 minutes)**
- **Game 1: Advanced Bobcat Relay:** Add fun challenges to the relay, such as hopping on one foot or walking backward.
- **Game 2: Team-Up Challenge:** A non-competitive game that requires scouts to use teamwork, reinforcing the den unity theme.

**Activity 3: Bobcat Badge Ceremony (10 minutes)**
- **Setup:** Form a circle with scouts and their families.
- **Ceremony Script:**
  "Today we celebrate your first great achievement in Cub Scouting. You have learned the foundations of being a Scout. You know the Scout Oath and Law, you have created a code for how your den will act, and you have learned to 'Do Your Best.' You have mastered the signs of a Cub Scout and are ready for your adventures as Bears. We are proud to present you with your Bobcat badge."
- **Presentation:** Call each scout forward, present their badge with a Cub Scout handshake, and congratulate them.

**Activity 4: Looking Ahead to Bear Adventures (8 minutes)**
- **Preview:** Briefly introduce the next adventure, like "Bear Strong" or "Bear Habitat."
- **Get Excited:** Show materials or talk about a fun activity for the next meeting to build anticipation.

**Closing Circle & Celebration (2 minutes)**
- **Reflection:** "What was your favorite part of becoming a Bobcat?"
- **Closing:** Led by the denner, all make the Scout sign and proudly shout the Scout motto: "Do Your Best!"
- **Celebration:** Enjoy snacks and take a group photo with the new Bobcat badges.
