# Bobcat Wrap-Up - Den Meeting Lesson Plan
**Date:** August 27, 2025  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Adventure:** Bobcat (Completion & Celebration)  
**Age Group:** Bear Cubs (8-9 years old)

## Lesson Objectives
By the end of this meeting, scouts will:
- Demonstrate completion of all Bobcat Bear requirements
- Verify parent completion of child protection review
- Receive their Bobcat Bear badge in a meaningful ceremony
- Celebrate their foundational Cub Scout achievement
- Be excited and prepared for Bear rank adventures

## Materials Needed
- Bobcat Bear badges for each scout
- Parent signature verification forms for Requirement 8
- Den code of conduct poster from last week
- Relay game supplies (balls, cones, etc.)
- Snacks for celebration
- Camera for photos
- Bear Strong adventure preview materials
- Certificate templates or achievement cards

## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome Back:** "Welcome back, future Bobcats!"
- **Excitement Check:** "Who practiced the Scout Oath and Law this week?"
- **Opening:** Scout sign and Scout Oath together

### Activity 1: Bobcat Bear Requirements Review (15 minutes)
**Purpose:** Verify completion of all 2025 Bobcat Bear requirements

**Verification Stations:**
1. **Den Unity Check (5 minutes)**
   - Review den code of conduct created last week
   - Recite Scout Oath and Law together as den
   - Discuss how den has bonded since Bear Line Up activity

2. **Skills Demonstration (5 minutes)**
   - Quick Bobcat Relay review - sign, salute, handshake
   - Each scout demonstrates proper form
   - Partner practice and peer support

3. **Parent Requirement Verification (5 minutes)**
   - Check that parents completed Requirement 8
   - Collect signed verification forms
   - Address any questions about child protection review

### Activity 2: Bobcat Bear Celebration Games (20 minutes)

**Game 1: Den Code Challenge (8 minutes)**
- Review the den code of conduct created in first meeting
- Act out scenarios where code applies
- "How would our den handle this situation?"
- Reinforce positive den behavior

**Game 2: Advanced Bobcat Relay (7 minutes)**
- Same relay format but with challenges
- Run to cone, demonstrate skill, answer question, return
- Examples: "Show scout sign and name one point of Scout Law"
- Teams work together to complete challenges

**Game 3: Bear Line Up Champions (5 minutes)**
- Repeat favorite Bear Line Up challenges from first meeting
- Show how much better the den works together now
- Celebrate improved teamwork and den unity

### Activity 3: Bobcat Badge Ceremony (10 minutes)

**Ceremony Setup:**
- Form circle with scouts and families
- Adult leaders in Scout uniform if possible
- Explain the significance of earning first badge

**Ceremony Script:**
"Today we celebrate a very special achievement. Each of you has completed your Bobcat Bear adventure. You've built unity with your den through Bear Line Up activities. You can recite the Scout Oath and Law together as a team. You've helped create a code of conduct that guides our den. You've mastered the Scout sign, salute, and handshake. Most importantly, your families have completed child protection training to keep you safe."

**Individual Recognition:**
- Call each scout by name
- Have them demonstrate one Bobcat skill
- Present badge with handshake
- "Congratulations, [Name], you are now a Bobcat!"

**Group Celebration:**
- All scouts say Scout Oath together one final time
- Bobcat cheer or applause

### Activity 4: Looking Ahead to Bear Adventures (8 minutes)

**Bear Strong Preview:**
- "Now that you're Bobcat Bears, you're ready for Bear Strong!"
- Show some fun exercises they'll try
- Talk about healthy eating and fitness

**Adventure Calendar Review:**
- Show the exciting Bear adventures coming up
- Bear Habitat camping trip preview
- Build excitement for the Bear rank journey

**Bear Facts Fun:**
- Share interesting facts about real bears
- Connect to upcoming adventures
- "Bears are strong, prepared, and take care of their habitat - just like Bear Scouts!"

### Closing Circle & Celebration (2 minutes)

**Reflection:**
- "What was your favorite part of learning to be a Bobcat?"
- "What are you most excited about as we start Bear adventures?"

**Closing:**
- Scout sign
- Scout motto: "Do Your Best!" - louder than ever
- Group photo with new Bobcat badges

**Take Home:**
- Information packet about Bear Strong adventure
- Remind about next week's meeting
- Encourage families to celebrate this achievement

## Notes for Den Leader

### Ceremony Tips:
- Keep ceremony meaningful but age-appropriate
- Have families participate by taking photos
- Consider having older scouts or parents help present badges
- Make each scout feel special and accomplished

### Follow-up Preparation:
- Ensure all scouts actually earned their Bobcat before ceremony
- Prepare Bear Strong materials for next week
- Send photos to families after meeting
- Update advancement records

### Celebration Ideas:
- Simple refreshments to mark the occasion
- Let scouts wear their new badges immediately
- Consider small celebration patches or stickers
- Create a "Bobcat Class of 2025" photo

### Safety Considerations:
- Keep games active but controlled
- Ensure adequate space for relay games
- Monitor excitement levels during ceremony
- Have first aid kit available

## Assessment Checklist
Before badge presentation, verify each scout has:
- ✅ Completed Bear Line Up team building activity
- ✅ Recited Scout Oath and Law with their den
- ✅ Participated in creating den code of conduct
- ✅ Demonstrated Scout sign, salute, and handshake in relay
- ✅ Parent/guardian completed child protection review (Requirement 8)
- ✅ All requirements signed off by den leader

## Next Week Preparation
- Bear Strong adventure materials ready
- Fitness activity supplies
- Healthy snacks for food group sampling
- Information about upcoming camping trip
