## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome Back:** "Strong Bears are back!"
- **Fitness Check-In:** "How did everyone feel doing exercises at home?"
- **Energy Boost:** Quick stretching routine to get ready
- **Opening:** Scout Oath emphasizing "physically strong, mentally awake"

### Activity 1: Fitness Log Review & Sharing (15 minutes)
**Requirement Completion:** Review Scouting America Annual Health and Medical record with parent/guardian

**Fitness Log Showcase:**
- Scouts share their fitness tracking logs from home
- Discuss activities they tried with families
- Share favorite healthy foods discovered
- Celebrate consistency and effort, not perfection

**Medical Record Review Check:**
- Collect signed forms confirming parents reviewed medical records
- Discuss how knowing our health helps us stay safe in activities
- Emphasize importance of communication with parents about health

**Group Discussion:**
- "What was the hardest part about tracking fitness?"
- "What was the most fun physical activity you tried?"
- "Which new healthy food did you like best?"

### Activity 2: Healthy Habits Game Show (15 minutes)
**Purpose:** Reinforce nutrition and fitness learning through fun competition

**Game Format:** Teams answer questions and demonstrate knowledge

**Nutrition Round:**
- Name foods from each food group
- "Healthy or Sometimes Food?" sorting game
- Quick food group relay

**Fitness Round:**
- Demonstrate different types of exercises (cardio, strength, flexibility)
- "Exercise Charades" - act out different physical activities
- Balance and coordination challenges

**Wellness Round:**
- Show relaxation techniques learned
- Discuss importance of sleep and rest
- Share stress-reducing activities

**Celebration:** All teams win for showing Bear Strong knowledge!

### Activity 3: Bear Strong Group Challenges (20 minutes)
**Purpose:** Complete group requirements and build teamwork while staying active

**Challenge 1: Den Fitness Circuit (8 minutes)**
- Collaborate to create a group exercise routine
- Each scout contributes one exercise
- Perform the routine together
- Focus on encouraging each other

**Challenge 2: Healthy Meal Planning (7 minutes)**
- Work in teams to plan a balanced meal
- Include foods from all 5 food groups
- Present meal ideas to the group
- Discuss why each food choice is healthy

**Challenge 3: Active Game Creation (5 minutes)**
- Groups invent a new active game
- Must include cardio, strength, and flexibility
- Teach the game to other scouts
- Play the new games together

### Activity 4: Celebration & Goal Setting (5 minutes)

**Bear Strong Celebration:**
- Recognize each scout's effort and growth
- Present Bear Strong completion certificates
- Share healthy snacks to celebrate
- Take group photos with certificates

**Future Fitness Goals:**
- Each scout shares one fitness goal for the coming month
- Discuss how they'll stay active between now and next adventure
- Exchange ideas for staying healthy

**Looking Ahead:**
- Preview Standing Tall adventure coming up
- Build excitement for safety and preparedness learning
- Connect fitness to being prepared for adventures
