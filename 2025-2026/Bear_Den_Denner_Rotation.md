### How It Works
- Selection: At the start of the year, run a quick chance-based draw (e.g., Numbered Stick Draw) to set the initial order.
- Rotation: The denner role moves to the next scout each den meeting; loop to the top after the last.
- Absences: If the scheduled denner is absent, the next scout fills in and the absent scout goes next week.
- New/Departing Scouts: Add new scouts to the end of the list; remove those who transfer out and close the gap.

### Denner Duties
- Lead opening with den leader (flag/Oath/Law) and assist with announcements
- Help run the warmup game and the cooldown/reflection at closing
- Assist with materials/snacks setup, passing out, and cleanup
- Serve as line leader; model Scout behavior during transitions
- Help mark attendance and maintain the rotation chart
