## Adventure Overview
This weekend camping experience focuses on Leave No Trace principles while completing the Bear Habitat adventure through outdoor observation, nature discovery, and responsible camping practices.

## Updated 2024-2025 Requirements Covered:
1. **Prepare for the Walk:** Gather Cub Scout Six Essentials and weather-appropriate gear
2. **"Know Before You Go":** Identify location on map and plan route
3. **"Choose the Right Path":** Learn about trail and surrounding area
4. **"Trash Your Trash":** Plan for waste management
5. **"Leave What You Find":** Document discoveries through photos/sketches
6. **"Be Careful with Fire":** Determine fire danger rating
7. **"Respect Wildlife":** Identify signs of mammals, birds, insects, reptiles

## Weekend Schedule

### Friday Evening (Arrival & Setup)

**5:00 PM - Arrival & Safety Brief (30 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Know Before You Go"**
- Show scouts location on map
- Point out planned hiking routes
- Discuss camping area boundaries and safety rules

**5:30 PM - Six Essentials Check (30 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Prepare for the Walk"**
- Each scout displays their Six Essentials:
  1. First aid kit
  2. Water bottle
  3. Flashlight
  4. Trail food
  5. Sun protection
  6. Whistle
- Discuss purpose of each item
- Check weather-appropriate clothing

**6:00 PM - Dinner & Fire Safety (90 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Be Careful with Fire"**
- Determine current fire danger rating for area
- Demonstrate proper fire safety practices
- Cook dinner using safe fire practices
- Discuss fire restrictions and safety rules

**7:30 PM - Evening Nature Observations (45 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Respect Wildlife"**
- Begin documenting wildlife signs
- Listen for animal sounds
- Look for tracks, scat, feeding signs
- Start nature observation journals

**8:15 PM - Leave No Trace Principles (30 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Leave What You Find"**
- Explain all 7 Leave No Trace principles
- Focus on appropriate for Cub Scouts
- Plan how to apply throughout weekend

### Saturday (Full Day Adventure)

**7:00 AM - Morning Wildlife Observation (45 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Respect Wildlife"**
- Early morning animal activity observation
- Look for birds, insects, signs of mammals
- Document findings in journals
- Practice quiet observation skills

**8:00 AM - Breakfast & Trail Preparation (60 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Choose the Right Path"**
- Review planned hiking route on map
- Discuss trail conditions and terrain
- Learn about local ecosystem and plants
- Pack for one-mile hike

**9:30 AM - One-Mile Nature Hike (90 minutes)**
- **All Requirements Combined**
- Follow planned route with map reference
- Look up, down, and around for wildlife signs
- Take photos of 5 things to remember
- Practice Leave No Trace principles
- Collect trash found along trail (if any)

**11:00 AM - Nature Documentation Time (60 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Leave What You Find"**
- Sketch 5 discoveries from hike in journals
- Take photos of interesting findings
- Discuss why we leave natural objects in place
- Create nature art with found objects (return after)

**12:00 PM - Lunch & Reflection (60 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Trash Your Trash"**
- Demonstrate proper waste management
- Pack out all trash including food scraps
- Discuss impact of litter on wildlife
- Plan for zero waste camping

**1:30 PM - Extended Exploration (90 minutes)**
- Continue wildlife sign identification
- Explore different habitats in camping area
- Practice using maps and navigation
- More nature journaling and photography

**3:00 PM - Free Time & Games (60 minutes)**
- Outdoor games that don't disturb environment
- Continue observing and documenting nature
- Practice Leave No Trace principles during play

**4:30 PM - Wildlife Sign Scavenger Hunt (60 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Respect Wildlife"**
- Search for remaining wildlife signs needed:
  - Track prints in soil/sand
  - Feathers or fur
  - Chewed plants or bark
  - Insect homes or signs
  - Bird nests (observe only, don't disturb)
  - Animal trails or paths

**6:00 PM - Dinner & Evening Program (120 minutes)**
- Cooking with continued fire safety
- Sharing discoveries from the day
- Review completed requirements
- Stargazing and night sounds observation

### Sunday Morning (Completion & Departure)

**7:00 AM - Final Wildlife Observations (30 minutes)**
- Last chance to find any missing wildlife signs
- Morning bird activity observation
- Complete requirement checklists

**8:00 AM - Breakfast & Leave No Trace Cleanup (60 minutes)**
- **Requirement: "Trash Your Trash"**
- Thorough campsite inspection
- Remove all trash and food scraps
- Leave campsite better than found
- Final Leave No Trace principles review

**9:30 AM - Adventure Completion Ceremony (30 minutes)**
- Review all requirements completed
- Celebrate discoveries and learning
- Share favorite moments from weekend
- Recognition for completing Bear Habitat

**10:00 AM - Departure**
