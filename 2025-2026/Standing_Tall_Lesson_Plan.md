## Lesson Objectives
By the end of this meeting, scouts will:
- Learn personal safety rules and protective guidelines
- Understand personal space and body safety
- Identify safety gear for different activities
- Begin setting up family digital device policies
- Feel empowered to keep themselves safe

## Important Notes for Leaders

## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome:** "Today we learn how to stand tall and stay safe!"
- **Safety Connection:** Bears protect themselves and their cubs - we'll learn to protect ourselves
- **Comfort Check:** Remind scouts they can ask questions or talk privately if needed
- **Opening:** Scout Law - "A Scout is trustworthy, helpful, friendly, kind..."

### Activity 1: Protect Yourself Rules Video & Discussion (18 minutes)
**Requirement:** With permission from your parent or legal guardian, watch the Protect Yourself Rules video for the Bear rank

**Video Viewing (10 minutes):**
- Introduce the video as important safety information
- Watch BSA "Protect Yourself Rules" video for Bear rank
- Ensure all scouts have parental permission (confirm beforehand)

**Group Discussion (8 minutes):**
- "What were the most important things you learned?"
- "Which rules do you think are most important to remember?"
- "How can these rules help keep you safe?"
- Reinforce key messages without repeating sensitive content

### Activity 2: Personal Space Bubble Workshop (15 minutes)
**Requirement:** Complete the Personal Space Bubble worksheet that is part of the Protect Yourself Rules resources

**Personal Space Activity:**
- Distribute Personal Space Bubble worksheets (BSA approved)
- Explain concept of personal space and body boundaries
- Complete worksheets individually with leader support
- Discuss comfort levels and trusted adults

**Trust Circle Exercise:**
- Identify trusted adults in their lives
- Discuss when and how to talk to trusted adults
- Practice saying "no" and getting help
- Role-play asking for help from trusted adults

**Key Messages:**
- Your body belongs to you
- Trust your feelings
- It's okay to say "no"
- Always tell a trusted adult

### Activity 3: Safety Gear Demonstration & Identification (15 minutes)
**Requirement 4:** Identify common personal safety gear for your head, eyes, mouth, hands, and feet. List how each of these items protect you. Demonstrate the proper use of personal safety gear for an activity.

**Safety Gear Stations:**

**Head Protection Station:**
- Helmets (bike, sports, construction)
- How they protect: prevent head injuries from impacts
- Proper fit and use demonstration

**Eye Protection Station:**
- Safety goggles, sunglasses, sports glasses
- How they protect: shield from debris, harmful rays, impacts
- Proper wearing and care

**Mouth Protection Station:**
- Mouthguards for sports
- How they protect: prevent dental injuries
- Proper fitting and cleaning

**Hand Protection Station:**
- Gloves (work, sports, weather)
- How they protect: prevent cuts, burns, weather damage
- Different gloves for different activities

**Foot Protection Station:**
- Various shoes/boots (hiking, sports, safety)
- How they protect: prevent injuries, provide traction
- Proper fit and activity matching

**Demonstration Activity:**
- Each scout picks an activity (biking, hiking, sports, etc.)
- Identifies and demonstrates proper safety gear use
- Explains how each piece protects them
- Group discusses gear for upcoming camping trip

### Activity 4: Family Digital Device Policy Planning (7 minutes)
**Requirement 3:** With your parent or legal guardian, set up a family policy for digital devices

**Policy Discussion:**
- Talk about digital devices they use (tablets, phones, games)
- Discuss appropriate use and safety online
- Introduce concept of family rules for device use

**Planning Session:**
- Scouts fill out "Family Digital Policy Planning Sheet"
- Include time limits, appropriate content, safe usage
- Plan when and how to talk with parents about this
- Emphasize this is homework to complete with families

**Safe Digital Habits:**
- Never share personal information online
- Tell trusted adults about uncomfortable online experiences
- Use devices in common family areas
- Take breaks from screens for physical activity

## Notes for Den Leader

### Youth Protection Compliance:
- Ensure all adults present are YPT trained
- Have clear reporting procedures ready
- Create safe, supportive environment for discussion
- Follow up privately with any concerning responses

### Sensitive Topic Management:
- Keep discussions age-appropriate
- Don't ask for personal examples of unsafe situations
- Focus on empowerment and safety skills
- Have contact information for parents readily available

### Safety Gear Tips:
- Borrow gear from local emergency services, sports stores
- Emphasize proper fit is crucial for protection
- Connect to upcoming outdoor activities
- Let scouts handle and try on appropriate gear

### Family Communication:
- Send home summary of topics covered
- Provide family discussion prompts
- Include digital policy template for families
- Remind families this is ongoing conversation
