## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome:** "Thankful Bears gather together!"
- **Gratitude Opening:** Each scout shares one thing they're grateful for
- **Fellowship Spirit:** "We celebrate how families show reverence and kindness"
- **Opening:** Moment of gratitude and reflection

### Activity 1: Acts of Kindness Sharing (15 minutes)
**Requirement 5 Completion:** Carry out an act of kindness

**Kindness Circle:**
- Scouts share acts of kindness they performed since last meeting
- Celebrate both big and small acts of service
- Discuss how kindness made them feel
- Connect kindness to family values and traditions

**Acts of Kindness Examples Shared:**
- Helping family members with chores
- Being kind to classmates or neighbors
- Volunteering with family or community
- Environmental stewardship actions
- Random acts of kindness

**Impact Discussion:**
- "How did performing kindness make you feel?"
- "How did others react to your kindness?"
- "Why do you think kindness is important in many traditions?"
- "How can we continue showing kindness every day?"

### Activity 2: Learning About Other Religions/Beliefs (15 minutes)
**Requirement 6 Completion:** Identify a religion or faith different from your own and determine two things it has in common with your family's beliefs

**Respectful Sharing:**
- Scouts share what they learned about different religions/beliefs
- Focus on common values discovered with families
- Emphasize respect and understanding
- Celebrate diversity while finding unity

**Common Values Discovered:**
- Golden Rule variations (treat others as you want to be treated)
- Importance of helping those in need
- Gratitude and thankfulness practices
- Family and community importance
- Environmental stewardship
- Peace and kindness teachings

**Unity in Diversity Activity:**
- Create visual display of common values found
- Discuss how different traditions teach similar positive lessons
- Emphasize respect for all beliefs and traditions
- Connect to Scout Law values

### Activity 3: Thankfulness Reflection & Scoutsgiving Prep (20 minutes)
**Purpose:** Practice gratitude and prepare for upcoming Scoutsgiving

**Gratitude Activity (10 minutes):**
- Individual thankfulness reflection time
- Create gratitude lists or journal entries
- Share family traditions of giving thanks
- Connect to upcoming Scoutsgiving celebration

**Scoutsgiving Preparation (10 minutes):**
- Plan November 19 Scoutsgiving activities
- Discuss service project for the community
- Prepare gratitude presentations for families
- Plan how to share Fellowship learning at Scoutsgiving

**Service Project Planning:**
- Choose community service activity for upcoming events
- Connect service to Fellowship adventure themes
- Plan how families can participate
- Emphasize ongoing commitment to helping others

### Activity 4: Fellowship Adventure Completion & Commitment (5 minutes)

**Adventure Requirements Review:**
- ✅ Talked about family faith traditions
- ✅ Identified three holidays/celebrations
- ✅ Made craft related to faith tradition
- ✅ Attended religious service/gathering (family report)
- ✅ Carried out act of kindness
- ✅ Learned about different religion and found common values

**Fellowship Completion Celebration:**
- Present Fellowship completion certificates
- Acknowledge family involvement in learning
- Celebrate growth in understanding and reverence
- Recognize commitment to kindness and service

**Ongoing Commitment:**
- Pledge to continue acts of kindness
- Commit to respectful understanding of different beliefs
- Promise to participate in family faith traditions
- Plan ongoing service to community
