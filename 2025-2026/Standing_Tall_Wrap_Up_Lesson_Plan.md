## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome:** "Safety-smart Bears are back!"
- **Safety Check:** "Who practiced safety rules at home this week?"
- **Confidence Building:** "You're learning to protect yourselves like strong bears!"
- **Opening:** Scout Law emphasizing "trustworthy" and "helpful"

### Activity 1: Family Work Review & Sharing (15 minutes)
**Requirement Completion:** Review family homework assignments

**Digital Device Policy Review:**
- Scouts share their family digital device policies (without sharing personal details)
- Discuss common rules families created
- Celebrate successful family conversations
- Review online safety concepts learned

**Home Safety Rules Discussion:**
- Each family created home/personal safety rules
- Scouts share examples of rules that help keep them safe
- Discuss emergency procedures and trusted adults
- Emphasize importance of family communication about safety

**Safety Skills Assessment:**
- Quick review of personal space and body safety concepts
- Confirm understanding of "no, go, tell" concepts
- Review when and how to get help from trusted adults

### Activity 2: Safety Role-Play & Scenarios (20 minutes)
**Purpose:** Practice safety skills through age-appropriate scenarios

**Safety Scenario Stations:**

**Station 1: Stranger Safety (7 minutes)**
- Role-play appropriate responses to strangers
- Practice saying "no" confidently
- Demonstrate getting help from trusted adults
- Emphasize staying with group during activities

**Station 2: Emergency Situations (7 minutes)**
- Practice calling for help
- Role-play what to do if lost
- Demonstrate using emergency whistle
- Review when to dial 911

**Station 3: Personal Safety (6 minutes)**
- Practice body safety concepts from previous meeting
- Role-play asking for help when feeling unsafe
- Demonstrate respecting others' personal space
- Review trusted adult identification

**Key Learning Reinforcement:**
- Your safety is most important
- Trust your feelings about situations
- It's always okay to ask for help
- Trusted adults are there to help you

### Activity 3: Safety Game Show (15 minutes)
**Purpose:** Review safety concepts through engaging competition

**Game Format:** Team-based safety knowledge review

**Round 1: Safety Gear Challenge (5 minutes)**
- Teams identify safety gear and explain protection
- Quick demonstrations of proper gear use
- Connect to outdoor adventures and activities

**Round 2: Safety Rule Charades (5 minutes)**
- Act out safety rules without words
- Other teams guess the safety rule
- Include digital safety, personal safety, and outdoor safety

**Round 3: Emergency Response Quiz (5 minutes)**
- Quick questions about emergency procedures
- Practice important phone numbers and information
- Review when and how to get help

**Celebration:** All teams win for showing excellent safety knowledge!

### Activity 4: Adventure Completion & Looking Ahead (5 minutes)

**Standing Tall Completion:**
- Review all requirements completed
- Present Standing Tall completion certificates
- Celebrate growth in safety awareness and skills
- Acknowledge family involvement in safety education

**Connection to Outdoor Safety:**
- Link personal safety to outdoor adventure safety
- Discuss how Standing Tall helps with camping and hiking
- Preview safety considerations for upcoming Chino Hike (Oct 25)
- Connect to Joshua Tree campout safety planning

**Ongoing Safety Commitment:**
- Scouts pledge to continue practicing safety skills
- Commit to talking with families about safety concerns
- Promise to help other scouts stay safe
- Plan to use safety skills in all activities
