## Lesson Structure

### Opening Circle (5 minutes)
- **Welcome:** "Citizen Bears ready to serve!"
- **Service Preparation:** Review service project plan and expectations
- **Civic Responsibility:** "Today we show what good citizens do"
- **Opening:** Pledge of Allegiance and Scout Oath

### Activity 1: Community Service Project (35 minutes)
**Requirement 5 Completion:** Participate in a service project

**Service Project Options Based on Previous Planning:**

**Option A: Food Bank Service**
- Sort and package food donations
- Prepare meal bags for families
- Learn about hunger in community
- Meet food bank volunteers and staff

**Option B: Animal Shelter Support**
- Prepare care packages for shelter animals
- Create toys and enrichment items
- Learn about animal care and adoption
- Meet shelter staff and volunteers

**Option C: Environmental Service**
- Community cleanup in local park or area
- Plant flowers or trees (if season appropriate)
- Create awareness materials about environmental protection
- Learn about environmental stewardship

**Option D: Senior Community Service**
- Create cards and crafts for nursing home residents
- Perform songs or skits for seniors
- Help with simple activities or games
- Learn about respecting and honoring elders

**Service Project Execution:**
- Follow safety guidelines and supervision requirements
- Work respectfully and enthusiastically
- Ask questions and learn about organization
- Document service with photos and notes
- Thank organization staff and volunteers

**Learning During Service:**
- Observe how nonprofit operates
- See volunteers and funding in action
- Understand community needs being addressed
- Experience satisfaction of helping others

### Activity 2: Service Reflection & Civic Discussion (15 minutes)
**Purpose:** Process service experience and connect to citizenship

**Service Experience Reflection:**
- "How did it feel to help others in our community?"
- "What did you learn about the organization we helped?"
- "How do you think our service made a difference?"
- "What would you like to do to help again?"

**Citizenship Connection:**
- Discuss how service is part of being a good citizen
- Connect to Scout Oath "duty to country"
- Talk about how communities work together
- Emphasize ongoing responsibility to help others

**Impact Understanding:**
- Learn about the difference their service made
- Understand how small actions create big impact
- Discuss ways to continue helping at home
- Connect individual action to community improvement
