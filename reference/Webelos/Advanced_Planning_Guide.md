# Webelos Advanced Planning Guide — Heavy Coordination Only

> Only requirements that need significant lead time, outside coordination, or qualified training. Listed by requirement number within each badge. Required badges first.

---

## Required Adventures

### Stronger, Faster, Higher
- **Req 1 — Plan, cook, and eat a balanced meal:** Kitchen/camp kitchen, ingredients, allergy & AHMR review (+ EpiPen plan). If knives used, **Chef's Knife must be completed first**. *Camp Cuisine* option = pack campout + campsite reservation + BALOO-trained leader.

### My Community
- **Req 2 — Speak with an elected official:** Advance outreach to schedule official (city council, school board, mayor, etc.) or field trip to a government meeting; brief them on the majority/plurality question.
- **Req 4 — Service project:** Partner organization (food pantry, *10,000 for Troops*, drive recipient), collection logistics, *Service Project Planning Checklist* + *SAFE Project Tool Use*; council *Scouting for Food* is seasonal.

### Webelos Walkabout
- **Req 2–6 — 2-mile walk as a unit:** Route scout + park/trail permission + transportation + Six Essentials per Scout + weather check (*Hazardous Weather* training, *Weather Related Safety* moment, contingency) + AHMR/EpiPen plan + buddy system & adult ratio.
- **Req 5 — First-aid demo (blister, sprain, sunburn, dehydration/heat illness):** Often needs guest — Scouts BSA troop, first responder, nurse/EMT.

---

## Elective Adventures

### Aquanaut
- **Req 5 — Attempt swimmer test & Req 6 — 30+ min free swim:** Pool/lake reservation + **Safe Swim Defense trained adult 21+ (1:10 ratio)** + buddy tags, ability groups, reach/throw gear.

### Paddle Onward
- **Req 1 — Pass swimmer test (before Req 5–9):** Separate pool session with Safe Swim Defense.
- **Req 3–9 — Safety Afloat + paddle skills + 30+ min paddle:** Flat calm water venue only (pond/controlled lake — no running water/expeditions) + **Safety Afloat trained adult 21+ (within 2 yrs) + CPR-trained adult (1:5 ratio)** + USCG life jackets (sized), craft, paddles, buddy tags, tip-over drill.

### Let's Camp Webelos
- **Req 1 — Overnight campout:** Campsite reservation + **BALOO-trained leader mandatory** + AHMR + *Hazardous Weather* training.
- **Req 6–7 — Fire safety + teepee fire lay/light/extinguish:** Fire restrictions/permit, fire ring, tinder/kindling/fuel wood, adult supervision.

### Catch the Big One
- **Req 1 & Req 7 — Plan fishing + ≥1 hour fishing:** Fishing venue permission/reservation + state licenses/permits/regulations/season + rods/tackle/bait + weather backup + offsite transportation.

### Build It
- **Req 4 — Build carpentry project:** Lumber/fasteners sourcing, tools (saws, levels, squares), workshop space, safety glasses, age-appropriate training + continuous supervision (plan + build = 2+ meetings).

### Champions for Nature Webelos
- **Req 6 — Conservation service project:** Partner (park/wildlife org) + location + *Service Project Planning Checklist* + *SAFE Project Tool Use*; bee hotel/bat house builds need tools & supervision.

### Earth Rocks
- **Req 2 — Find & safely break apart a rock/geode:** Eye protection, hammer/chisel, protective clothing; test beforehand.
- **Req 4 — Grow a crystal:** Multi-day growth; supplies (borax/alum/sugar).

### Pedal Away
- **Req 5 — Map ≥5-mile ride & Req 6 — Buddy-system ride:** Bike + helmet per Scout (e-bikes not allowed), **5-mile mapped route** (avoid busy roads, local regs), ABC Quick Check, support/first aid, weather plan; chain lube + locks for Req 3–4.

### Aware and Care
- **Req 4 — Meet someone with a disability or who works with people with disabilities:** Significant lead time to identify/schedule guest or facility (rehab center, service-dog org) + permissions/transportation.

### Race Time Webelos
- **Req 1 — Build Derby car / Regatta boat with adult:** Bulk official kits + tools/venue.
- **Req 5 — Participate in race:** Pack/district race event — track or raingutter trough + rules/sportsmanship.

### Tech on the Trail
- **Req 2 — Use GPS/geocaching & Req 3–4 — Plan & take 2-mile trek:** Smartphones/tablets, GPS app/account, online mapping, cell coverage; route/weather/Six Essentials/buddy system for trek.

### Chef's Knife — Schedule Early (Unlocks All Knife Use)
- **Req 3–4 — Kitchen knife care/use + slice/dice/mince:** Location must permit knives + supervision ratio + sharp knives/boards + food for practice. **Required before any Webelos knife use** (even if Bear Whittling earned).

### Special Electives — Not Den-Plannable
- **Archery / Slingshot / BB Guns:** Only at approved council/district events with qualified instructors.

---

*All other requirements (e.g., Bobcat, My Safety video, My Family faith discussions, Art Explosion, Modular Design, Yo-Yo, Math on the Trail) need only standard den supplies or at-home parent completion — no heavy coordination.*
