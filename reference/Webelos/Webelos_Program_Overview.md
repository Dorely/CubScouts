# Webelos Cub Scout Program Overview

## Requirements to Earn Webelos Badge of Rank
To earn the Webelos badge of rank (4th grade), a Cub Scout must complete:
- **6 required Adventures** (complete all)
- **2 elective Adventures** (any two of the available elective adventures)

## Required Adventures (Complete All 6)

### 1. Bobcat Webelos Adventure
- **First Required Adventure** — Must be completed before other adventures
- Focus: Introduction to Scouting, Scout Oath & Law, den relationships
- Key Requirements: Learn Scout basics, create den Code of Conduct, youth protection

### 2. Stronger, Faster, Higher Adventure
- Focus: Personal fitness — nutrition, exercise, and rest
- Key Requirements: Plan and eat a balanced meal, 30-minute group activity, 15-minute personal fitness, relaxing activity, health record review

### 3. My Safety Adventure
- Focus: Personal safety and home safety
- Key Requirements: Protect Yourself Rules video, hazardous household items storage, home/meeting-space safety, natural-events preparedness

### 4. My Family Adventure
- Focus: Family & reverence — faith traditions and kindness
- Key Requirements: Family faith traditions craft, act of kindness, interfaith understanding, reverence reflection

### 5. My Community Adventure
- Focus: Citizenship — voting and government
- Key Requirements: Majority vs. plurality voting, meet an elected official, federal law timeline, service project

### 6. Webelos Walkabout Adventure
- Focus: Outdoors — hiking preparation
- Key Requirements: Prepare for and complete a 2-mile walk with Cub Scout Six Essentials, route planning, weather, SAFE Checklist, first aid, Leave No Trace and Outdoor Code

## Elective Adventures
Webelos may complete as many elective adventures as they wish. Available elective adventures include:

### Standard Elective Adventures (17 Total)
- **Aquanaut** — Swimming safety, order of rescue, hypothermia, swimmer test
- **Art Explosion** — Drawing, paint, digital filters, mixed media
- **Aware and Care** — Visual/hearing impairment awareness, barriers to access, disabilities
- **Build It** — Tool safety, plumb/level/square, carpentry project
- **Catch the Big One** — Fishing planning, safety, knots, and 1+ hour fishing
- **Champions for Nature Webelos** — Habitats, threatened/endangered species, conservation project
- **Chef's Knife** — Kitchen knife safety and use (required before using any knife in Webelos)
- **Earth Rocks** — Rock types, mineral hardness, crystals and geology
- **Let's Camp Webelos** — Campout planning, tent setup, weather hazards, bowline, fire building, Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace
- **Math on the Trail** — Pace measurement and hiking-time estimation
- **Modular Design** — Modular construction and instruction writing
- **Paddle Onward** — Canoe/kayak/SUP — swimmer prerequisite, Safety Afloat, paddle skills, 30+ minutes afloat
- **Pedal Away** — Bicycle gear, chain lubrication, locks, 5-mile ride planning and completion
- **Race Time Webelos** — Pinewood Derby and Raingutter Regatta — propulsion and friction
- **Summertime Fun Webelos** — Summer participation (3 activities May–August)
- **Tech on the Trail** — Outdoor technology, GPS/geocaching, mapping a 2-mile trek
- **Yo-yo** — Yo-yo safety, stringing, and tricks (gravity pull, sleeper, breakaway)

### Special Elective Adventures
These adventures can only be completed at approved events with qualified instructors (no standalone Cub Scout adventure pages found at `scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/` — listed on the Webelos overview page only):
- **Archery Webelos** — Archery skills and safety
- **Slingshot** — Slingshot skills and safety
- **BB Guns** — BB gun safety and marksmanship

## Special Notes
- **Bobcat Webelos Adventure** is designed to be completed first
- All adventures emphasize safety through the Scouting America SAFE Checklist
- Many adventures include optional activities to choose from for each requirement
- Some adventures have prerequisites or special safety training (e.g., Safe Swim Defense, Safety Afloat, BALOO for overnight camping, Chef's Knife before any knife use)
- Program reflects the June 1, 2024 Cub Scout program updates (Fun, Simple, and Easy)
