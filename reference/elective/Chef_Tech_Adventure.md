# Chef Tech Adventure (Elective)

**Official Source**: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/chef-tech/

## Snapshot of Adventure
Cooking is a great way to see how technology has impacted our lives. As a Cub Scout, you may cook a hot dog using a stick on an open fire, which is the same way humans have cooked with fire for thousands of years. You could also cook a hot dog by boiling it in water in a pot on a stove. The fastest way to cook a hot dog is in a microwave in a matter of seconds. In this Adventure, we will explore technology in the kitchen.

## Prerequisites
**IMPORTANT:** Before starting this Adventure, each Cub Scout must have completed the Whittling Adventure because this adventure involves knife safety for kitchen knives.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1
Pick a kitchen appliance that uses technology to complete the following requirements.

### Requirement 2
With an adult, review the safety guidelines in the owner's manual and follow all safety guidelines.

### Requirement 3
With adult supervision, download a cooking app or search online to find a recipe.

### Requirement 4
With adult supervision, follow the selected recipe and make the recipe.

### Requirement 5
Think of a way technology can improve the appliance used to make your recipe.

## Activity Ideas

### Requirement 1 Activities
- **Now we are Cooking with Air**: Cub Scouts learn about air frying
- **Pick a Pot**: Cub Scouts learn about pressure cooking
- **The Science Oven**: Cub Scouts learn about microwave ovens
- Explore different kitchen appliances (blenders, food processors, slow cookers, toasters)
- Identify which appliances use technology and how

### Requirement 2 Activities
- **Read Me First**: Cub Scouts read and review the owner's manual for the chosen kitchen device
- Review safety features of kitchen appliances
- Practice safe operation procedures
- Identify warning labels and safety symbols

### Requirement 3 Activities
- **Time for an App-etizer**: Use the internet to identify a recipe that can be made in a den meeting
- Download age-appropriate cooking apps with adult supervision
- Search cooking websites for simple, safe recipes
- Learn to evaluate online recipes for safety and difficulty

### Requirement 4 Activities
- **It's Time To Be A Tech Chef**: Using the chosen appliance in Requirement 1 and the recipe chosen in Requirement 3, make the recipe
- Follow recipe instructions step-by-step
- Practice measuring ingredients accurately
- Use kitchen technology safely under supervision

### Requirement 5 Activities
- **Brainstorming Bear Chefs**: Cub Scouts brainstorm ideas on how to improve the appliance used in requirement 3
- **What If It Did This?**: Using cardboard, make a model of the appliance you used with one improvement
- Discuss innovations in kitchen technology
- Design drawings of improved appliances
- Think about future kitchen technology

## Safety Notes

### Before starting this Adventure:
- Review the Food Allergies safety moment
- Review each Cub Scout's Scouting America Annual Health and Medical Record for any food allergies or restrictions
- Ask if any member of the den has religious or cultural dietary concerns
- If someone carries an EpiPen due to severe allergies, ensure at least one adult knows how to administer it
- Review the Keep Your Food Safe guide to properly keep, store, and prepare food

### Knife Safety (Required):
- **Each Cub Scout must have completed the Whittling Adventure before starting Chef Tech**
- Watch the Cub Scout Knife Safety video
- Confirm that your meeting location will permit pocketknives or kitchen knives
- Know the Cub Scout knife safety rules:
  - **Stop** – make sure no one else is within arm's reach
  - **Away** – always cut away from your finger or other body parts
  - **Sharp** – a sharp, clean knife is a safe knife
  - **Store** – knives closed, in a sheath or knife block
- Secure additional adult supervision as needed

### During the Adventure:
- Review the Cub Scout knife safety rules
- Check that all knives being used are sharp and in good condition
- Provide continuous adult supervision for all cooking activities
- Use age-appropriate kitchen appliances
- Follow manufacturer's safety guidelines for all appliances
- Maintain clean work surfaces and practice good hygiene

### Digital Safety:
- Review Digital Safety and Online Scouting Activities guidelines before using cooking apps or searching online
- Ensure adult supervision when using digital devices
- Use age-appropriate, safe websites and apps

## Key Concepts

### Kitchen Technology
Examples of kitchen appliances that use technology:
- Microwave ovens (electromagnetic radiation)
- Air fryers (convection heating)
- Pressure cookers (steam pressure)
- Blenders and food processors (motors and blades)
- Slow cookers (temperature control)
- Toasters and toaster ovens (heating elements and timers)

### Technology Improvements
Ways technology can improve kitchen appliances:
- Timers and automatic shut-off for safety
- Temperature sensors for precision cooking
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Easier cleaning features
- Connectivity and smart features
- Better safety mechanisms
