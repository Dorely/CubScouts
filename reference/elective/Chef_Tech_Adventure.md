# Chef Tech Adventure (Elective)

## Snapshot of Adventure
Technology is everywhere, including in the kitchen! Modern cooking uses many different tools and technologies to help prepare food safely and efficiently. In this Adventure, you'll learn about kitchen technology and how it helps us cook better food.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1
Learn about kitchen technology and tools that help with food preparation.

### Requirement 2
Demonstrate how to use a food thermometer to check food temperature.

### Requirement 3
Learn about food safety and proper food storage using technology.

### Requirement 4
Use technology to find and prepare a simple, healthy recipe.

### Requirement 5
Demonstrate proper hand washing and kitchen hygiene practices.

### Requirement 6
Prepare a snack or meal using kitchen technology and tools.

## Activity Ideas

### Requirement 1 Activities
- Cub Scouts explore different kitchen appliances and their functions
- Learn about measuring tools, timers, and temperature devices
- Identify technology that helps make cooking easier and safer

### Requirement 2 Activities
- Cub Scouts practice using food thermometers correctly
- Learn safe internal temperatures for different foods
- Practice checking temperatures of various foods

### Requirement 3 Activities
- Cub Scouts learn about refrigeration and food storage technology
- Discuss expiration dates and food safety
- Learn about proper food storage containers and methods

### Requirement 4 Activities
- Cub Scouts use tablets or computers to find healthy recipes
- Learn to use recipe apps or websites safely
- Practice following digital recipes step by step

### Requirement 5 Activities
- Cub Scouts demonstrate proper handwashing technique
- Learn about kitchen cleanliness and sanitation
- Practice cleaning kitchen tools and surfaces

### Requirement 6 Activities
- Cub Scouts prepare simple snacks using kitchen tools
- Use timers, measuring tools, and other kitchen technology
- Practice safe food preparation techniques

## Safety Notes
- Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist for all activities
- Provide adult supervision for all kitchen activities
- Ensure proper food safety and hygiene practices
- Check for food allergies before any cooking activities
- Use age-appropriate kitchen tools and equipment
- Follow proper handwashing and sanitation procedures
- Maintain clean work surfaces and tools
- Store food at proper temperatures
