# Super Science Adventure (Elective)

## Snapshot of Adventure
Have you ever wondered why the sky is blue or how gravity works or what makes a rainbow? Scientists wonder about those things, too.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1
Conduct static electricity investigation.

### Requirement 2
Conduct the sink-or-float investigation.

### Requirement 3
Conduct the color-morphing investigation.

### Requirement 4
Conduct the color-layering investigation.

## Activity Ideas

### Requirement 1 Activities
- Foil reaction to static electricity
- Cub Scouts use static electricity to push a can

### Requirement 2 Activities
- Cub Scouts learn about experiment variables

### Requirement 3 Activities
- Cub Scouts test to see if liquids mix together

### Requirement 4 Activities
- Cub Scouts create a rainbow using 3 simple ingredients

## Safety Notes
- Review instructions completely for each experiment
- Conduct experiments before meetings to test them
- Confirm additional adult supervision as needed
- Review instructions fully with den before beginning
- Wear protective clothing and eye protection
- Keep food and drinks away from experiments
- Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist for all activities
- Follow Guide to Safe Scouting guidelines
