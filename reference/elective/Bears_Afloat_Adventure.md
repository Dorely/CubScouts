# Bears Afloat Adventure (Elective)

## Snapshot of Adventure
Water is an important part of outdoor fun. Whether it's a pool, lake, river, or ocean, being around water can mean lots of fun activities. But being around water also means following important safety rules. In this Adventure, you'll learn about staying safe around water and practice some basic boating skills.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1
Identify the safety equipment you need when going boating.

### Requirement 2
Learn and demonstrate how to safely board and exit a boat.

### Requirement 3
Learn and practice the proper way to sit in a boat.

### Requirement 4
Practice paddling a boat in calm water for at least 10 minutes.

### Requirement 5
Learn to steer a boat while paddling.

### Requirement 6
Make a boat that will float and move it across water.

## Activity Ideas

### Requirement 1 Activities
- Cub Scouts identify different types of life jackets and safety equipment
- Learn about required boating safety equipment
- Practice putting on life jackets properly

### Requirement 2 Activities
- Cub Scouts practice boarding and exiting boats safely
- Learn the three-point contact method for boat entry
- Practice boat boarding techniques on dry land first

### Requirement 3 Activities
- Cub Scouts learn proper sitting positions in different types of boats
- Practice maintaining low center of gravity in boats
- Learn how weight distribution affects boat stability

### Requirement 4 Activities
- Cub Scouts paddle canoes, kayaks, or rowboats in calm water
- Practice different paddle strokes
- Learn proper paddle grip and technique

### Requirement 5 Activities
- Cub Scouts learn steering techniques while paddling
- Practice turning boats left and right
- Learn how different paddle strokes affect boat direction

### Requirement 6 Activities
- Cub Scouts build boats using recycled materials
- Test homemade boats in water
- Design and build boats that can carry cargo

## Safety Notes
- All boating activities must follow Scouting America Safety Afloat guidelines
- Require proper fitting life jackets for all participants
- Adult supervision by trained boating instructor required
- Check weather conditions before any water activity
- Maintain proper adult-to-youth ratios for water activities
- Ensure all participants can swim or use non-swimmer protocols
- Use calm, protected waters only for Cub Scout activities
- Have rescue equipment readily available
