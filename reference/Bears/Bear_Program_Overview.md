# Bear Cub Scout Program Overview

## Requirements to Earn Bear Badge of Rank
To earn the Bear badge of rank, a Cub Scout must complete:
- **6 required Adventures** (complete all)
- **2 elective Adventures** (any two of the available elective adventures)

## Required Adventures (Complete All 6)

### 1. Bobcat Bear Adventure
- **First Required Adventure** - Must be completed before other adventures
- Focus: Introduction to Scouting, Scout Oath & Law, den relationships
- Key Requirements: Learn Scout basics, create den code of conduct, youth protection

### 2. Bear Habitat Adventure  
- Focus: Outdoor skills and Leave No Trace principles
- Key Requirements: Plan and complete a one-mile walk with Leave No Trace practices

### 3. Bear Strong Adventure
- Focus: Physical fitness and healthy living
- Key Requirements: Nutrition, exercise, relaxation, health record review

### 4. Fellowship Adventure
- Focus: Faith and family traditions (can be completed with Religious Emblem instead)
- Key Requirements: Family faith traditions, acts of kindness, interfaith understanding

### 5. Paws for Action Adventure
- Focus: Citizenship and community service
- Key Requirements: Flag etiquette, US symbols, nonprofit organizations, service project

### 6. Standing Tall Adventure
- Focus: Personal safety and protection
- Key Requirements: Protect Yourself Rules, personal space, digital safety, safety gear

## Elective Adventures
Bears may complete as many elective adventures as they wish. Available elective adventures include:

### Standard Elective Adventures (17 Total)
- **A Bear Goes Fishing** - Fishing skills, regulations, and water safety
- **Adventures in Coins** - Numismatics and coin collecting (available for all ranks)
- **Balancing Bears** - Balance skills and coordination activities
- **Baloo the Builder** - Woodworking and building projects with hand tools
- **Bears Afloat** - Boating safety and water activities
- **Bears on Bikes** - Bicycle safety and riding skills
- **Champions for Nature Bear** - Environmental conservation and natural resources
- **Chef Tech** - Kitchen technology and food safety
- **Critter Care** - Pet care and animal responsibility
- **Forensics** - Crime scene investigation and scientific analysis
- **Let's Camp Bear** - Camping skills and outdoor overnight experiences  
- **Marble Madness** - Traditional marble games and activities
- **Race Time Bear** - Pinewood Derby and Raingutter Regatta racing
- **Roaring Laughter** - Humor, jokes, and entertainment
- **Salmon Run** - Swimming, water safety, and aquatic skills
- **Summertime Fun Bear** - Summer safety and outdoor activities
- **Super Science** - Science experiments and investigations
- **Whittling** - Wood carving and knife safety

### Special Elective Adventures
These adventures require special instructors and approved events:
- **Archery Bear** - Archery skills and safety (special instructor required)
- **Slingshot** - Slingshot skills and safety (special instructor required) 
- **BB Guns** - BB gun safety and marksmanship (special instructor required)

## Special Notes
- **Bobcat Adventure** is designed to be completed first
- All adventures emphasize safety through the Scouting America SAFE Checklist
- Many adventures include optional activities to choose from for each requirement
- Some adventures have special safety requirements or instructor qualifications
- Program updates were implemented June 1, 2024, focusing on "Fun, Simple, and Easy"

## Adventure Organization
- **Required folder**: Contains all 7 required adventure reference materials
- **Elective folder**: For future elective adventure materials as they are added
