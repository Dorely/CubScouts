## Station 1 — Requirement 1: Celebrate America Through Art & Expression

**Station Name:** Stars, Stripes & Sketches

**Requirement:** Create something that celebrates America or what freedom means to you.

**Station Activity Choices (Den chooses one or more):**

- Draw "What America Means to Me"
- Design an America 250 poster
- Create a patriotic bookmark
- Make a patriotic coloring page station
- Decorate a mini-American flag craft

**Supplies:**

- Crayons/markers
- Construction paper
- Patriotic stickers
- Coloring sheets
- Scissors and glue

**Time:** 10–15 minutes

---

## Station 2 — Requirement 2: Learn About America's History

**Station Name:** History & Citizenship Discovery

**Requirement:** Learn about America's founding, symbols, or history.

**Station Activity Choices:**

- Audience Participation Story: "Cub Scouts Do Their Best!"
- Match historical flags to their time period
- Declaration of Independence mini lesson
- American symbols scavenger hunt
- Founding Fathers picture matching game

**Supplies:**

- Printed symbol cards
- Historic flag images
- Story scripts
- Trivia cards

**Time:** 10–15 minutes

---

## Station 3 — Requirement 3: Experience Colonial America

**Station Name:** Colonial Life Fun Fair

**Requirement:** Participate in an activity that connects Scouts to colonial-era life. The guide specifically highlights colonial games and candle making.

**Station Activity Choices:**

- Colonial Games Carnival
  - Hoop rolling
  - Graces (ribbon hoop game)
  - Cup-and-ball
  - Corn hole toss
- Rolled Beeswax Candle Making
- Quill pen writing activity
- Colonial toy demonstration

**Supplies:**

- Beeswax sheets and wicks
- Colonial game equipment
- Pencils/feather pens
- Activity instruction cards

**Time:** 15–20 minutes

---

## Station 4 — Requirement 4: Celebrate America's 250th Birthday

**Station Name:** America 250 Celebration Station

**Station Activity Choices:**

- America trivia challenge

## Station 5 — Requirement 5: Service to Others

**Station Name:** Service in Action

**Requirement:** Complete a service project that reflects citizenship and helping others. The guide specifically lists 10,000 for Troops and additional service project ideas.

**Station Activity Choices:**

- Create cards for 10,000 for Troops
- Write thank-you notes to veterans
- Assemble hygiene kits
- Scouting for Food collection project
- Community kindness cards

**Supplies:**

- Markers

- Service project materials
