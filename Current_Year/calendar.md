## Notes & Legend

* **Pack Event** — whole-pack activity; family attendance encouraged. Details (times, locations) are shown only when already confirmed from the GroupMe list.
* **Den Meeting** — weekly Wednesday meeting. Adventure assignments for September–December will be added once reference adventures are confirmed; January–May Wednesdays are currently **TBD** placeholders to preserve the weekly cadence.
* **No Meeting** — den does not meet (holiday or break). Listed explicitly so families can plan.
* All dates are for the **2026–2027 school/program year** (Aug 2026 – May 2027). Weekend dates that span multiple days are shown as ranges.
* **Gaps / TBD:** No den adventures, service projects, or spring pack events (Pinewood Derby, Blue & Gold, etc.) were included in the supplied GroupMe excerpt. Those are marked **TBD** above. When dates are confirmed, replace the TBD rows rather than reformatting the calendar.
* Previous year’s calendar is archived at `../Previous_Years/2025-2026/calendar.md` and is not the template for this year — this calendar follows the current `h3 + table` print style.
