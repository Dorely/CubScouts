## Opening and Bobcat Review — 10 Minutes

1. Have the denner lead the opening using the Cub Scout sign.
2. Recite the Scout Oath and Scout Law together.
3. Ask three Scouts to explain one point each of the Scout Oath.
4. Review the salute and handshake with partners.
5. Ask for one example of a Scout Law point shown during Lesson 1.

## Requirement 3 — 15-Minute Personal Exercise Circuit

Take up to 5 minutes to explain the circuit and demonstrate safe form. Then run the following uninterrupted 15-minute personal exercise block. Scouts work at their own pace and may use a lower-impact version of any movement.

| Minutes | Focus | Exercise Choices |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0–3 | Flexibility and warm-up | Arm circles, side reaches, walking lunges, gentle toe reaches |
| 3–6 | Heart rate | Jumping jacks, high knees, or fast marching |
| 6–9 | Muscles | Squats, wall push-ups, knee push-ups, or plank |
| 9–12 | Heart rate and muscles | Side shuffles, mountain climbers, or bear crawl |
| 12–15 | Flexibility and cool-down | Calf, thigh, shoulder, and seated butterfly stretches |

Give a water break after the timed block. Requirement 3 is complete when the Scout participates for the full 15 minutes in exercises that address all three areas: heart rate, muscles, and flexibility.

## Requirement 4 — Relaxing Activity for 10 Minutes

Use one complete 10-minute option:

- **Guided quiet breathing:** Sit or lie comfortably. Breathe in slowly, pause, and breathe out slowly while the leader guides attention from feet to shoulders.
- **Quiet reading:** Each Scout reads silently for the full 10 minutes.
- **Relaxing music:** Listen quietly while seated or lying down; keep conversation until the timer ends.
- **Slow observation walk:** Walk quietly together and notice sounds, colors, and movement without turning the walk into another fitness activity.

At the end, ask how each Scout's body and thoughts felt before and after the activity.

## Bobcat Catch-Up for New Scouts — 10 Minutes

Use returning Scouts as partners while the leader checks each new Scout individually:

- Recite the Scout Oath and Scout Law and describe the three points of the Oath.
- Name or demonstrate several points of the Scout Law.
- Review the den Code of Conduct.
- Explain one denner responsibility.
- Demonstrate the Cub Scout sign, salute, and handshake and explain when each is used.

Mark a Bobcat requirement complete only when the new Scout has actually completed or demonstrated it. Remind families that Bobcat Requirement 7 is completed at home with a parent or legal guardian; this review does not replace it.

## Homework Follow-Up and Closing — 10 Minutes

- **Requirement 1:** Confirm the next step for the den cooking outing, or remind each Scout to finish the family meal by planning, cooking, and eating it together.
- **Requirement 5:** Repeat the Annual Health and Medical Record homework. Record completion after the parent or guardian and Scout have reviewed it and discussed participation; do not ask the Scout to disclose private medical information to the group.
- Reflect: What helps your body most—balanced food, active exercise, or rest?
- Close with the Scout Oath or Scout Law and the Cub Scout sign.
