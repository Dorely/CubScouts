## Denner-Led Opening and Game — 10 Minutes

Have the denner lead the opening, run a short game, and help hand out maps and supplies during the meeting.

## Requirement 2 — Plan the Route — 15 Minutes

Hand out the planning maps and highlighters. Follow the [Plan A Route](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/plan-a-route/) activity and the 5 W's.

1. **Where are you going?** Point out the scale bar: the orange bar is 1/4 mile, so a 2-mile walk is eight of them. Find the trailhead star. Scouts highlight a route that starts and ends at the kiosk and adds up to at least 2 miles. Guide them toward the leader route: the Main Loop plus Conifers of California and the Aquatic Ecosystems pond loop. Point out Chaparral Vistas to Preservation Point as a strenuous 1.8-mile side trip that stays off the plan.
   - Ask: Where do we start and end? *(Trailhead and information kiosk.)*
   - Ask: How can we track how far we've gone? *(Phone GPS app, fitness watch, or checkpoints on the map.)*
   - Ask: How much time do we need? *(AllTrails lists the loop at 1–1.5 hours; plan about 80 minutes with breaks.)*
2. **When will you return?** Hike day is Saturday, October 10. The pack event runs 9:00–11:00 AM. Scouts set a start time (about 9:20) and a finish time (about 10:40).
3. **Who is hiking with you?** List den members, adults, and drivers. Assign buddies.
4. **Why are you going?** Scouts choose something to look for: redwoods and giant sequoias in the Conifers of California, wildlife at Duck Pond and Red-Wing Pond, the stream, or fall leaves in the Deciduous Forest.
5. **What are you taking?** The Cub Scout Six Essentials, closed-toe shoes, and layers.

Scouts record the plan on the Walkabout Plan or in the handbook. Collect a copy to send to families.

## Requirement 3 — Check the Forecast — 5 Minutes

Scouts open the weather app and look up Oak Glen, CA 92399, for Saturday morning, 9–11 AM. Record the forecast on the plan.

- What kind of weather is expected?
- What will the temperature be? Oak Glen is about 5,000 feet high, so mornings can be cooler than at home.
- Do we need special clothing or gear?
- Why check the weather before a 2-mile walk?

Forecast recorded: __________________________________________

Tell Scouts the den will check again the morning of the hike.

## Requirement 4 — SAFE Checklist for Oak Glen — 8 Minutes

Hand out the SAFE Checklist. Scouts take turns reading the four points, then apply each one to the hike:

- **Supervision:** Who are our adults? One adult leads, one sweeps at the back, and everyone hikes with a buddy. What do we do at each checkpoint? *(Headcount.)*
- **Assessment:** What risks could we meet? The Preserve map marks two steep sections on the Stream Trail. Walk carefully near pond edges. The Preserve lists mountain lions, black bears, rattlesnakes, and stinging nettle. Stay on the trail, keep the group together, and never approach an animal.
- **Fitness and Skill:** Why do leaders need the Annual Health and Medical Record? How do we set a pace that works for the slowest hiker?
- **Equipment and Environment:** Why do shoes need to fit the trail? What does the forecast mean for our clothing? What goes in the Six Essentials?

Ask: What steps will make our Oak Glen walk SAFE?

## Requirement 5 — Trail First-Aid Walk — 17 Minutes

Use the [Trail First Aid](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/trail-first-aid/) activity.

1. Spend 3 minutes showing each item in the first-aid bag and what it treats. Give each pair a set of response cards.
2. Scouts draw injury slips secretly and count off. Every Scout needs a slip; repeat injuries when the den has more than six Scouts.
3. Lead a pretend walk around the room. In order, each Scout acts out the injury. The den stops and gives first aid, then the walk resumes.
4. Rotate the first-aider role so every Scout **demonstrates first aid for all five events**: blister, sprained ankle, sunburn, dehydration, and heat-related illness. Have a different Scout lead each treatment and have partners repeat the key steps.

Check off each Scout on each of the five events before marking Requirement 5 complete.

- Blister
- Sprained ankle
- Sunburn
- Dehydration
- Heat-related illness


## Six Essentials Homework and Closing — 5 Minutes

- Send home the Six Essentials and clothing checklist. Scouts pack their own backpacks and bring them to Oak Glen, where the den checks them for Requirement 1.
- Remind families of the date, time, place, and meeting spot. Send the hike plan and route map home.
- Have the denner lead the closing.
