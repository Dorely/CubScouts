# My Community Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Learn about majority and plurality types of voting.
- [ ] **2.** Speak with someone who is elected to their position. Discover the type of voting that was used to elect them and why.
- [ ] **3.** Choose a federal law and create a timeline of the history of the law. Include the involvement of the 3 branches of government.
- [ ] **4.** Participate in a service project.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Voting and Laws

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 1, 3

Practice majority and plurality voting and make a federal-law timeline showing all three branches of government.

**Prep and materials:** Choose the law and gather sourced timeline material. Prepare ballot slips, a voting container, pencils, poster paper, and markers; add snack choices for Snack-lections.

### Day 2 — Elected Official and Service

**Approximate time:** 60–90 minutes · **Requirements:** 2, 4

Talk with an elected official about their election and participate in a small service project.

**Prep and materials:** Book the official and share the voting questions. Confirm the service recipient and requested supplies; gather cards, birthday-bag contents, or collection materials.

### Separate Service Activity

**Approximate time:** 60–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 4

Use a collection drive, delivery, or partner event for a larger service project.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm the partner, location, collection period, delivery arrangements, and family participation.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [My Community Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/required/My_Community_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/my-community/)

### Requirement 1

- **Denner Election**: Demonstrate plurality voting and majority voting to determine your next denner.
- **Snack-lections**: Using a variety of voting, select the snack for your next den meeting.

### Requirement 2

- **Our Elected Official**: Invite a community-elected official to learn about the type of voting used in their election.

### Requirement 3

- **History of the Americans with Disabilities Act**: Cub Scouts learn about the history of the Americans with Disabilities Act.

### Requirement 4

- **10,000 for the Troops - Webelos**: Cub Scouts participate in a special nation-wide project to create holiday cards for military personnel.
- **Diaper and Formula Drive**: With your den, host a diaper and formula drive to donate to an organization that gives them to those in need.
- **Happy Birthday Bag Project**: Cub Scouts help those less fortunate have a happy birthday by making birthday bags for a local food pantry.
- **Webelos Scouting for Food**: Participate in your Council-sponsored Scouting for Food event.
