# Bobcat Webelos Adventure Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Required — do first) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + open floor for games

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
>
> **Advanced planning needed? NO — but parent handout is critical before Day 1.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Print *How to Protect Your Children from Child Abuse: A Parent's Guide* booklet — one per family. Also print den Code of Conduct worksheet. Notify parents: Req 7 is done **at home with parent/guardian**; send booklet home Day 1 with due date. No venue, pool, or training to book.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Name tags, markers, large paper/whiteboard, printed Scout Oath & Law cards (one per Scout), den flag materials or poster board, attendance/denner rotation sheet.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** None. Fully den-based. If you miss the 2-meeting window, Req 7 can finish at home and be checked off next week — no extra den day needed.

**Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/required/Bobcat_Webelos_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Get to know members of your den.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Recite Scout Oath & Scout Law with den; describe 3 points of Scout Oath.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Learn about the Scout Law.
- [ ] **Req 4:** With den create a den Code of Conduct.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Learn about the denner position and responsibilities.
- [ ] **Req 6:** Demonstrate Cub Scout sign, salute, and handshake — show how each is used.
- [ ] **Req 7:** At home with parent/guardian do activities in *How to Protect Your Children from Child Abuse: A Parent's Guide* (homework — verify return).

## Day 1 — Welcome, Oath & Law, Den Identity (60 min)

*Classroom. Heavy on getting-to-know-you games — use outdoor flex if weather allows.*

**Opening (5 min)** — Welcome, review SAFE Checklist points, explain Bobcat is first before all other adventures.

**Activity 1 — Get to Know You (15 min) — Req 1**
- *Choose one:* Hula Hoop Pass (circle, no letting go) or Silent Line-Up (by birthday without talking). 7–9 is ideal.
- Debrief: one thing you learned about a denmate.

**Activity 2 — Oath & Law (20 min) — Req 2 & 3**
- Hand out Oath/Law cards. Read aloud together. Leader explains **3 points of Scout Oath:** *Duty to God and country, duty to others, duty to self.*
- Game: Scout Law Detective (12 points on strips — match to example) or Scout Law Relay (run, grab correct point).
- Each Scout recites Oath + Law with den (check off Req 2).

**Activity 3 — Code of Conduct (15 min) — Req 4**
- Brainstorm: "How do we want to treat each other?" Record 5–6 rules. All sign poster — this becomes den Code of Conduct. Post in room.

**Closing (5 min)** — Practice Cub Scout sign/salute/handshake preview for Day 2. Hand out Parent Guide booklets — explain Req 7 homework, return next week. Denner intro.

---

## Day 2 — Denner, Fundamentals & Wrap (60 min)

*No heavy coordination — this is intentionally light since Bobcat has no booking needs.*

**Opening (5 min)** — Roll call by denner, recite Oath & Law again.

**Activity 1 — Denner Position (15 min) — Req 5**
- Mini-lesson: denner duties (lead opening/closing, assist games, materials, line leader, attendance). Show denner rotation calendar.
- Practice: current denner leads Opening Ceremony.

**Activity 2 — Cub Scout Fundamentals (20 min) — Req 6**
- Demonstrate sign (right hand, two fingers), salute (hand to brow), handshake (two fingers along wrist). Pair practice — when to use each.
- Game: Roll a Cub Scout Fundamental (die with sign/salute/handshake images — do it correctly).

**Activity 3 — Code of Conduct + Protection Check (15 min) — Req 4 & 7**
- Revisit signed Code — any edits after one week? Finalize and photograph for families.
- Quick check: who completed Parent Guide at home? Collect verbal confirmations; follow up with families not yet done (no shaming — offer another copy).
- If time: Den Flag Webelos — add Bobcat symbols.

**Closing (5 min)** — First denner rotation posted. Celebrate Bobcat completion (those with Req 7 still pending finish at home — mark complete when returned).
