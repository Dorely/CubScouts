# My Family Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** With your parent or legal guardian, talk about your family's faith traditions. Identify three holidays or celebrations that are part of your family's faith traditions. Make a craft, work of art, or a food item that is part of your family's faith traditions.
- [ ] **2.** Carry out an act of kindness.
- [ ] **3.** With your parent or legal guardian identify a religion or faith that is different from your own. Identify two things that it has in common with your family's beliefs.
- [ ] **4.** Discuss with your parent or legal guardian what it means to be reverent. Tell how you practice being reverent in your daily life.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### At Home — Family Traditions and Reverence

**Approximate time:** 60–90 minutes across family conversations and a craft · **Requirements:** 1, 3, 4

Discuss family traditions, make a craft or food item, explore another faith, and talk about reverence.

**Prep and materials:** Print one My Family Passport per Scout and agree on a return date. Families choose the traditions, comparison faith, and craft or food supplies.

### At Home — Act of Kindness

**Approximate time:** 15–45 minutes · **Requirements:** 2

Help with dishes, a family chore, or a neighbor’s yard work.

**Prep and materials:** Choose the act of kindness with the family and gather the supplies it needs.

### Den Check-In

**Approximate time:** 10–15 minutes within another meeting · **Requirements:** 1–4 follow-up

Hand out passports and invite Scouts to share completed activities.

**Prep and materials:** Collect completed passports and record family-reported completion.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [My Family Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/required/My_Family_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/my-family/)

### Requirement 1

- **Multimedia Collage**: Create a multi-media collage celebrating your family's faith traditions.
- **My Family Passport**: Complete the Family and Reverence activities at home with your family.
- **Wood Crafting**: Make a gingerbread house from craft sticks that can be used as part of your favorite family faith tradition, holiday, or celebration.

### Requirement 2

- **A Dish of Kindness**: Clean up the dishes after dinner.
- **Helping Hands**: Help your family members with a chore.
- **My Family Passport**: Complete the Family and Reverence activities at home with your family.
- **Neighborly Kindness**: Help a neighbor with yard work.

### Requirement 3

- **Exploring Faith**: Learn about a religion different from your own and name two things that it has in common with your family's beliefs.
- **My Family Passport**: Complete the Family and Reverence activities at home with your family.

### Requirement 4

- **My Family Passport**: Complete the Family and Reverence activities at home with your family.
- **Reverence Reflection**: Discuss what it means to be reverent and how you practice being reverent in your daily life.
- **Tree of Reverence**: This activity will set a calm and reflective tone as Cub Scouts and their families arrive for the Reverence Adventure. It encourages participants to think about and express what reverence means to them in a creative way.
