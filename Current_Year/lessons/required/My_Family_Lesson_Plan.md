# My Family Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Required) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — parent notification for faith-based content (opt-out) before Day 1; no venue booking.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Send parent/guardian notification letter explaining that Reqs 1, 3, and 4 involve family faith traditions and reverence discussions. Offer **home-based opt-out**: families may complete any of those requirements at home via the *My Family Passport* family worksheet instead of in den. Collect acknowledgments. Print *Tree of Reverence* leaves, *My Family Passport* take-home packets, and kindness cards. Preview craft options for Req 1 (collage, wood craft sticks, or food item plan).
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** No venue or guest to book. Prepare kindness service station (cleanup/yard-work cards, thank-you notes) and *SAFE Project Tool Use* check if using tools. Prepare quiet reflection corner for reverence discussion.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Notification letters, My Family Passport packets, poster paper, markers, colored paper, scissors, glue sticks, craft sticks (if wood crafting), collage magazines or printed faith-holiday images, kindness action cards, gratitude notes/stationery, Tree of Reverence display (branch/ poster + paper leaves + string), attendance sheet.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** None required. Req 2 *Neighborly Kindness* may be extended as an optional auxiliary pack service hour (yard work, cards, chore help) — note as optional 3rd auxiliary service opportunity, not required for sign-off. All Reqs can finish in 2 den meetings + at-home family work.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/required/My_Family_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** With your parent or legal guardian, talk about your family's faith traditions. Identify three holidays or celebrations that are part of your family's faith traditions. Make a craft, work of art, or a food item that is part of your family's faith traditions.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Carry out an act of kindness.
- [ ] **Req 3:** With your parent or legal guardian identify a religion or faith that is different from your own. Identify two things that it has in common with your family's beliefs.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Discuss with your parent or legal guardian what it means to be reverent. Tell how you practice being reverent in your daily life.

## Day 1 — Family Traditions & Reverence at Home (60 min) — low-coordination requirements only

*Classroom. Quiet, respectful tone. Families who opt out do this work at home and return packet for verification — no on-the-spot faith disclosure required.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *reverent*), SAFE Checklist. Explain that family and faith are personal — we listen respectfully, we share only what we choose, and families lead these conversations at home.

**Activity 1 — Our Family Faith Traditions (25 min) — Req 1**
- Brief framing (5 min): Leader explains Req 1 is family-led. Scouts who are completing in den share only with permission; otherwise they work on the passport to finish at home — both paths count equally.
- Craft choice (20 min) — pick one classroom-friendly option for 7–9 scouts:
  - *Multimedia Collage*: Each Scout creates a collage showing three holidays/celebrations from their family's faith traditions (images, words, colors). Use magazines/printed icons; no one is asked to explain doctrine.
  - *Wood Crafting (craft sticks)*: Build a small gingerbread-house-style display or holiday frame from craft sticks to represent a family celebration.
  - Table alternative: plan a food item on paper (ingredients + occasion) to make at home with family — no cooking in den.
- Scouts label their three holidays on the back. Display briefly; send home for family discussion and sign-off. Completes Req 1 when family confirms conversation + craft/food item done (den craft + home conversation, or fully at home).

**Activity 2 — Reverence Reflection (25 min) — Req 4**
- *Tree of Reverence* (10 min on arrival + 15 min discussion): As Scouts arrive, each writes on a paper leaf what *reverent* means to them (respect, gratitude, quiet, care). Hang leaves on display.
- Small-group or partner share (10 min): Discuss prompts — What does it mean to be reverent? How do you show reverence daily (thankfulness, listening, caring for spaces, quiet reflection)? Use *Reverence Reflection* talking points — keep it personal, not comparative.
- *My Family Passport* bridge (5 min): Explain that final reverence conversation is completed at home with parent/guardian; Scouts bring back one sentence about how they practice reverence daily. This completes Req 4 with home verification (or den discussion if family opts to do it in den).

**Closing (5 min)**
Thank Scouts for respectful listening. Hand out *My Family Passport* packets for Reqs 1, 3, and 4 home completion. Preview Day 2 kindness service. Reminder: no Scout is required to share faith details in front of the den.

## Day 2 — Kindness in Action + Interfaith Understanding (60 min) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Classroom + outdoor flex if doing yard-work style kindness. No guest needed.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Roll call, revisit Code of Conduct (respect). Quick Tree of Reverence revisit — one new leaf if desired.

**Activity 1 — Acts of Kindness (30 min) — Req 2**
Choose classroom-appropriate kindness that fits 7–9 scouts and completes Req 2:
- *Helping Hands / A Dish of Kindness* role-play: Scouts practice offering help at home (clear table, dishes, laundry sort) and write a kindness pledge card for one act they will do this week with family.
- *Neighborly Kindness (den version)*: Assemble thank-you/kindness notes or small care cards for neighbors, custodians, or a local helper — no tools needed. If doing outdoor yard work (rake, pick up litter), review SAFE Project Tool Use, train on tools, maintain continuous adult supervision and age-appropriate tasks only.
- Each Scout logs their act on kindness card; leader verifies completion (in den or at-home follow-through both count).

**Activity 2 — Exploring Faith with Respect (20 min) — Req 3**
- Leader frames (5 min): We learn about others to find common ground; we do not debate beliefs.
- *Exploring Faith* paired work (15 min): With printed fact sheets about a faith different from one's own (general, respectful overview — e.g., shared values like kindness, service, gratitude), each Scout/pair identifies **two things it has in common** with their family's beliefs. Use sentence starters: "Both value …" / "Both celebrate … by …"
- Crucial: Scouts are not asked to present another faith as expert; they simply note two commonalities. Families may also complete this at home via passport — den work and home work are both valid. Completes Req 3 when Scout names two commonalities with parent/guardian acknowledgment.

**Closing (5 min)**
Collect/verify My Family Passport returns for Reqs 1, 3, 4 and kindness cards for Req 2. Offer grace period for home completion — sign off as packets return. Celebrate with a brief gratitude circle: one kind act you saw today.

## Auxiliary Day — Optional Kindness Service Extension

No auxiliary day is required — all requirements complete in two den meetings + at-home family work.

Optional 3rd auxiliary (pack or den service hour): Schedule a 60-minute *Neighborly Kindness* service opportunity (park clean-up, cards for service members, help with chartered org) for Scouts who want to extend Req 2. Use Service Project Planning Checklist, SAFE Project Tool Use, tool training, and qualified adult supervision. Log service hours separately; do not hold up adventure sign-off for Scouts who already completed Req 2 in den/at home.

## Materials Summary

Parent notification letters, My Family Passport packets (one per Scout), Tree of Reverence display materials, paper leaves, markers, colored paper, scissors, glue sticks, craft sticks, magazines/printed holiday images, kindness action cards, stationery for notes, poster paper, tape, attendance sheet. For optional service auxiliary: gloves, trash bags, stationery, age-appropriate tools with training plan.

## Safety Notes

Use SAFE Checklist every meeting; include Craft Tips review before craft. For any service with tools, review SAFE Project Tool Use, provide tool training, continuous qualified adult supervision, and follow manufacturer's age/skill restrictions (most restrictive applies). Keep faith discussions family-led and opt-out friendly — do not require personal disclosure. Maintain respectful, non-comparative tone. Follow Guide to Safe Scouting and local regulations.
