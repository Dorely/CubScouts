# Stronger, Faster, Higher Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Required) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — kitchen, ingredients, and health-record review must be booked before Day 2.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review each Scout's Scouting America Annual Health and Medical Record (AHMR) for food allergies, restrictions, religious/cultural dietary concerns, and EpiPen needs. Confirm at least one additional adult knows EpiPen administration. Send home AHMR review instruction for Req 5 (to be completed with parent/guardian and verified Day 2). If you will use knives, verify all Scouts have completed **Chef's Knife** adventure first — required before any Webelos knife use.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** Reserve kitchen or camp kitchen with hand-wash, refrigeration, and cooking surfaces. Purchase ingredients for one balanced meal (e.g., Culinary Creations: turkey tacos + fruit + water/milk). Plan allergy-safe substitutions. Print recipe cards and meal-planning worksheet. If choosing *Cub Camp Cuisine* campout option instead of den kitchen, reserve campsite and confirm a **BALOO-trained leader** is planning the campout; understand *Let's Camp* requirements.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Cones/markers for fitness circuits, jump ropes or balls, yoga mats or towels, speaker for relaxing music, printed balanced-meal worksheets, recipe cards, kitchen supplies (pots, pans, utensils, cutting boards, soap, paper towels, disposable plates/cups), ingredients per meal plan, first-aid kit, AHMR verification checklist, Food Allergies and Keep Your Food Safe handouts.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** Standard plan is den-based (2 meetings). If using *Cub Camp Cuisine*, the meal becomes an auxiliary pack campout day — schedule as 3rd auxiliary day and add campsite logistics, weather plan, and BALOO coordination.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/required/Stronger_Faster_Higher_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** With your den or family, plan, cook, and eat a balanced meal.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Be active for 30 minutes with your den or at least one other person in a way that includes both stretching and moving.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Be active for 15 minutes doing personal exercises that boost your heart rate, use your muscles, and work on flexibility.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Do a relaxing activity for 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Review your Scouting America Annual Health and Medical Record with your parent or legal guardian. Discuss your ability to participate in den and pack activities.

---

## Day 1 — Fitness, Stretch, and Reset (60 min) — low-coordination requirements only

*Classroom + outdoor flex or gym. No kitchen needed. Focus on moving, heart-rate, and rest.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law, SAFE Checklist preview. Explain Stronger, Faster, Higher = eat well + move well + rest well. Preview Day 2 kitchen meal — remind families to return AHMR verification.

**Activity 1 — Stretch + Move Together (30 min) — Req 2**
- Warm-up stretch circle (5 min): neck rolls, arm circles, leg swings — leader models, buddy mirrors.
- Active game (20 min): Choose one for 7–9 scouts: *Soccer and Scouting* (small-sided), *Frisbee Frenzy* (ultimate 4v4 with end zones), or *Volleyball Voyage* with beach ball. Emphasize continuous movement.
- Cool-down stretch (5 min): hamstrings, quads, calf. Count together. Check off Req 2 when 30 minutes inclusive of stretch is logged.

**Activity 2 — Personal Fitness Circuit (15 min) — Req 3**
*Scout Fit Challenge* stations — 3 rounds × 3 minutes with 1-minute transitions:
1. Heart rate: jumping jacks / high knees
2. Muscle: bodyweight squats / push-ups (knees allowed) / plank
3. Flexibility: toe touches, butterfly stretch, trunk twists
Scouts track reps on personal card; leader emphasizes form and breathing. Completes Req 3.

**Activity 3 — Relax & Reflect (10 min) — Req 4**
Choose one calming option:
- *Musical Tranquility*: dim lights, 10-minute lo-fi/ nature track, breathing (4-in, 4-hold, 4-out).
- *Book Bliss*: silent reading of Scout-themed book corner — no talking, focus on rest for mind.
- *Trailblazing Trek*: slow outdoor walk if weather allows — observe and breathe.
Debrief: How does rest help performance? Log Req 4.

**Closing (5 min)**
Water break, recap what was completed, preview meal planning worksheet for Day 2. Assign: bring AHMR review confirmation signed by parent/guardian; note any food restrictions by next meeting.

## Day 2 — Balanced Meal + Health Record Review (60 min) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Kitchen or camp kitchen required. Adult supervision ratio high; hand-wash station and allergy plan posted.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Hand-wash, aprons, safety briefing: Food Allergies moment, Keep Your Food Safe (cold storage, hand hygiene, cross-contact), EpiPen location, knife rule reminder (no knives unless Chef's Knife completed — use pre-cut or tear method if not).

**Activity 1 — Plan, Cook, and Eat a Balanced Meal (40 min) — Req 1**
*Culinary Creations (den meal) format:*
- **Plan (10 min):** Small groups design plate: protein + vegetable + grain + fruit + dairy/water. Use MyPlate visual. Vote on final menu (e.g., turkey or bean tacos with lettuce/tomato, brown rice, apple slices, milk/water). Check against allergy list.
- **Cook (20 min):** Stations (wash, assemble, heat with adult at stove, serve). Adults handle heat/knives if Chef's Knife not yet earned. Scouts measure, stir, assemble, set table.
- **Eat (10 min):** Together at table, discuss moderation and energy. Clean as you eat — wash, dry, sanitize. Completes Req 1 when all three — plan, cook, eat — are done as a den.

*If using Cub Camp Cuisine variant, move this entire block to the auxiliary campout and run a simple snack + meal-plan review on Day 2 instead.*

**Activity 2 — AHMR Review & Participation Check (10 min) — Req 5**
AHMR is home-based; den verifies only. Scouts share (no private medical detail disclosed) that they reviewed AHMR with parent/guardian and discussed ability to participate in den/pack activities. Leaders collect signed verification slips, confidentially note any activity modifications, and remind families to keep AHMR current on file. Provide AHMR link handout for updates.

**Closing (5 min)**
Thank kitchen host, reflection: which of eat/move/rest is hardest at home? Challenge: repeat 15-minute personal routine twice before next den meeting.

## Auxiliary Day — Cub Camp Cuisine Campout (only if elected)

If the den chooses the campout meal option, schedule a pack campout as a 3rd auxiliary day:
- **Logistics:** Campsite reservation, BALOO leader present, menu scaled for camp kitchen, cooler/ice plan, weather contingency, transportation, AHMR + EpiPen plan packed.
- **Program:** Scouts prepare the planned balanced meal at camp (same Req 1 standard), practice Leave No Trace clean-up, and combine with *Let's Camp* orientation if helpful.
- **Note:** Day 2 den meeting still runs (AHMR review + 30/15/10 fitness refresher or meal-plan rehearsal) so Scouts who miss the campout have a den path to Req 1.

If not using the campout, no auxiliary day is needed — both meetings above complete the adventure.

## Materials Summary

Cones, balls/frisbees, jump ropes, mats/towels, Bluetooth speaker, timer, printed meal-planning worksheets and recipe cards, MyPlate poster, AHMR verification slips, Food Allergies and Keep Your Food Safe handouts, kitchen equipment (pots, pans, utensils, boards, soap, sanitizer, plates/cups/napkins), ingredients per menu, cooler if needed, first-aid kit. For campout auxiliary: tents, camp kitchen, coolers, BALOO materials.

## Safety Notes

Use SAFE Checklist every meeting. Review Guide to Safe Scouting. Confirm BALOO for any campout. Chef's Knife prerequisite before knife use — otherwise adults handle all cutting/heat. Review Food Allergies moment and Keep Your Food Safe; check all AHMRs before food handling and keep EpiPen accessible with trained adult. Supervise stove/heat continuously; enforce hand-wash before and after cooking. No running in kitchen. Follow local food-handling regulations.
