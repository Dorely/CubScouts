## At the Trailhead — 20 Minutes

### Requirement 1 — Six Essentials Check

Each Scout opens their backpack and shows each of the Six Essentials: first-aid kit, filled water bottle, flashlight, trail food, sun protection, and whistle. Check closed-toe shoes and weather-appropriate layers. Share spare items quietly so every Scout can hike.

### Requirement 3 — Forecast Recheck

A Scout checks the forecast for the next two hours and compares it with the forecast recorded at the planning meeting. Adjust layers and sun protection.

### Requirement 6 — Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace

Hand out the reference sheet. Scouts read it silently, then name one way to follow each part on today's walk:

- **Stay on the trail** and boardwalks; walk carefully through the steep sections.
- **Leave what you find:** The Preserve prohibits collecting plants, rocks, and pinecones.
- **Trash your trash:** Pack out wrappers from trail food.
- **Respect wildlife:** Watch quietly from the trail.
- **Be kind to other visitors:** Step aside for other hikers and keep voices low near the ponds.

Start the GPS tracker, take a headcount, and confirm buddies.

## Requirement 6 — The Two-Mile Walk — About 80 Minutes

Follow the [leader route map](../../resources/Oak_Glen_Preserve_Hike_Maps.md#leader-route-map). The lead adult sets a pace for the slowest hiker; the sweep adult stays at the back. Take a headcount at every checkpoint.

| Checkpoint | Location | Stop |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Trailhead and information kiosk | Start tracker; headcount; buddies |
| 2 | Conifers of California | Look for redwoods and giant sequoias. Why do we leave cones and needles here? |
| 3 | Aquatic Ecosystems / Red-Wing Pond | Water break. Stay on the path and boardwalk near the water. |
| 4 | Deciduous Forest into the Stream Trail | Steep descent: slow down and keep buddies close. |
| 5 | Chaparral Loop junction | Stay on the Main Loop. Snack and water break; check feet for hot spots. |
| 6 | Sacred Springs and Hidden Hollow | Headcount. Skip Chaparral Vistas to Preservation Point. |
| 7 | Stream Trail | Steep climb: rest stops as needed; drink water. |
| 8 | Deciduous Forest to the south parking area | Restrooms if needed, then the Return Trail to the kiosk. |

At the kiosk, check the tracker. If it shows less than 2.0 miles, walk the short garden loops beside the kiosk (Sunflower Lane, Pioneer Loop, and Hummingbird Hill) until it does.

## Requirement 7 — Stop-Start-Continue — 15 Minutes

Gather the den in the shade near the kiosk. Use the [Stop-Start-Continue](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/stop-start-continue/) activity. Each Scout answers at least one question:

- **Stop:** What made the walk harder or didn't go well?
- **Start:** What should we do next time to make it better?
- **Continue:** What did we do right that we should keep doing?

## Closing — 5 Minutes

The denner leads a short closing. Thank the adults, check that every Scout leaves with their adult, and pack out all trash.
