## Opening and Bobcat Review — 10 Minutes

1. Have the denner lead the opening.
2. Recite the Scout Oath and Scout Law together.
3. Ask the den to name the three points of the Scout Oath: duty to God and country, duty to others, and duty to self.
4. Pair each new Scout with a returning Scout to practice the Cub Scout sign, salute, and handshake and explain when each is used.
5. Briefly introduce new Scouts and review the den Code of Conduct.

This is review for returning Scouts and catch-up practice for new Scouts. Mark a Bobcat requirement complete only when the new Scout has actually completed or demonstrated it.

## Requirement 2 — 30 Minutes of Stretching and Moving

Use the full 30-minute block:

- **Warm-up and stretching — 5 minutes:** Arm circles, side reaches, walking lunges, ankle circles, and gentle leg stretches.
- **Active team game — 20 minutes:** Choose one established game below. Keep everyone moving; use short rounds and quick resets.
- **Cool-down and stretching — 5 minutes:** Walk until breathing slows, then stretch calves, thighs, shoulders, and arms.

### Game Choices

#### Small-Sided Soccer — Soccer Ball

- Divide into two balanced teams and use natural landmarks for goals.
- Play without dedicated goalies, or rotate goalies every few minutes, so nobody stands in one place for long.
- No slide tackles or deliberate body contact.

#### Sharks and Minnows — No Gear

- Choose one or two Sharks. Everyone else lines up as Minnows at one end of the play area.
- On the signal, all Minnows run to the opposite safe line while the Sharks try to tag them.
- Tagged Minnows become Sharks for the next crossing and continue running as taggers.
- Use light hand tags only; no grabbing, pushing, or blocking.

#### Crows and Cranes — No Gear

- Divide the Scouts into Crows and Cranes. The teams face one another near the center of the play area, with a safe line behind each team.
- The leader calls either “Crows” or “Cranes.” The called team turns and chases the other team toward its safe line.
- Reset immediately after every chase and make the next call quickly so the group keeps moving.
- Use light hand tags only and leave enough space between Scouts for safe turning.

#### Capture the Flag — No Special Gear

- Divide the field into two territories and use jackets or water bottles already on hand as the two flags.
- Each team tries to take the other team's flag and carry it back across the center line without being tagged.
- Do not assign permanent flag guards. Rotate between attacking and defending so every Scout continues running.
- Use light hand tags only; no tackling, grabbing, or contact around the flags.

**Best choices for continuous running:** Sharks and Minnows or Crows and Cranes. Both need no gear and put the full group into each chase.
