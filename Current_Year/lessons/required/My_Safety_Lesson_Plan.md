# My Safety Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** With permission from your parent or legal guardian, watch the Protect Yourself Rules video for the Webelos rank.
- [ ] **2.** Identify items in your house that are hazardous and make sure they are stored properly. Identify on the package where it describes what to do if someone is accidentally exposed to them.
- [ ] **3.** Identify ways you and your family keep your home or your meeting space safe.
- [ ] **4.** Complete the Be Prepared for Natural Events worksheet. Complete a worksheet for at least two natural events most likely to happen near where you live.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Meeting Space and Natural Events

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 3, 4

Review the meeting space and complete worksheets for two locally likely natural events.

**Prep and materials:** Print the meeting-space checklist and natural-events worksheets. Bring pencils and handbooks; choose the two events before the meeting.

### At Home — Video and Household Review

**Approximate time:** 45–60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1, 2

Watch the Webelos video with a parent or guardian, then identify household hazards, storage, and label instructions.

**Prep and materials:** Share the parent notification, arrange permission, and send the official video link. Families need a viewing device and access to household labels; ask them to report completion and review the natural-event plans.

### Family Video Meeting Option

**Approximate time:** 45–60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1; finish 3–4 as needed

Invite parents or guardians to co-view the 22-minute video and finish worksheet discussions.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm parent/guardian attendance and permission; test the video, screen, and sound. Bring extra worksheets and pencils.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [My Safety Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/required/My_Safety_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/my-safety/)

### Requirement 1

- **Protect Yourself Video Webelos**: Watch the Protect Yourself Rules video with your parent or legal guardian.

### Requirement 2

- **Hazard Labels**: Walk around your home and identify items that are hazardous. Post the Poison Control information in a central place to have available should it be needed.
- **Safe Hazardous Material Storage**: Create a safe place in your meeting space to store hazardous items.

### Requirement 3

- **Safe Den Meeting Checklist**: Using the safety checklist, identify ways to keep your meeting space safe.
- **Safe Home Safety Checklist**: Using the safety checklist, identify ways to keeping their home safe.

### Requirement 4

- **Be Prepared for Natural Events**: Complete the "Be Prepared for Natural Events" worksheet.
