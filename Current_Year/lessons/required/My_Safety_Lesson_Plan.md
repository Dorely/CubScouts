> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**

Preview video yourself and review Youth Protection reporting guidelines. Print *Be Prepared for Natural Events* worksheets (2 per Scout, region-appropriate). Print Safe Home and Safe Den Meeting checklists for Req 3.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** No heavy booking. Prepare hazard-label examples (empty cleaned containers or photos), collect Poison Control magnet/handout, set up mock safe-storage station. No guest required — this requirement is home-based with den reinforcement.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Printed parent notification letters, Protect Yourself Rules video access (with screening capability), projector/speaker, printed natural-events worksheets, hazard-label samples or photo cards, Poison Control contact cards, blank home/meeting-space safety checklists, markers, poster paper, tape.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** None. Fully den-based + at-home family follow-up. Hazard identification (Req 2) is completed at home after den practice; no 3rd auxiliary day needed. If a Scout misses the video, they may complete it at home with parent/guardian and return verification.

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** With permission from your parent or legal guardian, watch the Protect Yourself Rules video for the Webelos rank.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Identify items in your house that are hazardous and make sure they are stored properly. Identify on the package where it describes what to do if someone is accidentally exposed to them.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Identify ways you and your family keep your home or your meeting space safe.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Complete the Be Prepared for Natural Events worksheet. Complete a worksheet for at least two natural events most likely to happen near where you live.

## Day 1 — Stay Safe: Protect Yourself + Be Prepared (60 min) — low-coordination requirements only

*Classroom. Private, respectful setting. No outdoor flex needed for video.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law, SAFE Checklist. Explain that safety is a Scout skill — we learn rules, we practice them, we help family stay prepared. Review how to ask for help if something feels unsafe and that adults are here to listen.

**Activity 1 — Protect Yourself Rules Video (25 min) — Req 1**
- Confirm parent permission slips returned. Any Scout without permission completes alternative quiet activity with second adult and watches at home with family — no pressure, no disclosure in front of peers.
- Watch *Protect Yourself Rules* video for Webelos rank together with two-deep leadership present. Pause only as designed in the video guide.
- Brief, age-appropriate discussion using video prompts: safe vs. unsafe touches, trusted adults, the buddy system, and what to do if rules are broken. Keep tone calm and factual. Do not solicit personal disclosures. Remind Scouts they can talk to parent/guardian or leader privately after.
- Leader note: If a child discloses suspected abuse, follow Scouting America Youth Protection reporting guidelines immediately after the meeting; do not investigate.

**Activity 2 — Natural Events Workshop (25 min) — Req 4**
- Introduce *Be Prepared for Natural Events* worksheet. Brainstorm two events most likely near where you live (e.g., severe thunderstorm/tornado, winter storm, flood, wildfire, earthquake — pick your region's top two).
- Small groups of 3–4 complete one worksheet per event: what happens, how to prepare a kit, where to shelter, family communication plan. Share one takeaway per group.
- Homework bridge: Scouts take worksheets home to review with family and finalize family plan; return signed/dated next week to verify completion. This completes Req 4 in den with family confirmation.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap Protect Yourself Rules in one sentence each, thank Scouts for maturity, preview Day 2 hazard and safety-check work. Hand out Poison Control cards to post at home with family. Collect permission verifications for records.

## Day 2 — Home Hazards + Safe Spaces (60 min) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Classroom with station tables. Safe-storage demo — no real chemicals opened.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Roll call, revisit SAFE Checklist four points. Ask: who posted Poison Control information at home? Quick show of hands — no personal detail needed.

**Activity 1 — Hazard Labels & Safe Storage (25 min) — Req 2**
- *Hazard Labels* station: Show cleaned empty containers or high-quality photo cards (cleaner, pesticide, medication, paint). For each, ask where label describes exposure response (first-aid panel, 800-number). Scouts point out signal words: Caution/Warning/Danger.
- *Safe Hazardous Material Storage* mock-up: shelves/boxes labeled high/locked vs. low/open. Scouts sort items to correct safe place (locked cabinet, original container, out of reach of children/pets). Emphasize never transferring chemicals to food containers.
- At-home task assignment: Walk home with parent/guardian, confirm hazardous items are stored properly and post Poison Control centrally. Scouts report back verbally next week or bring signed checklist — this completes Req 2 at home after den practice.

**Activity 2 — Keep Home and Meeting Space Safe (25 min) — Req 3**
- Split den into two teams:
  - Team A: *Safe Home Safety Checklist* — doors/locks, smoke/CO detectors, fire extinguisher, cords/rugs, water temperature, emergency numbers.
  - Team B: *Safe Den Meeting Checklist* — meeting-space walk-through (exits, first-aid kit location, trip hazards, lighting, bathroom buddy system).
- Teams mark checklist, then present top 3 findings and one fix they will do with family or den. Post den checklist in room.
- Each Scout notes two ways their family already keeps home safe and one improvement to make with family.

**Closing (5 min)**
Review: Protect Yourself Rules remembered, hazard storage rule (lock it, label it, leave it), safe-space check. Collect/verify Req 2 and Req 3 take-home confirmations or schedule return. Remind families to finish any remaining home review together.

## Auxiliary Day — None needed

No auxiliary hike, campout, or pack service day required. All requirements are den + at-home. If a Scout misses Day 1 or Day 2, requirements may be completed at home with parent/guardian and verified by leader at next den meeting — no separate event to schedule.

## Materials Summary

Parent notification letters, permission slips, Protect Yourself Rules video access, projector/speaker, printed *Be Prepared for Natural Events* worksheets (2 per Scout), Safe Home and Safe Den Meeting checklists, hazard photo cards or cleaned empty containers, Poison Control handouts/magnets, markers, poster paper, tape, extension cord, attendance sheet.

## Safety Notes

Use SAFE Checklist every meeting; maintain two-deep leadership, especially during sensitive video. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Youth Protection content before starting; preview video. Notify parents/guardians before meeting. Keep discussion non-graphic and do not press for personal disclosures. If abuse is suspected or disclosed, follow Scouting America Youth Protection reporting guidelines promptly. Keep hazard examples empty/cleaned — never open or sniff chemicals; keep mock storage away from food. Check local regulations for emergency preparedness guidance.
