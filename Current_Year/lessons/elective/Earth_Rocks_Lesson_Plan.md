# Earth Rocks Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Examine the three types of rocks, sedimentary, igneous, and metamorphic.
- [ ] **2.** Find a rock, safely break it apart and examine it.
- [ ] **3.** Make a mineral test kit and test minerals according to the Mohs scale of mineral hardness. Using the rock cycle chart or one like it, discuss how hardness determines which materials can be used in homes, in landscapes, or for recreation.
- [ ] **4.** Grow a crystal.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Rocks and Mineral Lab

**Approximate time:** 75–90 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–3

Examine the three rock types, break and inspect a rock, and make and use mineral test kits.

**Prep and materials:** Try the mineral kit beforehand. Gather rock samples, at least two minerals, pennies, glass, unglazed tile, files, eyedroppers, vinegar, safety glasses, Mohs scale, and a rock-cycle chart. For geodes, add geodes, socks, a shared geology hammer, and magnifiers; practice on an extra geode.

### Day 2 — Grow Crystals

**Approximate time:** 30–45 minutes to start; 10–15 minutes to observe later · **Requirements:** 4

Start Crystal Shapes or Sweet Crystal and return to examine the growth.

**Prep and materials:** Grow an example beforehand and arrange adult solution preparation and undisturbed storage. Crystal Shapes uses pipe cleaners, cups, fishing line, skewers, borax, water, gloves, and table covering; allow overnight or longer. Sweet Crystal uses sugar, water, jars, skewers, clothespins, a pan/heat source, measuring cup, paper towels, and covering; allow about a week. Use the reference’s linked recipes for quantities.

### Family Follow-Through

**Approximate time:** 10–15 minutes after growth · **Requirements:** 4

Observe and collect crystals at home or bring them back to the den.

**Prep and materials:** Agree where the crystals will grow and when families will collect them.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Earth Rocks Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Earth_Rocks_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/earth-rocks/)

### Requirement 1

- **Crayon Rock Formation**: Using crayons to recreate the formation process of igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rocks.
- **Rock Classification**: Cub Scouts learn the three types of rocks and how to identify them.
- **Rock Expert**: Invite a Rockhound or geologist to a den meeting.
- **Rock Hunt**: Cub Scouts hunt for rocks to identify and categorize.

### Requirement 2

- **Break a Geode**: Cub Scouts break apart a geode and examine.
- **Let's Go Rock Hunting**: Cub Scouts break apart rocks.

### Requirement 3

- **Mineral Lab**: Cub Scout study and share observation of the mineral test.

### Requirement 4

- **Crystal Shapes**: Cub Scouts create their own crystal.
- **Sweet Crystal**: Cub Scouts create their own crystal that they can eat.
