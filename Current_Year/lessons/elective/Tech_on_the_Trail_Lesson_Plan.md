# Tech on the Trail Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min + 1 auxiliary trek REQUIRED (60–90 min) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
>
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — 2-mile trek is a pack/den auxiliary; devices, GPS, and mapping must be secured before Day 2.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Tech_on_the_Trail_Adventure.md` + *Digital Safety and Online Scouting Activities*. Install a GPS/geocaching app (e.g., Geocaching® or AllTrails) on 3–4 shared devices; create a free geocaching account if using caches. Gather 3–4 smartphones/tablets (den-provided or parent-provided with permission), portable chargers, and printed screenshots of a GPS lock screen. Prepare "tech keeps you safe" cards (weather app, flashlight, compass, SOS, map offline). Confirm facility allows devices and has Wi-Fi or cell coverage for demo.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced — book 1–2 weeks out):** Choose an online mapping tool (Google Maps, AllTrails, CalTopo) and pre-scout a **2-mile trek** — quiet trail, park loop, or greenway with clear start/finish, restrooms, water, and cell coverage. Verify trail permission, parking, and bailout points. Print large maps + 3 group map sets, cue sheets, and distance scale. Arrange adult support (1 per 3–4 Scouts), first-aid kit, and weather go/no-go plan. Stage tablets/phones with offline map downloaded, and portable charger. Send parent notice + permission slip for auxiliary trek.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Shared smartphones/tablets (3–4) + chargers, GPS/geocaching app, printed GPS screenshots, tech-safety cards, local map prints (large + 3 small sets), markers, highlighters, cue-sheet templates, mileage scale/string, weather-app printouts, attendance sheet. Auxiliary adds: per-group phone/tablet with offline map, water, Six Essentials, first-aid kit, trail permission.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **REQUIRED auxiliary day — 3rd day, not optional (60–90 min family/den/pack 2-mile trek).** Day 1 completes Req 1–2 in classroom (tech safety + GPS). Day 2 in den completes Req 3 (plan 2-mile trek with adult on mapping tool). Req 4 (take the trek) is the REQUIRED auxiliary — not completable indoors. If combined with Webelos Walkabout, one 2-mile walk can cover both treks but log separately.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Tech_on_the_Trail_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Discuss how technology can help keep you safe in the outdoors.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Explore Global Positioning Satellite and how to use it.
- [ ] **Req 3:** With an adult, choose an online mapping program tool and plan a 2-mile trek.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Take your 2-mile trek.

## Day 1 — Tech Safety + GPS (60 min) — Req 1–2, classroom

*Classroom with devices on airplane mode until needed. Groups of 3.*

**Opening (5 min)** — Flag, Oath & Law (trustworthy with tech). Preview Tech on the Trail as the outdoor-technology elective: Day 1 = how tech keeps us safe + GPS; Day 2 = plan a 2-mile trek on a mapping program; Auxiliary = REQUIRED 2-mile trek with tech.

**Activity 1 — How Tech Keeps You Safe Outdoors (20 min) — Req 1**
- *Card Sort (10 min):* Teams of 3 sort tech cards into "helps safety" vs. "doesn't help" — weather app with alerts, offline map/GPS, headlamp, whistle/phone SOS, extra battery, compass app vs. games/social scrolling on trail. Discuss *Check the Weather App* and offline-map pre-download before losing signal.
- *Weather App Scavenger Hunt (10 min):* With one shared device per group (adult-supervised), find tomorrow's forecast, sunrise/sunset, and one alert type for your area. Each group shares one way they would change plans based on what they found (e.g., bring rain layer, start earlier). Completes Req 1 when each Scout can name 2 ways tech helps safety.

**Activity 2 — Explore GPS (30 min) — Req 2**
- *How GPS Works (10 min):* Leader demo — 3+ satellites triangulate position; show phone's GPS dot vs. map. Emphasize GPS needs sky view, not cell, but maps may need download. Review *Digital Safety* (no sharing live location publicly, ask permission for photos).
- *Webelos Geocache (20 min):* Hide 4–5 small containers or printed cache cards around building/outdoor flex. Groups of 3 use GPS app to navigate to each (follow arrow/distance), log find on worksheet, and note accuracy. If no outdoor caches available, use printed coordinates + map pin. Each Scout takes a turn holding the device and reading bearing/distance. Completes Req 2 when each Scout has used GPS to find a location.

**Closing (5 min)** — Recap: tech is a tool, not a replacement for Six Essentials and buddy system. Preview Day 2 — bring curiosity for mapping; remind families of auxiliary trek date and to charge devices.

## Day 2 — Plan a 2-Mile Trek on a Map Program (60 min) — Req 3 prep, advanced planning showcase

*Classroom with large map + devices. No hiking today.*

**Opening (5 min)** — Recap GPS. Show auxiliary trek area photo and explain route constraints (≤2 miles, loop or out-and-back, safe crossings, water/restroom).

**Activity 1 — Choose a Mapping Tool & Plan 2 Miles (40 min) — Req 3**
- *Tool Choice (10 min):* Demo 2 options side-by-side (e.g., Google Maps walking + AllTrails trail map). Discuss pros: street routing vs. trail distance/elevation, satellite view, offline download, printing. Den votes or leader picks one tool with adult guidance.
- *Plan in Groups of 3 (25 min):* Each group gets a printed base map + device with mapping tool. Task: mark start, waypoint, and finish totaling **2.0 miles** (use tool's distance measure or string scale), add elevation note, and pick 2 safety bailout points. Label rest stop. Groups present; den blends into one official auxiliary route. Leader prints final cue sheet + GPX and shares with families. Completes Req 3 when den has an adult-approved 2-mile trek planned on the chosen online tool.
- *Tech Packing Check (5 min):* List what tech to bring on the trek (charged phone, offline map, weather app check morning-of, portable battery, whistle) vs. what not to rely on (single phone, no backup, no water).

**Activity 2 — Trek Safety Brief (10 min)**
- Review for auxiliary: buddy pairs, Six Essentials, weather go/no-go, stay on marked trail, Leave No Trace, what to do if separated (stay put, blow whistle, phone adult). Assign buddy pairs and adult supports for auxiliary day. Hand out permission slips, water/Six Essentials checklist, and offline-map install instructions for families.

**Closing (5 min)** — Confirm auxiliary trek date/time/meet point, carpool, and that offline map must be downloaded before leaving home. Charge devices night before.

## Auxiliary Day — Take the 2-Mile Trek (REQUIRED)

- **When:** Weekend morning within 2 weeks of Day 2, 60–90 min. Backup rain date.
- **Where:** Pre-mapped 2-mile route — trail, park loop, or greenway. Confirm permission, parking, restrooms, water, and that route is ≥2 miles by GPS trace, not estimate.
- **Who:** Den + families + adult supports (buddy system — no Scout alone). One adult carries first-aid kit, water, phone, portable battery.
- **Program (90 min):** 0:00 gather + weather check + buddy assignment + Six Essentials check; 0:10 start trek — groups navigate with GPS/mapping app, check off waypoints, practice Leave No Trace; mid-point rest + water + GPS accuracy check; finish with group photo and reflection: did tech match reality? Log completion per Scout. Completes Req 4 when 2 miles is walked as a den/family/pack group using the planned route.
- **Logistics:** Signed permission slips, health forms on hand, weather check morning-of (cancel if lightning/heat advisory), offline map verified before departure, vehicle at finish if point-to-point.

## Materials Summary

Smartphones/tablets (3–4) + chargers + offline maps, GPS/geocaching app + account, printed maps + markers + cue sheets, mileage scale, weather-app printouts, tech-safety cards, Six Essentials, first-aid kit, attendance/permission sheets.

## Safety Notes

SAFE Checklist; *Digital Safety and Online Scouting Activities* (no live-location posting, photo permission); buddy system; Six Essentials + weather monitoring; stay on trail; offline map backup — do not rely solely on cell signal; adequate adult supervision and first-aid plan.
