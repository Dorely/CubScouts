# Tech on the Trail Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Discuss how technology can help keep you safe in the outdoors.
- [ ] **2.** Explore Global Positioning Satellite and how to use it.
- [ ] **3.** With an adult, choose an online mapping program tool and plan a 2-mile trek.
- [ ] **4.** Take your 2-mile trek.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Technology and Trek Planning

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–3

Explore weather apps and GPS, then plan a two-mile trek with an adult using an online mapping tool.

**Prep and materials:** Test devices, apps, internet access, and GPS. Prepare geocache practice, arrange device turns and adult help, and bring charging equipment and route information.

### Day 2 — Two-Mile Trek

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 4

Take the planned trek with the route available on the devices.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm access, meeting point, family arrangements, charged devices, and route-appropriate equipment.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Tech on the Trail Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Tech_on_the_Trail_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/tech-on-the-trail/)

### Requirement 1

- **Check the App**: Cub Scouts explore weather apps to prepare for outdoor activities.
- **Weather App Scavenger Hunt**: Cub Scouts explore weather apps and discover what the weather is like in various locations.

### Requirement 2

- **Webelos Geocache**: Cub Scouts explore how to use GPS to find geocaches.

### Requirement 3

- **Where and How Long?**: Cub Scouts use technology to find a place to take a 2-mile trek.

### Requirement 4

- **2-Mile Tech Trek**: Cub Scout take a 2-mile trek they planned.
