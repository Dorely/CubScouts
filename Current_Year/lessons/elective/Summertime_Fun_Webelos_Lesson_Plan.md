# Summertime Fun Webelos Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Anytime during May through August participate in a total of three Cub Scout activities.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Den Planning or Record Check

**Approximate time:** 10–15 minutes within a meeting · **Requirements:** 1 planning and follow-up

Choose three May–August Cub Scout activities or collect records of activities already attended. Webelos participation is in the summer after third grade.

**Prep and materials:** Gather council, den, and pack event information and a place to record the three activities and dates.

### Three Summer Activities

**Approximate time:** Three event dates; allow 1–3 hours per local activity or the published camp schedule · **Requirements:** 1

Participate in the chosen council, den, or pack activities during May–August.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm registration and attendance arrangements, send families the event details, and gather each organizer’s requested supplies.

- **Council activity**: Participate in an activity with your council.
- **Den activity**: Participate in an activity with your den.
