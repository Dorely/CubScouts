# Summertime Fun Webelos Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 30 min + **seasonal auxiliary REQUIRED (3 activities May–August)** | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
>
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — seasonal tracking, not den-meeting content.**
> - **Before Meeting 1 (at den meeting before summer):** Print summer tracking sheets (one per Scout) with 3 activity slots + dates. Build a draft summer calendar of pack/council/district options (day camp, resident camp, pack picnic, den hike, service project). Confirm which events count as "Cub Scout activities" (any council, pack, or den activity qualifies). Notify families: this badge is earned **May–August after 3rd grade**; winter work does not count. Hand out tracking + permission slips for council camps if needed.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced — fall, after summer):** Collect completed tracking sheets, gather photos/sign-in evidence for 3 activities, prepare reflection prompts. No booking — summer already happened. If a Scout missed an activity, plan a make-up pack activity early fall before closing.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Tracking sheets, summer calendar handouts, stickers/markers, poster for summer collage, photo printouts (optional), attendance sheet.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **REQUIRED — this badge cannot be completed in 2 den meetings alone.** Scouts must participate in **3 separate Cub Scout activities May–August** (e.g., council day/resident camp + pack picnic + den hike). These are 3 auxiliary days outside regular Wednesdays. Den meetings are for planning (spring) and reflection (fall); summer *is* the work.

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Anytime during May through August participate in a total of three Cub Scout activities.

> Note: Requirement is identical for Webelos and Arrow of Light. To earn as Webelos, the summer is the one after 3rd grade. Scouts may repeat the adventure the next summer for AOL.

---

## Day 1 — Summer Planning Party (30–40 min, spring before summer) — planning only

*Classroom. Not earning — just planning how to earn.*

**Opening (5 min)** — Flag, Oath & Law (helpful = show up in summer). Explain: this badge is earned *over summer*, not tonight. Tonight we plan.

**Activity 1 — What Counts? (15 min)**
- Mini-lesson: *Council activity* (day camp, resident camp), *Pack activity* (picnic, hike, service), *Den activity* (hike, game day) — any 3 count. Show draft summer calendar with council camp dates, pack events (Adventure Weekend, etc.), and den options. Circle 3–5 options that work for 7–9 families. Hand out tracking sheets.

**Activity 2 — Pick Your Three (10 min)**
- In pairs, each Scout circles 3 activities they intend to do and writes backup. Leader records intent on master summer roster. Discuss buddy system and what to bring (Six Essentials for hikes, swim gear for camp). Send home calendar + tracking sheet + council camp registration links.

**Closing (5 min)** — Challenge: bring back one photo or story from each summer activity. Preview Day 2 will be in fall — reflection + collage.

---

## Day 2 — Summer Show & Tell (60 min, fall after summer) — reflection & verification

*Classroom with poster.*

**Opening (5 min)** — Welcome back, recap summer plan vs. reality.

**Activity 1 — My Three Activities (30 min) — Req 1 verification**
- Each Scout presents 3 activities (photo, patch, or story): where, when, with whom (den/pack/council), one thing learned or favorite part. Leader checks tracking sheet, initials each activity. For 7–9 scouts, run as 3 rounds of 3 scouts (3 min each + Q&A). Group similar activities (e.g., all who did Adventure Weekend).

**Activity 2 — Summer Collage (20 min)**
- On large poster, each Scout adds a drawing/sticker for each activity. Label: date, activity type, den/pack/council. Display in meeting room or pack hallway. Alternative: silent reading or board game if collage supplies limited — keep hands busy while waiting to present.

**Closing (5 min)** — Celebrate completion; remind families that these same 3 activities count for AOL Summertime Fun next summer if they do it again. Collect tracking sheets for records. Note any Scout still needing a 3rd activity — schedule make-up pack activity within 2 weeks (park game day counts).

---

## Auxiliary Days — The Actual Earning (REQUIRED)

- **When:** 3 separate days **May through August**, 60–120 min each.
- **What counts:** Council day camp or resident camp (best single option — often covers one), pack picnics, pack hikes, den hikes, pack campouts (Adventure Weekend, Eerie Emerson), service projects, pack derby/races if council-run. Den decides; leader pre-approves.
- **Logistics:** Council camp = registration + health forms + fees; den/pack outings = permission, adult ratio, Six Essentials, weather plan. Leader tracks attendance at each and signs tracking sheets.
- **Failure mode:** If a Scout misses summer entirely, they cannot earn this elective in the school year — plan a June–July pack activity specifically to include them.

## Materials Summary

Tracking sheets, summer calendar handouts, poster + markers + stickers, photo printouts, council camp flyers, attendance record.

## Safety Notes

SAFE Checklist; council camps handle aquatics/climbing per their program — verify BALOO/Safe Swim as applicable for den/pack outings; buddy system and weather monitoring for hikes; keep AHMR on file.
