# Aquanaut Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min + auxiliary pool half-day (90 min) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — pool reservation, Safe Swim Defense qualified adult, and buddy-tag system must be secured before Day 1.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review `Aquanaut Adventure` reference and Scouting America Safe Swim Defense (8 points). Complete Safe Swim Defense training at my.scouting.org yourself (26 min). Print Safe Swim Defense handout, order-of-rescue cards, hypothermia prevention/treatment handout, and buddy-tag blanks. Gather reach poles (or broom handles), throw ropes / ring buoys, and hypothermia demo items (towel, blanket, thermometer). Send parent notice explaining two-meeting structure, swimwear needs, health forms, and auxiliary pool day. Confirm no in-water activity Day 1 — classroom only.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** **Book these before Day 1 if possible.** Reserve pool (community, YMCA, or school) with lifeguard confirmation; confirm date/time, fees, and locker room rules. Recruit and confirm Safe Swim Defense-qualified adult age 21+ as supervisor and additional rescuers to maintain **1 rescuer per 10 Cub Scouts**; confirm at least one adult holds current Safe Swim Defense. Prepare buddy tags (youth + adults), buddy board, and reach/throw gear (poles, ropes, throwable cushions). Verify Annual Health and Medical Record for every swimmer and non-swimmer, check ability groups, and brief lifeguard on Scouting America swimmer test and 30-min free-swim with **≥3 buddy checks per half hour**. Confirm pool rules, sunscreen, and emergency action plan. Arrange transportation and additional adult supervision.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Printed Safe Swim Defense posters, order-of-rescue sequence cards, hypothermia info sheets, buddy tags + board + markers, reach poles (6–10 ft), throw ropes/buoys (2–3), towels, blankets (hypothermia wrap demo), water bottles, sunscreen samples, whistles, attendance sheet, swimmer-test score sheets, stopwatch, first-aid kit, trash bags. Pool items: swimsuits/goggles (Scouts bring), kick boards optional.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **REQUIRED if no pool at den meeting site.** This plan assumes Day 2 at a reserved pool; if your den site has no pool, move all in-water work (Reqs 4–6) to a **3rd auxiliary pool day (90 min)** outside regular den time. Even with a pool at Day 2, an auxiliary make-up pool slot is recommended for Scouts who miss Day 2 — the 30-min free swim + buddy checks cannot be done in a classroom.

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** State the safety precautions you need to take before doing any swimming activity.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Explain the meaning of "order of rescue" and demonstrate the reach and throw rescue techniques from land.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Learn how to prevent and treat hypothermia.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Attempt to tread water.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Attempt Scouting America swimmer test.
- [ ] **Req 6:** Have 30 minutes, or more, of free swim time where you practice the Buddy System and stay within your ability group. The qualified adult supervision should conduct at least three buddy checks per half hour swimming.

> Alternative completion: This elective may also be earned by passing the Scouting America swimmer test OR taking swimming lessons — if a Scout has already done so, record alternative and still run safety classroom portions for the den.

## Day 1 — Classroom Water Safety: Precautions, Rescue Order, Hypothermia (60 min) — Reqs 1, 2, 3 — low-coordination only

*Classroom + hallway/floor space. No water entry. Groups of 3 for all activities.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *trustworthy* and *loyal* — buddy system), preview Aquanaut as the swimming elective. Explain Day 1 = classroom safety skills everyone needs before water, Day 2 = pool skills and free swim. Buddy assignment preview — no Scout swims alone.

**Activity 1 — Swimming Safety Precautions (20 min) — Req 1**
- *Safe Swim Defense Overview (10 min):* Walk the 8 points in Scout language — qualified supervision, physical fitness, safe area, response personnel, lookout, buddy system, discipline, and check of ability groups. Use poster + quick quiz in teams of 3. Scouts state aloud one precaution before any swimming activity.
- *Spot the Safe Spot (10 min):* Table stations with photos/scenarios (beach, pool, pond, river, backyard). Teams sort into Safe / Not Safe and name missing precautions (supervision, weather, buddy, permission, AHMR). Each Scout states at least two precautions to complete Req 1. Record answers on den poster.

**Activity 2 — Order of Rescue: Reach & Throw from Land (20 min) — Req 2**
- *Explain the Order (7 min):* Teach Reach → Throw → Row → Go (with Go requiring trained rescue) — emphasize we only do Reach and Throw from land as Cub Scouts. Why: keep rescuer safe, stay on land, call for adult help. Demonstrate voice + 911 step.
- *Reach Station (6 min):* In socks on gym floor, pairs practice reaching with pole/broom handle to a "victim" line on tape. Coach: lie flat, extend pole, pull, never enter water. Rotate roles.
- *Throw Station (7 min):* With coiled rope / throwable cushion, practice underhand throw beyond taped "victim" circle and pull in. Groups of 3 rotate; each Scout completes 2–3 successful reach and throw reps. Instructor verifies. Completes Req 2 demonstration from land.

**Activity 3 — Prevent & Treat Hypothermia (10 min) — Req 3**
- *Surviving the Chill (6 min):* What hypothermia is (body loses heat faster than it makes it — cold water, wind, wet clothes), signs (shivering, numbness, confusion, slurred speech), prevention (dry clothes, layers, limit time, warm up, stay hydrated/fed), and treatment (get out of water, dry off, warm blankets, warm drinks, seek adult medical help — do not rub limbs aggressively or use hot water).
- *Wrap Demo (4 min):* Teams practice hypothermia wrap with towel + blanket on a volunteer; leader checks steps. Each Scout can name two preventions and two treatments — completes Req 3.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap 8-point safety, rescue order (only Reach/Throw from land), and hypothermia steps. Homework: review swim safety with parent/guardian, confirm swimsuit/goggles/towel for pool day, return AHMR if not yet turned in. Remind families of pool location, time, and buddy-tag procedure for Day 2.

## Day 2 — At the Pool: Treading, Swimmer Test & Buddy Free Swim (60 min) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Reserved pool. Safe Swim Defense-qualified adult age 21+ present; lifeguard + rescuers at 1:10; buddy tags on board; reach/throw gear on deck. All in-water work happens here.*

**Opening on Deck (5 min)**
Attendance by buddy tags, ability group review (non-swimmer / beginner / swimmer), sunscreen application, pool rules, emergency signals, and buddy-check procedure (whistle + count, raise hand with buddy). Re-state Safe Swim Defense 8 points applied to this pool.

**Activity 1 — Treading Water Attempts (15 min) — Req 4**
- Small groups of 3 with one rescuer watching each group. In shallow-to-chest water, each Scout attempts to tread water (vertical, head above surface, using arms/legs). No distance requirement — attempt and coaching counts. Support with kick board if needed, then retry without. Leader records attempts. Non-swimmers stay in shallow with adult contact.

**Activity 2 — Attempt the Scouting America Swimmer Test (15 min) — Req 5**
- One at a time or pairs with rescuer + lifeguard eyes on: Jump into water over head, level off and swim 75 yards strong stroke + 25 yards elementary backstroke (100 yards total), then float/rest, as per Scouting America swimmer test. Emphasize "attempt" — Scouts do their best within ability; all attempts recorded, classification updated after. Scouts who pass are classified as Swimmers; others retain Beginner/Non-swimmer for the day and are celebrated for attempting.

**Activity 3 — 30-Minute Buddy Free Swim with Buddy Checks (20 min) — Req 6**
- Free swim within ability areas, buddy pairs stay together arm's reach, same ability group. Qualified adult conducts **≥3 buddy checks per 30 minutes** — whistle, Scouts pair up and raise joined hands; count off tags on board. Practice staying in assigned area, checking with buddy before entering/leaving water. Lifeguard and rescuers provide continuous supervision. End with orderly exit, towel, and tag collection.
- *If time short:* Run 30 min exactly; if pool slot allows 35–40 min, use extra minutes for additional supervised practice — still requires 3 checks minimum.

**Closing on Deck (5 min)**
Buddy-tag check-out, quick reflection: What did you do to stay safe today? What rescue would you use from land? How did buddy checks feel? Thank pool staff and qualified supervisor. Hand out completion stickers for Reqs 4–6 attempts.

## Auxiliary Day (if needed)

**Auxiliary Pool Half-Day (90 min) — REQUIRED if no pool at den site; otherwise make-up/overflow.**

If your regular den meets in a classroom or church without pool access (the usual case), treat Day 2 above as the auxiliary day: reserve a separate weekend or evening pool session and run the full Day 2 program there. If you *do* have pool access at the den site, no separate auxiliary is strictly needed — but schedule a make-up pool slot within two weeks for any Scout who missed Day 2, because Req 6's 30-min buddy swim and Req 4/5 cannot be made up on land.

Logistics for auxiliary: same staffing as Day 2 — Safe Swim Defense-qualified adult 21+, 1:10 rescuers, buddy tags/board, reach/throw gear, AHMRs, sunscreen, first-aid kit, and pool permission forms. Allow 90 min total (15 check-in/rules + 15 tread + 15 swimmer test + 30 free swim with 3 checks + 15 changing/buffer). Combine with pack family swim only if buddy checks and ability groups can still be enforced — pack families must stay out of the counted den swim area during the 30-min block.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and at the pool. All swimming must follow **Safe Swim Defense** — qualified adult 21+ who knows and accepts responsibility, 1 rescuer per 10 Scouts, buddy system with tags, ability groups, and lookout. Complete Safe Swim Defense training before the adventure; brief Scouts on Safe Swim Defense points before entering water. Provide continuous, qualified adult supervision and discipline both on deck and in water. Check AHMRs and note non-swimmers. Conduct ≥3 buddy checks per half hour during Req 6. Teach only Reach and Throw rescues from land — no wading or swimming rescues by Cub Scouts. When outdoors, require sunscreen. In cold water or wind, teach hypothermia prevention (dry, warm, limited exposure) and treatment (dry, insulate, warm, seek help). Know local pool rules and emergency action plan; follow Guide to Safe Scouting and local regulations that supersede Scouting America practices.
