# Aquanaut Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** State the safety precautions you need to take before doing any swimming activity.
- [ ] **2.** Explain the meaning of "order of rescue" and demonstrate the reach and throw rescue techniques from land.
- [ ] **3.** Learn how to prevent and treat hypothermia.
- [ ] **4.** Attempt to tread water.
- [ ] **5.** Attempt Scouting America swimmer test.
- [ ] **6.** Have 30 minutes, or more, of free swim time where you practice the Buddy System and stay within your ability group. The qualified adult supervision should conduct at least three buddy checks per half hour swimming.

**Alternate completion:** Pass the Scouting America swimmer test or take swimming lessons.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Dry-Land Skills

**Approximate time:** 45–60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–3

Review swimming precautions, practice reach and throw rescue, and learn about hypothermia.

**Prep and materials:** Arrange demonstration equipment and a leader familiar with the activities. Bring the swimming rules and rescue materials.

### Day 2 — Pool Session

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 4–6

Attempt treading water and the swimmer test, then spend at least 30 minutes in free swim.

**Prep and materials:** Book the pool, qualified supervision, and swimmer-test examiner. Arrange buddy tags and checks; tell families to bring swimwear and towels.

### Family Swimming Option

**Approximate time:** A swimmer-test appointment or the provider’s lesson schedule · **Requirements:** Alternate completion

Pass the Scouting America swimmer test or take swimming lessons.

**Prep and materials:** Arrange the test or lessons and ask families to report completion.
