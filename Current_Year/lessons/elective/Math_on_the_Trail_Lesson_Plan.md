**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min (+ auxiliary if needed) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Determine your walking pace by walking 1/4 mile. Make a projection on how long it would take you to walk 2 miles.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Walk 2 miles and record the time it took you to complete the two miles.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Make a projection on how long it would take you to hike a 20-mile trail over two days. List all the factors to consider for your projection.

## Day 1 — Quarter-Mile Pace & 2-Mile Projection (60 min) — low-coordination Reqs only

*Classroom + outdoor track/trail flex. No long hike. Groups of 3 for timing.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *thrifty* and *clean* — use time and energy wisely, leave no trace). Preview Math on the Trail as the pace-and-projection elective. Explain Day 1 = timed quarter-mile → math to project 2 miles; Day 2 = timed 2-mile → math to project 20 miles over two days. Show measured quarter-mile map, timers, and worksheets. Buddy and water check.

**Activity 1 — Time Your Quarter Mile (25 min) — Req 1 (pace portion)**
- *Route Brief (5 min):* Walk the measured quarter-mile boundaries (cones/tape every ~1/8 mi), point out start/finish, turnarounds, and safety rules — buddy pairs, stay on trail/sidewalk, no running, hydration, LNT.
- *Timed Walks (15 min):* Groups of 3 walk the quarter-mile at normal Scout pace while one Scout per group times with stopwatch and another records. Each Scout walks and is timed individually (leader/adult staggers starts by 30 sec). Adults verify distance and timing. If indoor gym, use measured laps (e.g., 8 laps = 1/4 mi depending on gym) with same timing method.
- *Record (5 min):* At tables, Scouts log quarter-mile time (mm:ss) on pace worksheet. Leader checks each log for completeness. Completes Req 1 pace portion — every Scout has a timed quarter-mile.

**Activity 2 — Project Your 2-Mile Time (25 min) — Req 1 (projection portion)**
- *Math Huddle (10 min):* Teach projection — 2 miles is 8× a quarter-mile, so multiply quarter-mile time by 8. Demo with one example on poster: 5:30 × 8 = 44:00. Show conversion of seconds to minutes. In groups of 3, Scouts calculate own 2-mile projection with calculators, checking each other's math. Leader spot-checks every sheet.
- *Reasonableness Check (10 min):* Discuss pace variance — hills, stops, surface, pack weight, weather. Each Scout writes predicted 2-mile time and one factor that could make actual time faster or slower. Groups share one insight with den.
- *Preview & Factors Seed (5 min):* Introduce Req 3 seed — what else matters for a 20-mile/2-day hike? Start list: terrain, elevation gain, rest/eat/sleep, weather, daylight, pack weight, fitness, trail surface, water sources. Save list for Day 2.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap pace = time per fixed distance, projection = pace × distance. Confirm Day 2 logistics — measured 2-mile route location, arrival time, water/sunscreen, buddy system, weather go/no-go. Homework: bring water and comfortable walking shoes. Collect pace worksheets for Day 2 comparison.

## Day 2 — Walk 2 Miles & Project 20 Miles (60 min) — Advanced / Coordinated Reqs HERE

*Outdoors on measured 2-mile route (classroom backup if weather). Adults for active supervision. Groups of 3.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Buddy roll call, route map review with quarter-mile marks, rest stop and turnaround points, LNT reminders, and weather/SAFE check. Distribute timers, clipboards, and 20-mile projection worksheets. Restate goal: walk 2 miles, record actual time, then project 20 miles over two days with factors.

**Activity 1 — Timed Two-Mile Walk (35 min) — Req 2**
- *Brief & Pace Plan (5 min):* Each Scout states quarter-mile-based projection; remind steady pace, buddy stays together, hydration at half-mile marks, no racing. Adults positioned at front, middle, and sweep.
- *The Walk (25 min):* Den walks measured 2-mile route together — if loop, one continuous walk; if out-and-back, turn at 1-mile mark. One adult per group of 3 keeps official group time plus each Scout's personal split if possible (stagger still together). Leader records start/finish clock time and notes stops for accuracy. Enforce LNT — stay on trail, pack out trash. Expected pace ~15–20 min/mi for Webelos → 30–40 min for 2 miles; adjust route to fit 25-min movement plus brief water stops within the hour.
- *Record & Compare (5 min):* At finish or back in classroom, Scouts log actual 2-mile time vs. Day 1 projection, calculate difference, and note why variance occurred (crowding, slope, stops, surface). Leader initials each log. Completes Req 2 — every Scout walked 2 miles and recorded time.

**Activity 2 — Project 20 Miles Over Two Days (15 min) — Req 3**
- *Math (7 min):* Use actual 2-mile time to project 20 miles — either 10 × 2-mile time or recalculate per-mile pace × 20. Groups of 3 compute, then split over two days (e.g., 10 mi + 10 mi) with suggested hiking window (6–7 hours per day including breaks) to show daily pace and rest.
- *Factors List (8 min):* Den builds factors poster — terrain/elevation, pack weight, weather (heat/rain), daylight, rest/sleep, meals, water resupply, trail condition, group fitness/age, breaks/first aid, elevation at camp, early start vs. late . Each Scout copies final factors to worksheet and circles top 3 that would change their projection most. Leader verifies each worksheet lists multiple factors. Completes Req 3 — projection plus factors list.
- *Walkabout Pairing Note (1 min inside closing):* If Walkabout auxiliary is scheduled, note that this 2-mile log also satisfies Walkabout's walk when done as a combined pack hike — leader marks both adventures accordingly with date/route shared.

**Closing (5 min)**
Circle: how close was projection to actual? What changed the time? What would you plan differently for a real 20-mile weekend (shakedown hike, early start, lighter pack, water plan)? Thank adult sweep/lead and note any Scout who missed Day 2 can make up the 2-mile on the auxiliary/ Walkabout combined hike or a scheduled 90-min makeup within the week. Collect worksheets for records.

## Auxiliary Day (if needed)

**OPTIONAL Combined Auxiliary — 2-Mile Trail Day (90 min) with Walkabout.**

No separate auxiliary is required if both walks were completed on Day 1/Day 2 as planned. Use an auxiliary only if: (a) weather blocked Day 2's 2-mile, or (b) you prefer to combine Math on the Trail Req 2 with Webelos Walkabout's auxiliary 2-mile hike to reduce family trips. For a combined hike: scout the same measured 2-mile route with Walkabout's Six Essentials, weather, SAFE, and first-aid plan; time the walk for Math on the Trail credit while practicing LNT/Outdoor Code for Walkabout credit; record both adventures' logs on the same day; complete Walkabout's Req 7 reflection after. Schedule within one week of Day 2 with same route, timers, buddy system, and AHMR plan. If no Walkabout pairing, a standalone 90-min auxiliary on the same measured 2-mile route completes Req 2 + Req 3 projection with identical Day 2 worksheets.

## Materials Summary

Measured quarter-mile and 2-mile route maps + cones/markers (8–10), stopwatches/timers (3–4), clipboards + pace/projection worksheets (12 of each), pencils, calculators (3), 20-mile factor cards/poster, local park/trail maps, weather printouts, first-aid kit, water bottles, whistles, buddy tags/attendance, trash bags, optional measuring wheel, reflective vests. All time-based requirements depend on pre-measured distance — do not estimate on the day.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and on the walk. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines. Scout measured routes in advance for surface, traffic, hazards, restroom/water access, and cell coverage; obtain park/trail permission and plan go/no-go on weather. Use buddy system, active adult supervision (front/ middle/ sweep), and no solo walking. Carry first-aid kit, ample water, and sun protection; enforce LNT — stay on trail, pack out trash. Know heat- and cold-related illness signs, and check AHMRs for mobility, asthma, or other fitness considerations. Follow the most restrictive local regulation and site rules; if heat index, lightning, or air quality triggers, postpone to rain date or indoor track backup and adjust pace expectations.
