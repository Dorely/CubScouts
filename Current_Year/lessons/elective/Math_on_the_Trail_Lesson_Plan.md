# Math on the Trail Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Determine your walking pace by walking 1/4 mile. Make a projection on how long it would take you to walk 2 miles.
- [ ] **2.** Walk 2 miles and record the time it took you to complete the two miles.
- [ ] **3.** Make a projection on how long it would take you to hike a 20-mile trail over two days. List all the factors to consider for your projection.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Measure and Walk

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes · **Requirements:** 1, 2

Time a quarter-mile walk, predict the two-mile time, then walk two miles and record the result.

**Prep and materials:** Measure the course and lap counts. Bring timing devices, maps or markers, paper, and pencils for each Scout’s predictions and results.

### Day 2 — Two-Day Trail Projection

**Approximate time:** 20–30 minutes within a meeting · **Requirements:** 3

Use the recorded results to project time for a 20-mile trail over two days and discuss terrain, breaks, and pace.

**Prep and materials:** Bring the recorded times, paper, pencils, and calculators. This discussion can also follow the walk.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Math on the Trail Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Math_on_the_Trail_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/math-on-the-trail/)

### Requirement 1

- **Timing a Quarter Mile Indoor**: Cub Scouts project how long it will take to walk 2 miles in an indoor setting
- **Timing a Quarter Mile On the Trail**: Cub Scouts project how long it will take to walk 2 miles on a trail.
- **Timing a Quarter Mile Outdoor Track**: Cub Scouts project how long it will take to walk 2 miles on a track.

### Requirement 2

- **Timing 2 Miles Indoor**: Cub Scouts validate how long it will take to walk 2 miles in an indoor setting.
- **Timing 2 Miles On the Trail**: Cub Scouts validate how long it will take to walk 2 miles on a trail.
- **Timing 2 Miles Outdoor Track**: Cub Scouts validate how long it will take to walk 2 miles on an outdoor track.

### Requirement 3

- **If I could walk 20 miles**: Using the times recorded in requirements 1 and 2, determine how long it will take to walk 20 miles.
