# Let's Camp Webelos Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** With your den, pack, or family, plan and participate in a campout.
- [ ] **2.** Upon arrival at the campground, determine where to set up a tent.
- [ ] **3.** Set up your tent without help from an adult.
- [ ] **4.** Identify a potential weather hazard that could occur in your area. Determine the action you will take if you experience the weather hazard during the campout.
- [ ] **5.** Show how to tie a bowline. Explain when this knot should be used and why.
- [ ] **6.** Know the fire safety rules. Using those rules, locate a safe area to build a campfire.
- [ ] **7.** Using tinder, kindling, and fuel wood, properly build a teepee fire lay. If circumstances permit, and there is no local restriction on fires, show how to safely light the fire while under adult supervision. After allowing the fire to burn safely, extinguish the flames with minimal impact to the fire site.
- [ ] **8.** Recite the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids from Memory.
- [ ] **9.** After your campout, share the things you did to follow the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids with your den or family.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Campout Preparation

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 1 planning; 3–5, 8

Plan the campout, practice tent setup and bowlines, discuss weather responses, and practice the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace from memory.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm reservations, meals, equipment responsibilities, and the BALOO-trained leader for a pack overnighter. Bring tents, rope, weather prompts, and Outdoor Code/Leave No Trace materials.

### Campout

**Approximate time:** One overnight; about 2–3 hours of adventure activities across the stay · **Requirements:** 1–3, 6–8

Select a tent site, set up the tent, practice fire skills, and recite the outdoor principles.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm campground fire restrictions and gather tinder, kindling, fuel wood, and permitted fire equipment. Families pack camping gear and food; allow Scouts time to set up their tents.

### Den or Family Reflection

**Approximate time:** 15–20 minutes after the campout · **Requirements:** 9

Share how Scouts followed the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace.

**Prep and materials:** Arrange reflection before departure or at the next den meeting.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Let's Camp Webelos Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Lets_Camp_Webelos_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/lets-camp-webelos/)

### Requirement 1

- **Let's Camp Webelos**: Cub Scouts attend an overnight campout with your pack or at a council or district event.

### Requirement 2

- **Home Tent Home**: Once at the campsite, Cub Scouts learn where to set up a tent at the campground.

### Requirement 3

- **Indoor Tent Setup Practice**: Cub Scouts practice setting up a tent indoors before the campout without help from an adult.
- **We Got This!**: Cub Scouts compete in a relay race to set up a tent with no adult assistance.

### Requirement 4

- **Weather Emergency Preparedness Drill Game**: Cub Scouts play a game to learn what to do during a weather hazard.

### Requirement 5

- **Bowline Just In Time**: Cub Scouts learn about the bowline knot and compete to tie one the fastest
- **Bowline Knot**: Cub Scouts learn how to tie a bowline knot and when it should be used.

### Requirement 6

- **Fire Safety Rule Review**: Cub Scouts review fire safety rules and locate a safe area for a campfire.

### Requirement 7

- **Build A Fire**: Cub Scouts learn how to build a teepee fire and properly extinguish it.

### Requirement 8

- **Cut and Paste**: Cub Scouts unscramble phases and place in order to learn the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids.
- **Telephone**: Cub Scouts play telephone while learning the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids.

### Requirement 9

- **Following the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids**: Cub Scouts review their ability to follow the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles For Kids.
