# Let's Camp Webelos Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min + auxiliary overnight campout (REQUIRED, 1 night) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — overnight campout is REQUIRED; BALOO, campsite, Hazardous Weather, and fire logistics must be booked before Day 1.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Lets_Camp_Webelos_Adventure.md` and Age Appropriate Guidelines for Scouting Activities (camping). Print Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids cards/posters, bowline instruction sheets, tent-site selection checklist (flat/level, high/dry, wind/rain, hazard-free, water/toilet distance), and Req 9 reflection worksheet. Complete leader Hazardous Weather training (my.scouting.org) and watch Weather Related Safety moment (1:48) yourself. Send parent notice with campout date options (weekend overnight), packing list (Six Essentials + tent/sleeping gear), AHMR collection reminder, and note that Scouts will set up tents indoors without adult help.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** **Book before Day 1 if possible.** Reserve campsite (pack overnighter or council/district event) and confirm BALOO-trained registered adult will be present the entire campout — mandatory for pack overnighters. Confirm fire permit/site rules, cooking plan and food-allergy coverage, AHMRs for all youth and adults (check EpiPen plan, ensure another adult can administer), Hazardous Weather training current for at least one leader, campsite reservation + parking/restrooms/water/trash, weather-hazard briefing (leader-chosen local hazard — e.g., thunderstorm, flash flood, extreme heat), fire safety area scouting, transportation and buddy pairs. Stage indoor tent(s) for Day 1 practice (2–3 tents, footprints/ground cloths, stakes/mallets for demo only indoors), bowline ropes (paracord 3 ft per Scout + 2 longer demo ropes), and fire-lay demo kit for Day 2 prep (tinder/kindling/fuel sticks — no ignition on Day 2 den night).
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Outdoor Code + LNT for Kids cards/posters, tent site checklist posters, tents (2–3) + footprints, stakes/mallets, bowline ropes (12 short + 2 long), bowline instruction cards, campout planning worksheets, weather hazard scenario cards, fire safety rules poster + safe-site diagram, teepee fire-lay demo materials (tinder/kindling/fuel sticks, fire ring photo), first-aid kit, attendance/buddy-tag sheets, trash bags for LNT practice, flashlight/whistle demo, AHMR folder, campout info/permission packet, reflection worksheets. Indoor tent setup on Day 1 uses no adult help — adults observe only.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **REQUIRED auxiliary overnight — 3rd day, not optional.** This plan deliberately puts the overnight on an auxiliary campout day: 1 night at reserved campsite with BALOO coverage. Auxiliary completes Req 1 (campout), Req 4 (weather hazard action on site), Req 6 (locate safe fire area), Req 7 (teepee lay / light if permitted / extinguish with minimal impact), and Req 9 (reflection on Outdoor Code/LNT after campout). Day 1 covers Req 2 (tent site), Req 5 (bowline), Req 8 (memory work) indoors plus Req 3 indoor rehearsal; Day 2 is final campout/fire/Outdoor Code prep — no ignition indoors. Families must treat the campout as the adventure's core, not an extra.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Lets_Camp_Webelos_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** With your den, pack, or family, plan and participate in a campout.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Upon arrival at the campground, determine where to set up a tent.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Set up your tent without help from an adult.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Identify a potential weather hazard that could occur in your area. Determine the action you will take if you experience the weather hazard during the campout.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Show how to tie a bowline. Explain when this knot should be used and why.
- [ ] **Req 6:** Know the fire safety rules. Using those rules, locate a safe area to build a campfire.
- [ ] **Req 7:** Using tinder, kindling, and fuel wood, properly build a teepee fire lay. If circumstances permit, and there is no local restriction on fires, show how to safely light the fire while under adult supervision. After allowing the fire to burn safely, extinguish the flames with minimal impact to the fire site.
- [ ] **Req 8:** Recite the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids from memory.
- [ ] **Req 9:** After your campout, share the things you did to follow the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace Principles for Kids with your den or family.

## Day 1 — Tent Site, Bowline & Outdoor Code Memory (60 min) — low-coordination Reqs only

*Classroom + open floor for indoor tents. Groups of 3. No fire.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *trustworthy* and *clean* — be prepared, leave no trace). Preview Let's Camp Webelos as the campout elective and explain structure: Day 1 = choose site + bowline + memory work; Day 2 = campout/fire/weather prep; Auxiliary = the actual REQUIRED overnight where fire and campout complete. Confirm campout date, BALOO leader, and that Scouts will pitch tents without adult help starting today.

**Activity 1 — Where to Pitch? + Indoor Tent Without Adult Help (25 min) — Req 2 & Req 3**
- *Site Selection (10 min):* With photos/diagram of a campground (or taped "campsite" on floor with hazard cards: low/wet spot, widow-maker branch, slope, too close to water/fire ring, high-wind exposure), teams of 3 pick the best tent spot and justify — high/flat/dry, level, hazard-free overhead, 200 ft from water where needed, near but not on trails, wind to back. Leader records den criteria poster. Completes Req 2 concept (final site choice confirmed at the real campground on the auxiliary).
- *Indoor Tent Setup — No Adult Help (15 min):* Teams of 3 erect one tent each on floor with footprint only — adults observe and give verbal guidance only, no hands on poles/fabric. Scouts sort poles, thread sleeves, raise, and square doors away from wind (as marked). Leader checks that each Scout did measurable work and that no adult touched the tent. If stakes would damage floor, stage staking outside or mark with tape. Completes Req 3 rehearsal indoors; verified again at the auxiliary campsite.

**Activity 2 — Bowline: Tie and Tell (15 min) — Req 5**
- *Learn (7 min):* Leader demos bowline — "rabbit up through hole, around tree, back down" — forms fixed loop that won't slip. Show uses: rescue loop, securing load, around post. Two methods (bunny and hand-wrap) so Scouts find one that clicks.
- *Practice & Explain (8 min):* Each Scout ties bowline on short paracord at least twice; groups of 3 peer-check loops for correct dressed knot. Each Scout states when/why: non-slip loop for rescue/hanging bear bag/tying around object where loop must hold. Leader verifies each Scout can tie and explain. Completes Req 5.

**Activity 3 — Outdoor Code & LNT for Kids — From Memory (10 min) — Req 8**
- *Cut-and-Paste / Telephone Prep (6 min):* With words cut on strips, teams of 3 unscramble Outdoor Code (4 points: clean, careful, considerate, conservation-minded) and LNT for Kids (plan ahead, stick to trails/campsites, trash your trash, leave what you find, be kind to others, respect wildlife). Practice reciting without reading.
- *Memory Check (4 min):* Each Scout attempts recitation from memory to adult — allow prompt then re-try. Those not yet fluent take cards home to practice; final memory check continues Day 2 and is verified at the campout. Progress toward Req 8 — full sign-off when recited from memory.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap tent-site rules, bowline uses, and Outdoor Code/LNT memory task. Preview Day 2 — weather hazard plan, fire safety rules, teepee lay dry-build, and campout logistics (packing, buddy system, AHMR/permission). Assign home practice: bowline + memorization.

## Day 2 — Campout Prep: Weather, Fire Safety & Teepee Lay (60 min) — Advanced / Coordinated Reqs HERE

*Classroom with floor space. All heavy coordination is pre-booked; actual ignition is auxiliary only.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Buddy roll call, weather window for campout weekend preview, and campout info sheet (map, arrival time, site rules, BALOO leader name, Hazardous Weather training confirmation, fire permit status). Restate: Day 2 is prep — light/extinguish happens at the campout fire ring only.

**Activity 1 — Weather Hazard & Action Plan (15 min) — Req 4**
- *Hazard Brief (7 min):* Leader presents local hazard most likely for the season (e.g., thunderstorm/lightning, heat wave, flash flood, high wind). Show forecast source and campout-specific triggers (lightning within 6 miles, heat index > 90, flood watch).
- *Action Table (8 min):* Groups of 3 fill action rows — if hazard occurs *before* pitching: postpone/seek shelter; *during* campout: seek vehicle/building, lightning position, hydrate/shade, account for buddies; *after*: check site, LNT cleanup. Each group presents one action; leader records den weather plan and adds to campout packet. Completes Req 4.

**Activity 2 — Fire Safety Rules & Safe Site Location (15 min) — Req 6**
- *Rules Review (8 min):* Present fire safety rules — adult present, clear 10 ft circle, wind check, no flammables, water/shovel nearby, keep small, never leave unattended, fully extinguish. Use photo of a proper fire ring.
- *Find the Spot (7 min):* On campground map/photo, teams of 3 locate a safe build area using rules — leeward, level, away from tents/trees/overhangs, near water source, existing ring if available. Teams mark site and justify. Leader confirms every Scout can state at least 3 rules and locate a safe site conceptually; final on-site location happens at the auxiliary. Completes Req 6 knowledge; site verified at campout.

**Activity 3 — Teepee Lay Dry Build & Outdoor Code Rehearsal (20 min) — Req 7 prep + Req 8**
- *Teepee Build — No Flame Indoors (12 min):* At tables in groups of 3, Scouts lay teepee with tinder bundle base, kindling teepee, then fuel wood teepee — no ignition. Leader demos lean order, airflow gap, and fuel gradation. Each Scout places at least one kindling/fuel piece. Discuss lighting only under adult supervision at campout if permitted, and extinguishing with minimal impact (drown-stir-feel, cold to touch, disperse ash per site rules).
- *Outdoor Code / LNT Finish (8 min):* Recite again from memory in pairs — adults initial progress on Req 8; Scouts not yet fluent get extra home week. Quick preview of Req 9 — after campout we will share how we followed Outdoor Code/LNT at the site.

**Closing (5 min)**
Packing check: Six Essentials, tent, sleeping gear, water, weather layers, headlamp, trash bag for LNT. Buddy pairs and supervision plan, AHMR/permission turn-in deadline, and campout arrival logistics. Remind families: campout and fire are auxiliary — attendance required to complete Reqs 1, 4, 6, 7, 9. Thank adults and confirm BALOO leader contact.

## Auxiliary Day — REQUIRED Overnight Campout (1 night + morning reflection)

*Reserved campsite. BALOO-trained registered adult present entire time. Hazardous Weather training current. Fire permit confirmed. AHMRs, buddy system, active supervision, and LNT throughout. This day is mandatory — Reqs 1, 4, 6, 7, 9 cannot be signed off without it.*

**Logistics (leader pre-check day-of):** Confirm reservation, BALOO leader on site, Hazardous Weather training verification, fire permit and site fire rules, campsite map with tent sites and fire ring, water/toilet/trash access, parking/kit restrictions, weather forecast and go/no-go, AHMRs + EpiPen plan + first-aid kit + emergency access/route, buddy tags and attendance, cooking plan with allergy coverage (review Food Allergies Safety Moment, Keep Your Food Safe, each Scout's AHMR, religious/cultural concerns), kitchen/clean-up plan.

**Program:**
- **Arrival — Pick the Site & Pitch (60–90 min) — Req 1, 2, 3:** Upon arrival, Scouts together determine where to set up tents using Day 1 criteria; leader confirms choice maps to high/flat/dry and hazard-free. Scouts pitch tents without adult help — adults observe only. Leader verifies Req 2 (site determination on arrival) and Req 3 (no adult hands). Plan the remaining campout schedule (fire time, meal, LNT walk, star/quiet time).
- **Weather Hazard Live Check (15 min) — Req 4:** At camp, re-identify today's hazard and state action if it occurred now — where to shelter, buddy/account, gear protection, LNT. Matches Day 2 plan to real conditions.
- **Fire Safety & Teepee — Locate, Build, Light (if permitted), Extinguish (60–75 min) — Req 6 & Req 7:** Using Day 2 rules, Scouts locate safe fire area (existing ring), clear hazard zone, stage water/shovel, build teepee lay with tinder/kindling/fuel. If site permits and no restriction, adult lights under direct supervision, keeps fire small, and Scouts observe safe burning; then fully extinguish — drown, stir, drown again, feel for cold, disperse per site's minimal-impact guidance. If burn ban prohibits ignition, build and extinguish drill still completes Req 7's lay and extinguish portions with leader note on restriction.
- **Outdoor Code / LNT Reflection (15 min) — Req 8 & Req 9:** After campout (evening or morning circle), Scouts recite Outdoor Code and LNT Principles for Kids from memory (final verification of Req 8), then share how they followed them — stayed on sites, trashed trash, left what they found, respected wildlife, were considerate, stored food properly. Each Scout names at least one action. Completes Req 9 (and finalizes Req 1 for participation in the full campout).

**Make-up:** If a Scout misses the auxiliary campout, they may complete Req 1 with family at an approved family campout and return with parent verification, then join a later fire/LNT session for Reqs 6, 7, 9 — do not sign off campout or fire without the overnight or a verified equivalent. Keep the den campout as the strongly preferred path.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and on the campout. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines — BALOO is mandatory for any pack overnighter (at least one registered BALOO-trained adult present). Complete Hazardous Weather training, watch Weather Related Safety moment, and check the forecast 3 days and 1 day before plus morning-of with go/no-go criteria. Review every Scout and adult AHMR for allergies, medications, and EpiPen needs — ensure at least one other adult can administer, and review Food Allergies and anaphylaxis Safety Moments plus Keep Your Food Safe for any cooking. Apply Behavior Around Campfires — locate safe area, keep fire small and attended, water/shovel within reach, full drown-stir-feel extinguish with minimal impact. Use buddy system, active supervision, and no-Solo tent setup indoors or outdoors. Confirm campsite reservation, fire permit, water/toilet access, and emergency route; follow the most restrictive local regulation and site rules. Have first-aid kit, incident plan, and adequate adult ratio (at least 1:3 for tool/fire supervision) on hand.
