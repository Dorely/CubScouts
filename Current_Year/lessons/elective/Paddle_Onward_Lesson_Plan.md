# Paddle Onward Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min + auxiliary flat-water day REQUIRED (60–90 min) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Very high coordination — flat-water pack activity REQUIRED. BOOK POOL + POND/LAKE BEFORE DAY 1.**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — auxiliary flat-water day controls this adventure; Safety Afloat, craft, and swimmer test must be locked before Day 1.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Paddle_Onward_Adventure.md`, Safety Afloat (my.scouting.org, 30 min) and Safe Swim Defense. Decide paddle craft for the den — canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard — and keep one choice for all Scouts to simplify craft/paddle/jacket logistics (mixed craft is allowed but complicates ratios). Print Safety Afloat 9-point handouts, life-jacket fit cards (USCG-approved types, size by weight/chest, "chin-shoulder-strap" check), commitment card template, buddy-tag sheets, and craft-choice worksheet. Send parent notice with auxiliary date options (pool session + flat-water pond/lake, calm water only — no float trips/expeditions/running water), swimmer-test prerequisite warning (Req 1 must pass before Reqs 5–9), AHMR collection, and life-jacket sizing info (ask for weight/chest). Confirm participant limits: council/district/pack/den event on flat-water pond or controlled lake area free of powerboats/sailboats for Cub Scouts.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** **Book before Day 1 if possible — this is the long pole.** Reserve pool for swimmer tests + Req 5 jump/swim and calm flat-water site for Req 6–9 (pond or controlled lake with easy launch, shallow entry, no powerboats). Confirm Safety Afloat-trained adult (21+ within last 2 years) with commitment card + CPR-trained adult — both present the entire auxiliary — plus 1 supervisor per 5 Scouts, buddy tags for every youth and adult near water, and sufficient USCG-approved properly fitted life jackets (one per person by size) + paddles sized to Scout + craft (canoes/kayaks/SUPs + paddles, 3–4 craft for 7–9 Scouts). Book certified swim-test administrator and lifeguard coverage if required by site. Pull site permissions, parking, restrooms, water, weather, and emergency access; set auxiliary go/no-go on wind/weather. Stage life jackets (6–10 by size), demo paddles (3 lengths), craft photos/diagrams, Safety Afloat poster, and AHMR/buddy-tag kit for Day 2 fitting. Confirm no running water and that all parents understand swimmer test is prerequisite before any paddling Reqs 5–9.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Safety Afloat 9-point posters/cards, commitment cards, life jackets (USCG-approved, youth S/M/L sized — 10), demo paddles (3 lengths + grip/ferrule samples), craft photos/diagrams (canoe/kayak/SUP with parts labeled), buddy tags + attendance, AHMR folder, whistle, first-aid kit, throw rope (demo), tape/markers, craft-choice worksheets, life-jacket fit checklists, auxiliary info/permission packet, weather printouts, trash bags for LNT. Auxiliary adds: flat-water craft + paddles (3–4 craft, 8–10 paddles), USCG jackets for all, buddy-tag board, sunscreen, water, emergency plan, site permission.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **REQUIRED auxiliary flat-water day — 3rd day, not optional (60–90 min pack activity + pool time).** This plan puts Req 2 (craft pick), Req 3 (Safety Afloat), Req 4 (life-jacket choice/wear) on Day 1 in the classroom; Day 2 in the den is briefing/fitting/rehearsal for Req 6 (enter/exit), Req 7 (tip discussion), Req 8 (paddle sizing/response) with dry-land practice only. The actual water — Req 1 swimmer test, Req 5 jump + 25-ft swim in jacket, and Req 6–9 execution plus 30-min paddle time — is the REQUIRED auxiliary pack flat-water day (pool + pond/controlled lake). Day 2 without water does not complete Reqs 5–9. Families must treat the auxiliary as the adventure's core.

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Before attempting requirements 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 for this Adventure, you must pass Scouting America swimmer test.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Pick a paddle craft for which to complete all requirements: canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Review Safety Afloat.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Demonstrate how to choose and properly wear a life jacket that is the correct size.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Jump feet first into water over your head while wearing a life jacket Then swim 25 feet wearing the life jacket.
- [ ] **Req 6:** Demonstrate how to enter and exit a canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard safely.
- [ ] **Req 7:** Discuss what to do if your canoe or kayak tips over or you fall off your stand-up paddleboard.
- [ ] **Req 8:** Learn how to pick a paddle that is the right size for you. Explore how the paddle craft responds to moving the paddle.
- [ ] **Req 9:** Have 30 minutes, or more, of canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard paddle time.

## Day 1 — Choose Your Craft, Safety Afloat & Jackets (60 min) — low-coordination Reqs only

*Classroom. No water. Groups of 3.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *brave* and *clean* — be prepared near water, leave shoreline clean). Preview Paddle Onward as a flat-water paddling elective on calm water only. Explain structure: Day 1 = pick craft + Safety Afloat + life jackets in the classroom; Day 2 = dry-land briefing and fitting for enter/exit, tip recovery, and paddle sizing; Auxiliary = REQUIRED pool + pond/lake day for swimmer test, jump/swim, and all on-water skills + 30-min paddle. Emphasize **swimmer test (Req 1) is prerequisite before Reqs 5–9** — no one paddles until they pass.

**Activity 1 — Pick a Paddle Craft (15 min) — Req 2**
- *Craft Options (8 min):* With photos/diagrams, compare canoe (open, 2-person, kneel/sit, J-stroke), kayak (decked, sit-in/on-top, double paddle), SUP (standing, single paddle, balance). Cover parts, stability, and where each shines on calm water. Note Cub Scout limit — calm or gently flowing water only, no float-trip expeditions, council/district/pack/den event on flat-water pond or controlled lake free of powerboats/sailboats.
- *Den Choice (7 min):* Groups of 3 discuss, then den picks one craft to complete all requirements — record choice on poster and worksheets. Leader notes craft choice drives jacket and paddle sizing at auxiliary. If site only offers canoes, pick canoe. Completes Req 2 — every Scout has picked the craft (den single choice is permitted and strongly recommended for logistics).

**Activity 2 — Safety Afloat Review (20 min) — Req 3**
- *Nine Points Walk (12 min):* Present Safety Afloat — qualified supervision (21+ trained within 2 years), commitment, skill proficiency, planning, equipment, etc. — on poster. Emphasize: adult leader 21+ with Safety Afloat within 2 years + CPR-trained adult present, 1 supervisor per 5 Scouts, buddy system, proper jackets, calm water only, and that swimming portions meet Safe Swim Defense.
- *Commitment & Buddy Tags (8 min):* Groups of 3 complete commitment-card prompts and practice buddy-tag assignment (pairs, check-in/out). Quiz 4 quick scenarios — missing jacket, wind picks up, buddy separated, craft drifts — Scouts name the Safety Afloat response. Each Scout can state at least 3 points and the need for trained supervision. Completes Req 3.

**Activity 3 — Life Jackets: Choose & Wear (15 min) — Req 4**
- *Fit Lab (10 min):* Show USCG-approved jacket types, size by weight/chest, and fit test — lift shoulders: jacket should not rise above chin, straps snug, zip + buckle fastened, crotch strap if provided. Common errors: too big, loose straps, wrong type. Each Scout tries 2 sizes and learns their size with leader measurement (weight/chest).
- *Wear Check (5 min):* In groups of 3, each Scout demonstrates choosing correct size and properly wearing — cinch straps, zip, check chin movement, arm mobility. Peer check with checklist; leader initials each Scout. Jackets are not used in water today — fitting only. Completes Req 4.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap craft choice, Safety Afloat points, and jacket fit. Preview Day 2 dry-land rehearsal (enter/exit, tip, paddle pick) and REQUIRED auxiliary with swimmer test first — bring swimsuit/towel, confirm jacket sizes sent home, and return AHMR/permission. No Scout attempts Reqs 5–9 before passing swimmer test.

## Day 2 — Dry-Land Briefing & Fitting (60 min) — Advanced / Coordinated Reqs HERE

*Classroom + open floor. Dry land only — no water. Use tape floor craft outlines, demo jackets, and paddles.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Buddy roll call, confirm auxiliary date/location, Safety Afloat-trained adult + CPR adult names, 1:5 ratio, and site rules. Reconfirm craft choice and that swimmer test is first at the auxiliary.

**Activity 1 — Enter & Exit Safely (15 min) — Req 6 (dry-land briefing)**
- *Demo (7 min):* With floor tape as dock/shoreline and a stable bench or taped craft outline, demo canoe/kayak/SUP enter/exit — low center, 3 points of contact, paddle staged, adult steadying, weight centered, buddy assist. Show dock vs. shallow-water entry variants.
- *Rehearse (8 min):* Groups of 3 rotate — each Scout practices low-center stepping, holding gunwale/rail, and exiting without tipping (dry). Adults spot and correct. Leader checks every Scout can narrate steps. Note: live in-craft enter/exit verification happens at the auxiliary — this is the briefing/fitting pass.

**Activity 2 — If You Tip / Fall (15 min) — Req 7**
- *Discussion (10 min):* For chosen craft: stay with craft, hold paddle if safe, stay on upstream side (for canoe/kayak), signal buddy, roll/ remount for SUP, defensive float if needed. Show hold-the-craft and "reach the shore" sequence. Stress life jacket does the float — stay calm, stay with craft, call for buddy/adult.
- *Scenarios (5 min):* Teams of 3 act out one tip/fall scenario and name 3 correct actions. Leader verifies understanding for each Scout. Full in-water tip discussion is reinforced again at the auxiliary shore before launching.

**Activity 3 — Pick the Right Paddle & How the Craft Responds (20 min) — Req 8 (briefing)**
- *Sizing (10 min):* Demo paddle sizing — canoe: handle to chin/nose when seated; kayak: fingertip-to-fingletip + 6–8 in / paddle height check; SUP: overhead reach to wrist. Common errors: too long (clumsy) vs. too short (bending). Each Scout is measured and assigned a target paddle length printed on wristband/card.
- *Response Preview (10 min):* At tables, use tabletop craft model or paddle over bench to explore how moving paddle moves craft — forward stroke = forward, sweep = turn, draw = sideways, back = stop/reverse, and for SUP keep blade angle forward. Each Scout handles a paddle (no water) and demonstrates grip (knuckles up, hands shoulder-width, blade angle). Live response exploration completes at the auxiliary on calm water.

**Closing (5 min)**
Review what transfers to the auxiliary: jackets already fitted, paddle lengths assigned, enter/exit and tip plan known, Safety Afloat points memorized. Hand out auxiliary packet — swimsuit, towel, fitted jacket size confirmation, water/shade, buddy tags, arrival time, and reminder that swimmer test is first before any Req 5–9 water work. Collect Day 2 checklists; no water requirements are signed off until the auxiliary.

## Auxiliary Day — REQUIRED Pool + Flat-Water Paddle Day (60–90 min pack activity)

*Pool for Req 1 + Req 5, then flat-water pond or controlled lake area free of powerboats/sailboats for Req 6–9. Council/district/pack/den event. Safety Afloat 21+ within 2 years + commitment card + CPR-trained adult present entire time. 1 supervisor per 5 Scouts. Buddy tags for everyone near water. USCG jackets on all on-water participants. Qualified adult supervision continuous. This day is mandatory — Reqs 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 cannot be signed off without it.*

**Logistics (leader pre-check morning-of):** Confirm pool reservation + flat-water site permission, Safety Afloat-trained adult (21+) with current card + CPR adult on site, craft + paddles + jackets staged and counted by size, buddy-tag board + attendance + AHMR/EpiPen + first-aid kit + emergency access, weather/wind check with go/no-go (postpone on whitecaps/high wind), shoreline hazard scan, launch area clear, Safe Swim Defense for any swim portion, and parental permissions with swim history. Bring sunscreen, water, trash bags for LNT, and throw rope.

**Program — Pool First:**
- **Swimmer Test — Req 1 (20 min):** Certified administrator gives Scouting America swimmer test (jump in, swim 75 yd strong + 25 yd restful, float). Requirement is **prerequisite** — any Scout not passing does not attempt Reqs 5–9 that day; provide practice plan and retest date. Record passes.
- **Jackets & Jump/Swim — Req 5 (15 min):** At pool edge, Scouts demonstrate correctly fitted USCG jacket, then each jumps feet first into water over head in jacket and swims 25 ft wearing jacket. Adults within reach, buddy observed, lifeguard present per site rules. Leader initials each pass.

**Program — Flat Water (pond/controlled lake, calm):**
- **Enter & Exit — Req 6 (10 min):** At shoreline/dock with adult steadying, each Scout demonstrates entering and exiting the chosen craft safely — low center, 3 points, paddle staged, adult assist. Verify for each Scout.
- **Tip / Fall Discussion Live (5 min) — Req 7:** On shore with craft in shallow water, review what to do if canoe/kayak tips or SUP fall — stay with craft, hold, signal, remount. Quick demo in ankle-deep if site allows.
- **Paddle Pick & Craft Response — Req 8 (10 min):** At shallow launch, each Scout confirms paddle length (wristband match) and explores response — forward, sweep, draw, stop — with brief solo strokes held by adult at shore before free paddling. Correct grip and blade angle.
- **30-Minute Paddle — Req 9 (30+ min):** Buddy pairs paddle on calm flat water within sight of supervisors, jackets on, 1:5 ratio enforced — 30 minutes continuous paddle time minimum. Leader logs time for each Scout; include rest water stops but keep paddle time 30+ min. Stay in designated area, no racing beyond control, LNT — pack out trash, leave shoreline as found.

**Make-up:** No den-meeting substitute for water. If a Scout misses the auxiliary, they may complete Reqs 1, 5–9 at a council/pack flat-water make-up within the program year with the same Safety Afloat/CPR/1:5/jacket/buddy-tag coverage — do not sign off Reqs 5–9 without swimmer test + supervised flat-water time.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and on the water. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines — before any watercraft, adult leaders must have completed Safety Afloat training within the previous two years and hold a commitment card; at least one leader must be trained in first aid including CPR (my.scouting.org or council training). Supervision by an adult 21+ trained in Safety Afloat with 1 supervisor per 5 Cub Scouts; buddy tags for every youth and adult near water. Wear properly fitted U.S. Coast Guard-approved life jackets at all times on open water. Skill proficiency: Cub Scout canoeing/rowboating/rafting is limited to council/district events on flat-water ponds or controlled lake areas free of powerboats and sailboats; no trips/expeditions or running water. Confirm swimmer test is passed before Reqs 5–9; check AHMRs for medical, allergy, and EpiPen needs and ensure another adult can administer. Watch for weather/wind, use go/no-go, provide continuous qualified supervision, and follow Safe Swim Defense for any swimming. Follow the most restrictive local regulation and site rules; have first-aid kit, emergency plan, and adequate adult ratio on hand.
