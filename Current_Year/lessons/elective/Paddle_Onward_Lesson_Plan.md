# Paddle Onward Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Before attempting requirements 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 for this Adventure, you must pass Scouting America swimmer test.
- [ ] **2.** Pick a paddle craft for which to complete all requirements: canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard.
- [ ] **3.** Review Safety Afloat.
- [ ] **4.** Demonstrate how to choose and properly wear a life jacket that is the correct size.
- [ ] **5.** Jump feet first into water over your head while wearing a life jacket Then swim 25 feet wearing the life jacket.
- [ ] **6.** Demonstrate how to enter and exit a canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard safely.
- [ ] **7.** Discuss what to do if your canoe or kayak tips over or you fall off your stand-up paddleboard.
- [ ] **8.** Learn how to pick a paddle that is the right size for you. Explore how the paddle craft responds to moving the paddle.
- [ ] **9.** Have 30 minutes, or more, of canoe, kayak, or stand-up paddleboard paddle time.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Craft and Swimming Preparation

**Approximate time:** 60–90 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–4

Complete swimmer testing, choose the paddle craft, review Safety Afloat, and fit life jackets.

**Prep and materials:** Coordinate with a council/district aquatics provider for qualified staff, swimmer testing, craft availability, and correct life-jacket sizes. Bring swimwear, towels, and buddy tags.

### Day 2 — Paddle Session

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 5–9

Complete the life-jacket swim, entry/exit and capsize-response skills, paddle practice, and at least 30 minutes of paddling.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm swimmer-test passes before Reqs 5–9. Reserve the chosen craft, correctly sized paddles and life jackets, and suitable water access; share arrival and equipment details with families.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Paddle Onward Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Paddle_Onward_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/paddle-onward/)

### Requirement 1

- **Webelos Swimmers Test**: Cub Scouts swim for the Scouting America swimmer test.

### Requirement 2

- **Webelos Pick a Paddle**: Cub Scouts pick from one of the paddle crafts to complete remaining requirements.

### Requirement 3

- **Webelos Swimming Safely**: Cub Scouts review the attributes of being on the water safely.

### Requirement 4

- **Webelos Life Jacket Safety**: Cub Scouts learn about life jackets.

### Requirement 5

- **Webelos Float with Confidence**: Cub Scouts use life jacket in the water.

### Requirement 6

- **Webelos Launch and Depart Canoe**: Cub Scouts practice how to enter and exit a canoe safely.
- **Webelos Launch and Depart Kayak**: Cub Scouts practice how to enter and exit a kayak safely.
- **Webelos Launch and Depart Stand-up Paddle Board**: Cub Scouts practice how to launch and dismount a paddle board.

### Requirement 7

- **Webelos Canoe Resilience**: Cub Scouts learn what to do if their canoe tips over.
- **Webelos Kayak Resilience**: Cub Scouts learn what to do if their kayak tips over.
- **Webelos Stand-up Paddle Board Resilience**: Cub Scouts learn what to do if their paddle board tips over.

### Requirement 8

- **Webelos Canoe Paddle Perfect**: Cub Scouts pick a canoe paddle and learn how to use it.
- **Webelos Kayak Paddle Perfect**: Cub Scouts pick a kayak paddle and learn how to use it.
- **Webelos Stand-up Paddle Board Paddle Perfect**: Cub Scouts pick a stand-up paddle board paddle and learn how to use it.

### Requirement 9

- **Webelos Paddle**: Cub Scouts enjoy 30 minutes of paddle craft activity.
