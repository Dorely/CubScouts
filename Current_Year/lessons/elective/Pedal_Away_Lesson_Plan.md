## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Decide on gear and supplies you should bring for a long bike ride.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Discover how multi-gear bicycles work and how they benefit a rider.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Practice how to lubricate a chain.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Pick a bicycle lock that you will use. Demonstrate how it locks and unlocks, how it secures your bicycle, and how you carry it while you are riding your bicycle.
- [ ] **Req 5:** With your family, den, or pack, use a map and plan a bicycle ride that is at least 5 miles.
- [ ] **Req 6:** With your den, pack, or family and using the buddy system, go on a bicycle ride that is a minimum of 5 miles.

## Day 1 — Gear, Gears, Chain & Locks (60 min) — low-coordination Reqs only

*Classroom. No riding. Groups of 3.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *brave* and *thrifty* — take care of equipment). Preview Pedal Away as the cycling elective. Explain structure: Day 1 = classroom skills (gear, gears, chain, lock) for 7–9 Scouts; Day 2 = map planning + ABC Quick Check; Auxiliary = REQUIRED 5-mile buddy ride on a mapped route avoiding busy roads (family/den/pack). Note e-bikes prohibited and helmets required on every ride.

**Activity 1 — Gear & Supplies for a Long Ride (15 min) — Req 1**
- *Card Sort (7 min):* Teams of 3 sort gear cards into "bring" vs. "leave" — helmet (CPSC), water, Six Essentials, bike repair kit (tube/pump/levers), weather layer, closed-toe shoes, sunscreen, map/cue sheet, phone/whistle. Discuss why each matters on a long ride vs. short loop.
- *Pack the Kit (8 min):* Each Scout builds a personal gear checklist on worksheet and shares one item with the den. Leader posts den master list. Completes Req 1 — each Scout has decided what to bring for a long ride.

**Activity 2 — How Multi-Gear Bikes Work (15 min) — Req 2**
- *Demo (8 min):* With a rear cassette or photo board, show chainrings, cassette, derailleur, and shifters. Shift through gears (bike on stand, wheel off ground) — low gear = easy pedaling uphill, high gear = distance per pedal on flats/downhill. Explain cadence and why gears benefit the rider.
- *Try It (7 min):* In safe stationary demo, each Scout operates shifter (or points to correct gear combo on diagram), names one benefit of low and one of high gear. Groups of 3 quiz each other with scenarios (hill, headwind, flat). Completes Req 2.

**Activity 3 — Lubricate a Chain (10 min) — Req 3**
- *Demo (5 min):* Cover floor, gloves on. Leader shows bike-specific chain lube, wiping old grime, applying one drop per link, back-pedaling, then wiping excess. Stress: lube the chain, not the tire/rim/brakes; avoid over-lube and keep fingers clear of moving parts.
- *Practice (5 min):* In groups of 3, each Scout practices on a chain segment or the stand bike — apply, pedal, wipe — with adult spotting. No riding. Leader initials each Scout for safe technique. Completes Req 3.

**Activity 4 — Pick a Lock & Show How You Carry It (10 min) — Req 4**
- *Compare (5 min):* Show U-lock, cable, and combination types — strength, weight, where to lock (frame + wheel to fixed rack), and how to carry (mounted bracket, in bag, wrapped — never dangling near spokes).
- *Demo (5 min):* Each Scout picks one lock type, demonstrates lock/unlock, secures bike to a chair/rack prop, and shows carry method. Peer check in threes. Completes Req 4.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap gear, gears, chain lube, and lock carry. Preview Day 2 map planning for the ≥5-mile ride and ABC Quick Check rehearsal. Remind families to confirm bike size + helmet fit for the REQUIRED auxiliary ride; no Scout rides without a properly fitted CPSC helmet and ABC-passed bike.

## Day 2 — Map the 5 Miles & Ride-Ready Check (60 min) — Advanced / Coordinated Reqs HERE

*Classroom. Maps, markers, helmets. No riding — heavy coordination reserved for auxiliary.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Buddy pair assignments, re-state buddy-system rule for every ride. Confirm auxiliary date/window, mapped route avoiding busy roads, support adult + phone/first-aid plan, and weather go/no-go.

**Activity 1 — Plan the Ride on a Map (25 min) — Req 5**
- *Map Brief (8 min):* With a large local map and an online-mapping projection (if available), show how to measure distance — scale bar, mileage tool, or string-and-scale — and how to avoid busy roads by choosing trails, neighborhood streets, and protected crossings. Mark start, rest/water stops, and finish to total ≥5 miles.
- *Group Planning (17 min):* Teams of 3 each draft a ≥5-mile loop/ out-and-back on their copy, label streets/trail segments, crossings, and stops, and calculate total distance. Den compares routes and adopts one official ride map; leader photocopies/print-screens the final map + cue sheet for every family. Each Scout can point out the route and total mileage. Completes Req 5.

**Activity 2 — ABC Quick Check & Ride Readiness (25 min) — Preps Req 6**
- *ABC Stations (15 min):* Rotate through Air (tire pressure + pinch test), Brakes (pads, levers, cables), Cranks/Chain/Cogs (tight cranks, clean/lubed chain, cassette teeth), Quick release (wheels/seat tight, check ride — lift and spin, bounce test). Use poster/cards; each Scout completes a checklist on the demo bike with adult verification.
- *Helmet & Buddy Rules (10 min):* Each Scout demonstrates correctly fitted helmet — level, two fingers above brow, V-straps below ears, one finger under buckle, shake test — and states buddy-system rules (stay with buddy, check-in at stops, notify adult if separated, no headphones while riding). Adults adjust fits. This is the dress rehearsal for the auxiliary; no miles logged today.

**Closing (5 min)**
Review the adopted 5-mile map, ABC pass standard, and gear list. Hand out auxiliary packet — map/cue sheet, start time/location, buddy pairs, what to bring (bike, helmet, water, layer, Six Essentials), and parent support assignments. Remind: e-bikes prohibited, every bike gets an ABC check at the auxiliary before rolling, and the ride is buddy-pairs only with adult support.

## Auxiliary Day — REQUIRED Family/Den/Pack 5-Mile Buddy Ride (60–90 min)

*Pre-mapped ≥5-mile route avoiding busy roads — trail, greenway, or quiet neighborhood loop. Buddy pairs, adult support, first-aid kit + phone, support adult/vehicle trailing or at stops. CPSC helmets required; e-bikes prohibited. ABC Quick Check on every bike before start. Council/den/pack/family activity with signed AHMR/consent.*

**Logistics (leader pre-check morning-of):** Confirm route permission (park/trail) and low-traffic conditions, weather go/no-go, buddy-pair sheet + attendance + AHMRs, first-aid kit and emergency access, water/restroom stops, support adult with phone and basic repair kit, helmet-count by size, and that every bike passes ABC Quick Check (air, brakes, cranks/chain/cogs, quick release, check ride). Brief rules: ride on right, single file, obey signals/stop signs, callouts ("on your left," "stopping," "hole"), buddy stays together, adults at front and rear, no earbuds, walk bikes across major crossings if required.

**Program:**
- **ABC & Helmet Check — Pre-ride (10 min):** Each Scout's bike gets a leader-verified ABC check; each helmet fit checked. Fix or sideline any fail.
- **Buddy Ride — Req 6 (45–70 min):** Buddy pairs depart with adult front and rear. Leader navigates via cue sheet/map; tail adult carries phone/first-aid. Stop at 1–2 planned water/rest points for buddy check and headcount. Minimum 5 miles — track via bike computer, GPS, or mapped distance; leader logs mileage for each Scout. Use buddy system entire time — no solo riding.
- **Wrap (5 min):** At finish, group reviews the mapped vs. ridden route, celebrates completion, and logs Req 6. Discuss one thing gears made easier if the route included a hill.

**Make-up:** No classroom substitute for the ride. If a Scout misses the den auxiliary, they may complete Req 6 with family/pack on the same mapped ≥5-mile route (or leader-approved equivalent avoiding busy roads) using the buddy system, with a parent/guardian logging the ride and confirming ABC/helmet compliance — do not sign off Req 6 without a verified 5-mile buddy ride.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and on the ride. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Bicycle Safety guidelines — CPSC helmet required for every rider, e-bikes and hybrid-electric bikes prohibited in Scouting activities, conduct an ABC Quick Check (Air, Brakes, Cranks/Chain/Cogs, Quick release, Check ride) before every ride, and use the buddy system with qualified adult supervision at the front and rear plus a support adult with phone/first-aid. Choose a pre-mapped ≥5-mile route that avoids busy roads — prefer trails, greenways, and quiet streets with signalized crossings; obey traffic laws, ride single file on the right, and walk bikes where required. Check AHMRs for medical/allergy/EpiPen needs, ensure another adult can administer, and follow the most restrictive local regulation and site rules. Set a weather go/no-go (wind, heat, lightning) and postpone if conditions are unsafe — never ride in low visibility or storms.
