# Pedal Away Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Decide on gear and supplies you should bring for a long bike ride.
- [ ] **2.** Discover how multi-gear bicycles work and how they benefit a rider.
- [ ] **3.** Practice how to lubricate a chain.
- [ ] **4.** Pick a bicycle lock that you will use. Demonstrate how it locks and unlocks, how it secures your bicycle, and how you carry it while you are riding your bicycle.
- [ ] **5.** With your family, den, or pack, use a map and plan a bicycle ride that is at least 5 miles.
- [ ] **6.** With your den, pack, or family and using the buddy system, go on a bicycle ride that is a minimum of 5 miles.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Bikes and Route Planning

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–5

Choose gear, explore gears, lubricate chains, practice locks, and map a ride of at least five miles.

**Prep and materials:** Bring bicycles, a multi-gear demonstration bike, lubricant, rags, locks, maps, and a gear list. Arrange an adult to help with maintenance and plan turns for each Scout.

### Day 2 — Five-Mile Ride

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 6

Ride with the den, pack, or family using the buddy system.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm the route, start/finish, transport, and borrowed equipment. Families bring bikes, helmets, water, and essentials; allow time for equipment checks.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Pedal Away Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Pedal_Away_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/pedal-away/)

### Requirement 1

- **All We Need**: Cub Scouts learn the additional gear needed for a longer bike ride.
- **I Went on a Bike Ride and Took**: Cub Scouts determine the gear needed for a longer bike ride through a game.

### Requirement 2

- **Webelos Gear Guidance**: Cub Scouts learn about multi-gear bikes.

### Requirement 3

- **Webelos Chain Maintenance**: Cub Scouts learn how to maintain their bike chain.

### Requirement 4

- **Webelos Lock It Up**: Cub Scouts learn about different types of bike locks and how to open them.

### Requirement 5

- **Webelos Map The Ride**: Cub Scouts plan and choose route for the 5-mile bike ride.

### Requirement 6

- **Webelos Bike Ride**: Cub Scouts and adults go on a 5-mile bike ride.
