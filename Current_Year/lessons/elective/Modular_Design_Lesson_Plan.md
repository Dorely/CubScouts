# Modular Design Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Learn what modular design is and identify three things that use modular design in their construction.
- [ ] **2.** Using modular-based building pieces, build a model without a set of instructions.
- [ ] **3.** Using the model made in requirement 2, create a set of step-by-step instructions on how to make your model.
- [ ] **4.** Have someone make your model using your instructions.
- [ ] **5.** Using the same modular pieces used in requirement 2, build another model of something different.
- [ ] **6.** With your parent or legal guardian's permission, watch a video demonstrating how something was built using modular design.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Build and Make Instructions

**Approximate time:** 60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–3

Explore modular examples, build a model, and write, draw, or record its instructions.

**Prep and materials:** Gather modular pieces, trays or labeled bags, paper, pencils, and three real-world examples. Set model sizes that leave time for instructions; provide a recording device for Live Action.

### Day 2 — Test, Rebuild, and Watch

**Approximate time:** 60 minutes · **Requirements:** 4–6

Have a partner build from the instructions, use the same pieces for a different model, and watch a modular-construction video.

**Prep and materials:** Keep models or photos, instructions, and labeled pieces from Day 1. Arrange partners and matching pieces or disassembly. Preview the video and obtain parent/guardian permission.

### Single-Meeting Option

**Approximate time:** 90 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–6

Use small models to complete the build, instructions, partner test, rebuild, and video together.

**Prep and materials:** Sort pieces and set up partners and the video before Scouts arrive.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Modular Design Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Modular_Design_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/modular-design/)

### Requirement 1

- **Modular**: Cub Scouts learn concepts of modular design and construction and it benefits.

### Requirement 2

- **Let's Build It**: Using modular based building pieces Cub Scouts build a model.

### Requirement 3

- **Draw It Out**: Cub Scouts draw instructions on how to make their design structure.
- **Live Action**: Cub Scouts create video instructions on how to make their design structure.
- **Write It Down**: Cub Scouts write down instructions on how to make their design structure.

### Requirement 4

- **Following Instructions**: Cub Scouts follow instructions to build a model.

### Requirement 5

- **My Build**: Cub Scouts design and build a model house of their own imagination or something different.

### Requirement 6

- **It's Real**: Watch a video of a real-life project that uses modular design.
