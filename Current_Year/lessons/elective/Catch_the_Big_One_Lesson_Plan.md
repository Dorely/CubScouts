# Catch the Big One Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min + auxiliary fishing day 60–90 min (REQUIRED) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — fishing venue, permissions, licenses/permits, gear, and +1 hour fishing block must be secured before Day 1.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review `Catch the Big One Adventure` reference and Scouting America fishing content plus Fishing Basics resources. Print SAFE Checklist, Fishing Plan worksheet (where/when/what fish), environment + tackle box lists, and knot cards (improved clinch, Palomar). Gather sample rods/tackle, line, hooks (barbless demo), bobbers, sinkers, and practice cord/rope for knots. Send parent notice explaining classroom Day 1 vs. fishing Day 2/auxiliary and that fishing day requires adult supervision and buddy system.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** **Book these before Day 1 if possible.** Reserve fishing venue — pond, lake, or stocked community water — and get written permission to fish; confirm dates, hours, parking, restrooms, shade, and no-swimming separation. Check state youth/adult fishing license and permit requirements (many states exempt under-16 but require adult license; confirm locally), catch-and-release rules, and size/limits. Confirm additional adults who can assist Scouts 1:3 on the bank, review emergency plan and weather go/no-go, and confirm gear inventory (rods/tackle, bait, stringers/nets, measuring tape). Pull weather forecast 3 days and 1 day before fishing; arrange transportation and pack-family invitations.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Printed SAFE Checklist, Fishing Plan and Tackle Box worksheets, fish environment cards/diorama supplies, knot-tying cord (paracord practice line, 3 ft per Scout) + fishing line + hooks (demo), fishing rods (3–5 shared) + tackle boxes, bobbers/sinkers/plastics, bait (worms/powerbait — buy day-before), bucket, stringer/net, measuring tape, scissors/nail clippers, hand sanitizer, paper towels, trash bags (pack out line), sunscreen, water bottles, whistles, attendance/buddy sheet, first-aid kit, AHMR folder, fishing permissions/licenses folder.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **REQUIRED — 60–90 min pack family fishing auxiliary day.** This plan teaches plan/knots/gear on Day 1 in the classroom and runs the ≥1 hour fishing (Req 7) on Day 2. If your den site has no fishable water, move Req 7 entirely to a 3rd auxiliary pack family fishing day (weekend morning at a pond/lake, 60–90 min lines-in-water plus check-in/out). Even with a Day 2 pond, keep an auxiliary make-up fishing slot for Scouts who miss Day 2 — Req 7 cannot be completed indoors.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Catch_the_Big_One_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Make a plan to go fishing. Determine where you will go and what type of fish you plan to catch. All of the following requirements are to be completed based on your choice.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist to plan what you need for your fishing experience.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Describe the environment where the fish might be found.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Make a list of the equipment and materials you will need to fish.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Determine the best type of knot to tie your hook to your line and tie it.
- [ ] **Req 6:** Choose the appropriate type of fishing rod and tackle you will be using. Have an adult review your gear.
- [ ] **Req 7:** Using what you have learned about fish and fishing equipment, spend at least one hour fishing following local guidelines and regulations.

## Day 1 — Plan, Habitat & Tackle Prep (60 min) — Reqs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 — low-coordination only

*Classroom with table stations. No water travel. Groups of 3.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *trustworthy* and *obedient* — follow local guidelines and buddy system), preview Catch the Big One as the fishing elective. Explain Day 1 = plan and prep in classroom, Day 2/auxiliary = real fishing for ≥1 hour. Confirm closed-toe shoes and sun protection for fishing day.

**Activity 1 — Make the Fishing Plan + SAFE Checklist (20 min) — Req 1 & Req 2**
- *Plan Your Fishing Adventure (10 min):* In teams of 3, Scouts choose a local venue (e.g., community pond, lake pier) and a target fish (bluegill, bass, catfish, trout) from fish cards. Record where, when, how to get there, and what fish they plan to catch on the Fishing Plan worksheet. Leader combines into one den plan that all subsequent requirements will follow — satisfies Req 1 and anchors Reqs 3–7 to that choice.
- *Safety First with SAFE (10 min):* Walk Scouting America SAFE Checklist (Supervision, Assessment, Fitness & Skill, Equipment & Environment) applied to this fishing plan — adult supervision and ratios, venue assessment (bank condition, current, weather), Scout ability, and equipment/environment (PFDs if on dock/boat, no swimming area separation). Each team writes one action per point and shares. Completes Req 2.

**Activity 2 — Where Fish Live + What to Bring (15 min) — Req 3 & Req 4**
- *Think Like A Fish / Fish Diorama Lite (8 min):* For the chosen fish, Scouts describe its environment — water type (pond/lake/stream), cover (weeds, structure, shade), depth, temperature, and food. Teams place fish cutouts on a habitat poster or build a mini diorama on paper plates. Each Scout can describe where that fish might be found — completes Req 3.
- *Group Tackle Box (7 min):* Teams list equipment and materials for this trip — rod/reel, line, hooks, bobbers, sinkers, bait, tackle box, pliers, measure tape, sunscreen, water, first-aid kit, trash bag. Sort into Must-Have vs. Nice-to-Have. Copy to personal list to bring/check on fishing day. Completes Req 4.

**Activity 3 — Knots & Gear Check (15 min) — Req 5 & Req 6**
- *Knot Stations (8 min):* Demo improved clinch (and Palomar as alternative) — thread line through hook eye, wrap 5–7 times, through loop, tighten, trim. Scouts practice first with paracord on large hooks, then with fishing line on demo hooks. Use nail clippers to trim. Each Scout ties at least one secure hook knot while adult verifies — completes Req 5.
- *Gear Check (7 min):* Display appropriate rod/tackle for the chosen fish (e.g., light spinning rod for bluegill). In groups of 3, Scouts match rod/reel/tackle to fish card and have an adult review their kit — leader checks knots, line, bobber/sinker placement, and safe hook handling. Initial each worksheet — completes Req 6.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap plan, SAFE, habitat, tackle, knot, and gear approval. Homework: with parent/guardian, confirm fishing day transportation, review local fishing guidelines handout, gather personal gear if owned, and check weather-appropriate clothing. Emphasize buddy system and giving space to nearby anglers.

## Day 2 — Fishing Day (60 min lines-in-water + 30 min buffer) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Off-site pond/lake bank or pier. Buddy system, active adult supervision, and SAFE in effect. If no fishable water at den site, run this as auxiliary pack family fishing day — same program.*

**Opening on the Bank (10 min)**
Buddy tags/attendance, AHMR check, sunscreen and water, venue rules and local guidelines review (licenses/permits on hand, catch limits, catch-and-release procedure, no fishing where people swim). Assign buddy pairs and bank spots with spacing; adults at 1:3. Review casting safety arc and hook handling.

**Activity 1 — Catch the Big One: ≥1 Hour Fishing (60 min) — Req 7**
- Lines in water for at least 60 minutes following local guidelines and regulations. Adults coach casting, baiting, bobber adjustment, and fish handling; pliers for hook removal and measuring tape for size. Scouts practice patience, re-baiting, and safe fish release (wet hands, gentle hold, quick return). Rotate coaching so each Scout gets 1:1 help. Leader tracks time — do not count travel/setup toward the hour. Buddy pairs stay within sight of adult; no Scout alone on bank or pier. If fishing from dock/boat, verify PFD rules per Guide to Safe Scouting. Completes Req 7.

**Closing on the Bank (5 min + travel buffer)**
Pack out all line/trash (line is wildlife hazard), clean gear, release or properly care for kept fish per local rules, buddy check, and circle reflection: What habitat did we fish? What knot/tackle worked? What would we do differently next time? Thank host/venue and adults. Record completions; plan catch-and-release photo board for next den meeting if desired.

## Auxiliary Day (if needed)

**Auxiliary Pack Family Fishing Day (60–90 min) — REQUIRED if Day 2 was not held at fishable water, or as make-up.**

Treat as the official 3rd day outside regular den time: invite pack families, keep den Scouts in buddy pairs under den leader supervision for the counted 60-min fishing block (families fish adjacent, not intermixed, so buddy checks remain clean). Same logistics as Day 2 — venue permission, licenses/permits checked, weather forecast, buddy system, adult ratios, first-aid kit, trash bags, and ample water/sunscreen. Schedule a 90-min window total (15 check-in/rules + 60 fishing + 15 clean-up/reflection). Scouts who missed Day 2 complete Req 7 here with parent verification and adult review; no classroom substitute.

## Materials Summary

Fishing Plan + Tackle Box worksheets, fish environment cards/habitat poster or paper plates, knot cards, paracord practice line + fishing line + demo hooks + bobbers/sinkers, nail clippers/scissors, fishing rods (3–5 shared) + tackle boxes + bait (day-before purchase), bucket/net/stringer, measuring tape, pliers, sunscreen, water bottles, hand sanitizer, paper towels, trash bags for line, whistles, attendance/buddy sheet, AHMR and permission/license folder, first-aid kit, pop-up shade if no cover.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and on the bank. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines; follow local fishing regulations that supersede Scouting America practices. Fish only with proper adult supervision, venue permission, and buddy system — Scouts must remain visible to buddy and adult. Check state license/permit requirements for youth and adults and keep documents on hand; never fish in swim areas, in thunderstorms, or in poor weather. Provide continuous, qualified adult supervision at fixed bank/pier ratios; keep spacing between anglers, enforce casting safety zones, and handle hooks with care (barbless demo, pliers for removal). Bring sunscreen, water, and first-aid kit; review Digital Safety if Scouts researched venues online. Check AHMRs and weather before you go; pack out all line and trash.
