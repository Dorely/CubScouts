# Catch the Big One Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Make a plan to go fishing. Determine where you will go and what type of fish you plan to catch. All of the following requirements are to be completed based on your choice.
- [ ] **2.** Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist to plan what you need for your fishing experience.
- [ ] **3.** Describe the environment where the fish might be found.
- [ ] **4.** Make a list of the equipment and materials you will need to fish.
- [ ] **5.** Determine the best type of knot to tie your hook to your line and tie it.
- [ ] **6.** Choose the appropriate type of fishing rod and tackle you will be using. Have an adult review your gear.
- [ ] **7.** Using what you have learned about fish and fishing equipment, spend at least one hour fishing following local guidelines and regulations.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Fishing Preparation

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–6

Choose a fishing location and target fish, plan supplies, practice knots, and have an adult review gear.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm location access and local fishing requirements. Bring SAFE Checklist, fish/habitat information, paper, practice line, hooks, rods, tackle, and bait examples. Add diorama or game materials for the chosen habitat activity.

### Day 2 — Fishing Outing

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 7

Spend at least one hour fishing.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm the meeting point, equipment sharing, adult help, and family transport. Match tackle and bait to the chosen fish.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Catch the Big One Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Catch_the_Big_One_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/catch-the-big-one/)

### Requirement 1

- **Plan Your Fishing Adventure**: Cub Scouts plan their fishing adventure.

### Requirement 2

- **Safety First**: Cub Scouts use Scouting America SAFE checklist for their fishing adventure.

### Requirement 3

- **Fish Diorama**: Cub Scouts create a fish environment diorama.
- **Tag, You're Caught!**: Cub Scouts learn what different fish like to eat and their environments by playing a tag-like game.
- **Think Like A Fish**: Cub Scouts describe fish environments and where they might be found.

### Requirement 4

- **Group Tackle Box**: Cub Scouts learn what equipment should go into a tackle box.

### Requirement 5

- **Knot Stations**: Cub Scouts practice fishing knots by doing stations around the meeting space.

### Requirement 6

- **Gear Check**: Cub Scouts have their fishing gear checked by an adult leader.

### Requirement 7

- **Catch the Big One**: Cub Scouts go fishing for at least one hour.
