# Chef's Knife Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min (+ auxiliary if needed) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **PREREQUISITE — SCHEDULE EARLY IN YEAR:** Chef's Knife is the **prerequisite for ANY Webelos knife use** during a Cub Scout activity. Even if a Scout earned Whittling as a Bear, they must earn Chef's Knife as a Webelos before using any knife in den/pack activities. Schedule this adventure early.
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — location must permit knives, sharp kitchen knives + cutting boards, and 1:3 supervision must be booked before Day 1.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Review `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Chefs_Knife_Adventure.md`, watch Cub Scout Knife Safety Adventures video, confirm meeting location permits knives (kitchen/community room). Print Cub Scout Knife Safety Rules sheets, safety circle mats/floor tape, and Knife Safety Rules crossword/promise cards. Send parent notice with prerequisite status, allergy/dietary questionnaire (fruit/salsa Day 2), closed-toe shoes, and AHMR review prompt. Confirm at least 3 adults for Day 2 (1:3 ratio) and that knives will be sharp/clean and in good condition.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** **Book before Day 1 if possible.** Reserve kitchen-permitted room with washable tables, hand-washing sink, and trash/compost access. Source sharp kitchen knives (chef's 6–8", paring — 3–4 sets), stable cutting boards (3–4), knife guards/blocks for storage, produce for fruit salad + salsa, bowls/serving spoons, soap/towels, and first-aid kit. Pre-wash produce; stage allergy-safe alternative (plain fruit vs. salsa with onion/cilantro/lime/jalapeño separated). Confirm allergy plan — check AHMRs, ask about religious/cultural dietary concerns, confirm EpiPen location and that at least one other adult can administer, review anaphylaxis and food allergy Safety Moments. Sharpen/check knives night before; do not bring dull or damaged knives.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Printed Knife Safety Rules, safety circle floor markers/tape, attendance sheet, demonstration dull/training knife for Day 1, sharp kitchen knives (3–4 sets, chef + paring), cutting boards (3–4), knife blocks/guards, produce — melons, pineapple, berries, apples, bananas for fruit salad; tomatoes, onion, cilantro, lime, bell pepper for salsa (+ allergy alternatives), bowls, serving spoons, paper towels, soap, disposable gloves, trash bags, cleaning wipes, first-aid kit, printed fruit salad/salsa recipe cards, take-home safety pledge sheets.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** **NO auxiliary required.** Both requirements complete in den meetings. Optional pack-family cooking night can be added for fun/practice but is not required to finish the adventure. Highlight prerequisite status at every family communication so leaders do not plan later knife activities before this is signed off.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Chefs_Knife_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Read, understand, and promise to follow the "Cub Scout Knife Safety Rules."
- [ ] **Req 2:** Demonstrate the knife safety circle.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Demonstrate that you know how to care for and use a kitchen knife safely.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Choose the correct cooking knife and demonstrate how to properly slice, dice, and mince.

## Day 1 — Safety Rules & Safety Circle (60 min) — low-coordination Reqs only

*Classroom. No sharp knives. Groups of 3 for practice.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *obedient* — follow safety rules every time). Preview Chef's Knife as the **prerequisite** for all later Webelos knife activities. Explain Day 1 = rules + safety circle with no sharp knives, Day 2 = care + real cutting (fruit salad/salsa) with sharp kitchen knives at supervised stations. Review hand-washing and closed-toe shoes.

**Activity 1 — Knife Safety Rules & Promise (25 min) — Req 1**
- *Rules Introduction (10 min):* Present four core rules — **Stop** — no one within arm's reach; **Away** — cut away from fingers/body; **Sharp** — sharp clean knife is safe; **Store** — closed/sheath or knife block. Add kitchen specifics: dry hands, flat stable board, curl fingers (claw grip), carry tip-down with adult permission, ask before using. Leader demos with a dull/training knife and poster.
- *Crossword / Teach-Back (10 min):* In groups of 3, Scouts complete Knife Safety Rules crossword, then each Scout explains one rule aloud in own words to the group. Correct misunderstandings immediately.
- *Promise (5 min):* Each Scout reads, understands, and signs promise to follow Cub Scout Knife Safety Rules on card; leader initials. Collect cards for records. Completes Req 1 — every Scout has read, understood, and promised.

**Activity 2 — The Safety Circle (25 min) — Req 2**
- *Demonstration (8 min):* Leader shows safety circle — arm extended with knife, rotate 360° plus overhead, no person/object inside. Include sitting-at-table variant (chair + board). Emphasize checking behind before starting and re-checking if anyone moves.
- *Practice Rotation (12 min):* Groups of 3 rotate through floor markers with training/dull knife — each Scout establishes circle, calls "Clear?" and waits for "Clear!" from others, demonstrates hold and "knives closed/stored when not in use." Adults correct reach and foot placement. Each Scout demonstrates at least twice, once standing and once seated at table.
- *Scenario Challenge (5 min):* Quick "what if?" — someone walks into your circle, board wobbles, knife drops — Scouts name the safe action (stop, re-establish circle, pick up only with adult nod). Leader verifies each Scout can demonstrate the safety circle without prompting. Completes Req 2.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap four rules and safety circle call-and-response. Preview Day 2 produce choices and allergy check results, confirm hand-wash + 1:3 stations, and that sharp knives appear only at supervised Day 2 boards. Hand out take-home safety pledge to review with parent/guardian. Collect attendance.

## Day 2 — Care, Slicing, Dicing & Mincing (60 min) — Advanced / Coordinated Reqs HERE

*Kitchen-permitted room with washable tables and sink. Adults 1:3. Sharp knives only at supervised boards.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Review four rules and safety circle, assign stations of 3 Scouts + 1 adult coach. Hand-wash, gloves if needed, safety reminder — one cutter at a time, claw grip, cut away, flat stable board, stop if distracted. Show where knives are stored (block/guard) and that Scouts only handle when adult says "go."

**Activity 1 — Care and Safe Use (15 min) — Req 3**
- *Care Demo (7 min):* Leader shows how to care for a kitchen knife — keep sharp/clean, hand-wash and dry immediately, store in block/sheath, never soak in sink, carry tip-down at side with sheath, check handle tightness.
- *Scout Return Demo (8 min):* At stations, each Scout demonstrates: check knife condition (sharp/clean), safe carry short distance, proper storage, and safe hand-off (handle to receiver, tip away, adults only for hand-off until verified). Each Scout also shows stable board (damp towel underneath), dry hands, and claw grip without cutting. Adults verify safe carry, storage, and grip for each Scout. Completes Req 3.

**Activity 2 — Choose the Knife & Cut: Slice, Dice, Mince (35 min) — Req 4**
Rotate within the group so each Scout uses the correct knife for each cut under adult line-of-sight:
- *Knife Choice (5 min):* Show chef's knife (slicing/dicing) vs. paring knife (small fruit, mincing herbs). Scouts name which knife for each task — slice (chef), dice (chef), mince (chef/paring). Leader corrects choices.
- *Slice (10 min):* With chef's knife on stable board, each Scout slices banana and strawberry or melon plank — straight even slices, curl fingers, tip stays on board, cut away from body. Adult coaches hand position and rhythm; one cutter at a time per station.
- *Dice (10 min):* Each Scout dices bell pepper/tomato or pineapple cube — first slices then cross-cuts into even dice. Emphasize anchor hand, controlled rock, re-tuck fingers.
- *Mince (10 min):* Each Scout minces cilantro/parsley or soft herb (or finely minces garlic if allergy allows) — rock blade, keep tip anchored, gather and re-mince. Alternative herb if cilantro allergy.

Combine cuts into two shared bowls: **Fruit Salad** and **Mild Salsa.** Leader verifies each Scout demonstrated correct knife choice and proper slice, dice, and mince technique at least once. Scouts who finish early wash boards, dry knives with adult help, and return knives to block. Taste only after all knives stored and hands washed; honor allergy alternatives.

**Closing (5 min)**
Knives counted, cleaned, and stored in blocks — leader confirms count. Circle reflection: which cut felt hardest, which knife for which job, and how care keeps knives safe. Review "ask adult before any knife use" and prerequisite status — Scouts now have privilege to use knives at future den activities with adult supervision. Thank adult coaches and note remaining adventure sign-offs.

## Auxiliary Day (if needed)

**No auxiliary required.** If a Scout misses Day 2, schedule a 30–45 min makeup at the same kitchen-permitted location with 1:3 supervision, same knife/board/setup, allergy plan, and AHMR review — do not sign off Req 3 or Req 4 without live supervised demonstration of care and slice/dice/mince with sharp kitchen knives. Retain promise cards and station checklists for absentees.

## Materials Summary

Knife Safety Rules sheets/crosswords, promise cards, floor tape/markers, dull/training knife, sharp kitchen knives (chef 6–8" + paring, 3–4 sets), stable cutting boards (3–4), knife blocks/guards, produce for fruit salad and salsa, bowls/serving spoons, disposable gloves, soap/towels, paper towels, cleaning wipes, trash bags, first-aid kit, recipe cards, attendance sheet. Optional: damp towels for board stability, EpiPen location sheet.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines. Confirm meeting location permits kitchen knives before Day 1. Keep knives sharp and in good condition — dull knives slip — and store closed/in sheath or knife block when not in use. Enforce safety circle, Stop-Away-Sharp-Store, claw grip, cut away from body, dry hands, stable board on damp towel, and one cutter at a time with adult within arm's reach (1:3 ratio). Review Annual Health and Medical Records, Food Allergies Safety Moment, Keep Your Food Safe guide, anaphylaxis response, and ensure at least one other adult can administer an EpiPen if needed. Ask about religious/cultural dietary concerns and keep allergen ingredients separated. Wash hands and produce, keep food/drinks away from knife-storage area, and count knives before and after. Have first-aid kit and incident plan on hand; supervise continuously and follow the most restrictive local regulation.
