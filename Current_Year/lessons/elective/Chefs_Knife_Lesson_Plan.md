# Chef's Knife Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Read, understand, and promise to follow the "Cub Scout Knife Safety Rules."
- [ ] **2.** Demonstrate the knife safety circle.
- [ ] **3.** Demonstrate that you know how to care for and use a kitchen knife safely.
- [ ] **4.** Choose the correct cooking knife and demonstrate how to properly slice, dice, and mince.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Kitchen Knife Skills

**Approximate time:** 75–90 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–4

Review the knife rules and circle, demonstrate care, and practice slicing, dicing, and mincing while preparing food.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm a food-preparation location and enough workstations and adult help. Bring the rules crossword, appropriate kitchen knives, cutting boards, care/cleanup supplies, bowls, and ingredients for the chosen fruit-salad or salsa activity. Choose ingredients that support all three cuts.

### Second Practice Session

**Approximate time:** 45–60 minutes · **Requirements:** 3, 4

Give Scouts additional turns for knife care and food preparation when equipment is shared.

**Prep and materials:** Keep the same workstation setup and prepare fresh ingredients. Schedule this adventure before later Webelos activities involving knife use.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Chef's Knife Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Chefs_Knife_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/chefs-knife/)

- **Knife Safety Rules Crossword**: Cub Scouts learn and explain the knife safety rules.

### Requirement 2

- **Webelos Knife Safety Circle**: Cub Scouts practice the knife safety circle.

### Requirement 3

- **Webelos Kitchen Knife Care and Use**: Cub Scouts demonstrate proper care of a kitchen knife.

### Requirement 4

- **Make Fruit Salad**: Cub Scouts practice their knife skills by making fruit salad.
- **Make Salsa**: Cub Scouts practice their knife skills by making salsa.
