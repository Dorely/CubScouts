# Art Explosion Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Create a piece of art by exploring drawing techniques using pencils.
- [ ] **2.** Using a digital image, explore the effect of filters by changing an image using different editing or in-camera techniques.
- [ ] **3.** Create a piece of art using paint as your medium.
- [ ] **4.** Create a piece of art combining at least two media.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Drawing and Digital Images

**Approximate time:** 60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1, 2

Explore pencil techniques and experiment with digital-image filters.

**Prep and materials:** Bring pencils, erasers, paper, drawing examples, and devices with images and editing filters ready. Plan device turns.

### Day 2 — Paint and Mixed Media

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 3, 4

Create a painting and a piece combining at least two media.

**Prep and materials:** Choose painting and mixed-media activities. Gather paint, paper, brushes or alternative tools, water containers, and collage or sculpture supplies. Cover tables and arrange drying and take-home space.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Art Explosion Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Art_Explosion_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/art-explosion/)

### Requirement 1

- **Art in the Out of Doors**: Cub Scouts can get inspiration by going outside to look at nature, architecture, weather, and wildlife.
- **Drawing the Artist**: Cub Scouts learn the art of drawing a self-portrait.
- **The Style of Art**: Cub Scouts draw in a specific artist's style.

### Requirement 2

- **Say Cheese**: Cub Scouts experiment with digital photography and editing.

### Requirement 3

- **No Brushes Needed**: Cub Scouts explore painting without using traditional paint brushes.
- **Watercolor**: Cub Scouts make a watercolor painting.

### Requirement 4

- **3D Sculpture**: Cub Scout create a mixed medium 3D sculpture using wood, wire, paint, and beads.
- **Mixed Medium Madness game**: Cub Scouts will create a completely original work of art with random art mediums and supplies.
