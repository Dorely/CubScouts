# Art Explosion Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Elective) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min (+ auxiliary if needed) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — LOW: tablets/phones, editing app, photo permissions, and art supplies must be confirmed before Day 2.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Print drawing technique handouts (shading, cross-hatch, perspective), paint technique cards (watercolor vs. acrylic vs. finger paint), and supply stations guide. Gather pencils (HB, 2B, 4B), erasers, sharpeners, sketch paper, watercolor/acrylic paints, palettes, cups, smocks, newspaper/table covers, and paper towels. Review Craft Tips video (2 min 34 sec). Cover tables and set drying area with names.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** Confirm photo permission for every Scout (signed parent form); brief Digital Safety and Online Scouting Activities for leaders. Test tablets/phones or laptops (1 per 2–3 Scouts, 3–4 devices total), install free editing app (e.g., Snapseed, Photos, Canva) and offline filter options; charge devices and confirm Wi-Fi or offline mode. Pre-load 2–3 practice photos per device if Scouts will not use their own images. Gather mixed-media bins for Req 4 (wood scraps, wire, beads, tissue paper, glue, scissors, paint remnants). Invite extra adult (1 per 3 Scouts) for paint/mixed-media supervision.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Sketch paper/drawing pads, pencil sets (HB/2B/4B), erasers, sharpeners, watercolor or acrylic paints, brushes (varied sizes), water cups, palettes or paper plates, smocks/aprons, newspaper/table covers, drying rack or line + clips, tablets/phones/laptops with editing app, photo permission forms, Digital Safety handout, mixed-media supplies (wood pieces, wire, beads, fabric scraps, tissue paper, glue sticks, tacky glue, scissors), baby wipes, trash bags.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** None needed — fully den-based. Both meetings run in classroom. Optional auxiliary: gallery display at a pack meeting (5–10 min show-and-tell, OPTIONAL) — not required to complete the adventure.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/elective/Art_Explosion_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Create a piece of art by exploring drawing techniques using pencils.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Using a digital image, explore the effect of filters by changing an image using different editing or in-camera techniques.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Create a piece of art using paint as your medium.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Create a piece of art combining at least two media.

---

## Day 1 — Pencil & Paint Studio (60 min) — Reqs 1 & 3 — low-coordination only

*Classroom with covered tables and drying area. Groups of 3. No devices needed.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *creative* and *cheerful*), preview Art Explosion as four art methods. Explain Day 1 = pencils and paint (hands-on), Day 2 = digital filters and mixed media. Review craft safety: smocks, wash hands, keep paint off floors, ask before sharing tools.

**Activity 1 — Drawing with Pencils (25 min) — Req 1**
- *Technique Mini-Lesson (8 min):* Demo 3 pencil techniques — shading (light vs. heavy pressure), cross-hatching, and contour/outdoor sketching. Show artist-style examples (self-portrait, nature, architecture). Each Scout chooses a subject.
- *Create (17 min):* At tables of 3, Scouts create a pencil piece on sketch paper using at least two techniques. Encourage "Art in the Out of Doors" inspiration — set up still-life (leaves, pinecones, building blocks) or let Scouts sketch from window. Leader and adults circulate to coach technique, not correctness. Each Scout signs and dates work; set aside to avoid smudging. Completes Req 1.

**Activity 2 — Paint Exploration (25 min) — Req 3**
- *No Brushes? No Problem (5 min):* Offer brush and non-brush options — cotton swabs, sponge dabs, finger paint, or traditional brush — Scouts pick one. Show watercolor wash vs. thicker paint demo on scrap paper.
- *Create (20 min):* Scouts paint a new piece (not over the pencil piece) using paint as the medium. Themes: weather, wildlife, or abstract emotion — emphasize "no right answer." Keep water cups stable (one per 2 Scouts), teach rinse-between-colors. Adults help with smocks and spill control. Move finished paintings to drying line with name clips. Completes Req 3.

**Closing (5 min)**
Gallery walk — each group of 3 shares one thing they tried. Recap pencil techniques and paint choices. Homework: with parent permission, pick a favorite photo to bring (or use den-provided image) for digital filters Day 2; confirm permission form returned. Collect and store Day 1 art flat for Day 2 display.

---

## Day 2 — Filters & Mixed Media Mash-Up (60 min) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Classroom with device stations and mixed-media tables. Digital Safety brief first.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, recap Day 1 pencil and paint pieces, introduce Day 2 goals. Brief Digital Safety: use only approved photos, no sharing online without parent permission, ask before photographing others, keep devices on task. Confirm photo permissions on file.

**Activity 1 — Digital Filters Lab (25 min) — Req 2**
- *Say Cheese Demo (7 min):* Leader shows one original image and 4–5 filtered versions (black-and-white, sepia, high contrast, blur, vibrant) on projector or one tablet. Explain in-camera vs. editing filters and how they change mood/emphasis.
- *Explore Stations (18 min):* In groups of 2–3 per device (3–4 devices total), Scouts open the same starter image and apply at least 4 different filters/edits, saving before/after versions. Each Scout narrates which filter they liked and why (e.g., "sepia makes it feel older"). If Scouts brought personal photos with permission, allow one personal edit as a second round. Leader verifies each Scout changed an image with multiple techniques. Completes Req 2.

**Activity 2 — Mixed-Media Creation (25 min) — Req 4**
- *Mixed Medium Madness (5 min):* Show 3D sculpture example combining at least two media (e.g., wood + paint + beads, or paper + wire + fabric). Rule: must use ≥2 distinct media in one piece.
- *Create (20 min):* At pre-set bins, groups of 3 create an original mixed-media piece — sculpture, collage, or assembled art. Options: wood scrap base + wire + paint + beads; tissue collage + paint; cardboard relief + fabric. Adults supervise glue and scissor use, enforce safe tool handling. Each Scout contributes to own piece or a shared group piece with individual identifiable sections. Completes Req 4.

**Closing (5 min)**
Final gallery: display Day 1 and Day 2 art together; each Scout points out pencil, paint, filtered image, and mixed-media work. Thank adults, remind Scouts to take art home after drying. Pack meeting display is optional — invite families to view at next pack gathering.

## Auxiliary Day (if needed)

None needed — fully den-based. All four requirements complete in the two den meetings above. If you want to celebrate, add an **optional** 10-minute pack-meeting gallery (tape art to boards, display tablets with filtered images on slideshow) — do not schedule a separate auxiliary day to earn the adventure.

## Materials Summary

Drawing pads/sketch paper, pencil sets (HB, 2B, 4B), erasers, sharpeners, still-life objects, watercolor/acrylic paints, brushes and non-brush tools (sponges, swabs), water cups, palettes/paper plates, smocks/aprons, newspaper/table covers, drying line + clips, tablets/phones/laptops with editing app + chargers, projector (optional), pre-loaded practice photos, photo permission forms, Digital Safety handout, mixed-media bins (wood scraps, wire, beads, fabric, tissue, glue sticks/tacky glue, scissors), baby wipes, trash bags, attendance sheet.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines. Before any craft, watch Craft Tips. Review Digital Safety and Online Scouting Activities before Req 2 — use only permission-approved images, no online posting without parent approval, and keep devices supervised. Ensure scissors and tacky glue are age-appropriate and supervised; provide smocks and cover tables for paint. Keep water cups stable to prevent spills, and supervise hand washing after paint/glue. Be aware of allergies to latex or art materials and have a first-aid kit on hand.
