# Webelos Walkabout Lesson Plan

**Rank:** Webelos (Required) | **Estimated Time:** 2 den meetings × 60 min + auxiliary 2-mile hike (90–120 min) | **Group:** 7–9 scouts, classroom + outdoor flex

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ADVANCE PREP — READ FIRST**
> **Advanced planning needed? YES — 2-mile route, weather, and first-aid guest must be booked before Day 2; auxiliary hike day required.**
> - **Before Meeting 1:** Print Cub Scout Six Essentials cards, weather-appropriate clothing checklist, *Plan A Route* / *Digital Map* worksheets, blank route maps of local park/trail, SAFE Checklist handout, Leave No Trace Principles for Kids and Outdoor Code cards. Complete leader *Hazardous Weather* training (my.scouting.org) and watch *Weather Related Safety* moment video (1:48) yourself. Send parent notice with hike date options, transportation plan, and AHMR reminder.
> - **Before Meeting 2 (Advanced):** Scout and confirm 2-mile route (park/trail permission, parking, restrooms, shade, water, cell coverage, turnaround points). Check AHMRs for every Scout and adult on the walk; confirm EpiPen plan and that at least one other adult knows administration. Confirm first-aid guest (Scouts BSA troop, first responder, nurse/EMT) for Req 5 demos — brief them on blister, sprained ankle, sunburn, dehydration/heat illness. Confirm buddy pairs, adult ratio, and Six Essentials per Scout. Pull weather forecast 3 days and 1 day before hike; set go/no-go and rain date. Arrange transportation and permissions.
> - **Shopping & Materials (both meetings):** Printed Six Essentials posters/cards, local trail maps (paper + digital map access), weather forecast printouts, SAFE Checklist posters, first-aid demo supplies (moleskin, gauze, elastic wrap, sunscreen sample, water bottles, instant cold pack), Leave No Trace and Outdoor Code cards, whistles, attendance/buddy-tag sheets, route-risk worksheet, first-aid kit, trash bags for LNT.
> - **Auxiliary / Pack activity?** YES — Req 6 two-mile walk is an auxiliary pack/den hike day (not a classroom meeting). Plan as 3rd auxiliary day: 90–120 min on trail + Req 7 reflection after. Day 1 and Day 2 are indoor prep; the walk itself must be outside with buddy system and active adult supervision. Pack may combine with family participation.
> - **Reference:** `../../reference/Webelos/required/Webelos_Walkabout_Adventure.md`

## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** Prepare for a 2-mile walk outside. Gather your Cub Scout Six Essentials and weather appropriate clothing and shoes.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Plan a 2-mile route for your walk.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Check the weather forecast for the time of your planned 2-mile walk.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Review the four points of Scouting America SAFE Checklist and how you will apply them on your 2-mile walk.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Demonstrate first aid for each of the following events that could occur on your 2-mile walk: blister, sprained ankle, sunburn, dehydration and heat related illness.
- [ ] **Req 6:** With your den, pack, or family, go on your 2-mile walk while practicing the Leave No Trace Principles for Kids and Outdoor Code.
- [ ] **Req 7:** After your 2-mile walk, discuss with your den what went well and what you would do differently next time.

## Day 1 — Hike Prep: Essentials, Route, Weather, SAFE (60 min) — low-coordination requirements only

*Classroom + outdoor flex for relay. No guest, no trail travel — all table and floor activities.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *brave* and *clean*), preview Webelos Walkabout as the outdoor adventure. Explain Day 1 = plan, Day 2 = first-aid skills, Auxiliary = actual 2-mile walk.

**Activity 1 — Six Essentials Relay (20 min) — Req 1**
- *Cub Scout Essential Six Review* (8 min): Present Six Essentials (filled water bottle, flashlight, whistle, first-aid kit, sun protection, trail food) plus weather-appropriate clothing/shoes. Pass real items around; Scouts check own daypack mock-up.
- Relay (12 min): Teams of 3–4 race to pack correct essentials from mixed table (include distractors like game controller, soda). Judge for completeness. Each Scout verifies they can gather essentials before the hike. Completes Req 1 prep portion.

**Activity 2 — Plan the Route & Check the Weather (20 min) — Req 2 & Req 3**
- *Plan A Route / Digital Map* (12 min): On paper and projected digital map, Scouts plot a 2-mile loop (e.g., 1 mile out + 1 mile back) with start, rest stop, water point, and bail-out. Measure distance, note surface and elevation, mark hazards (road crossings, water). Leader records leader-scouted primary and backup routes.
- *What's The Forecast?* (8 min): Pull forecast for planned hike window — temperature, wind, precipitation chance, heat index/ wind chill. Scouts decide clothing layer and water quantity. Record go/no-go criteria and contingency date. Completes Req 2 planning and Req 3 forecast check (rechecked Day 2 and hike morning).

**Activity 3 — SAFE Checklist for Your Walk (10 min) — Req 4**
- *Safety First*: Walk four points — Supervision, Assessment, Fitness & Skill, Equipment & Environment — and apply to this hike: adult ratio + buddy system, AHMR/EpiPen, route assessment, weather, essentials. Scouts write one action per point on poster.
- Buddy assignment preview and Outdoor Code teaser.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap essentials, route, weather, SAFE. Homework: gather Six Essentials with parent/guardian, break in hiking shoes, bring water bottle Day 2. Confirm hike date and transportation needs with families.

## Day 2 — First Aid + Trail Ready (60 min) — Advanced / coordinated requirements HERE

*Classroom with floor space for demos. Guest present. Final go/no-go prep.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Roll call by buddies, re-check weather update for hike window, confirm AHMR/EpiPen plan and buddy pairs. Review Leave No Trace Principles for Kids and Outdoor Code on cards.

**Activity 1 — Trail First Aid Stations (30 min) — Req 5**
Guest-led (Scouts BSA troop, first responder, or nurse/EMT) — 5 stations × 5–6 min, groups of 2–3 rotate:
1. **Blister:** prevention (fit, socks, moleskin), care — clean, cover, do not pop, pad.
2. **Sprained ankle:** RICE, elastic wrap demo, when to stop walking and get adult help.
3. **Sunburn:** prevention (sunscreen, hat, shade), cool compress, hydration, when to seek care.
4. **Dehydration & heat illness:** signs (thirst, headache, nausea, confusion), sip water, shade, cool, rest, alert adult — heat exhaustion vs. heat stroke.
5. **Scenario wrap:** "What would you do on the trail?" — Scouts demonstrate one skill each back to group.

Each Scout performs at least one hands-on demo; guest verifies. Completes Req 5.

**Activity 2 — Final Hike Rehearsal (20 min) — Req 4, Req 6 prep**
- Reconfirm route (8 min): Show final map with distance, rest stops, bathrooms, water, emergency access. Review LNT for Kids — plan ahead, stick to trail, trash your trash, leave what you find, be kind.
- Pack check + buddy system (7 min): Whistle test, Six Essentials shakedown, buddy-tag practice, active supervision plan.
- Weather & SAFE final (5 min): Re-apply SAFE four points to tomorrow's conditions; set meeting time/place, check-in procedure, and turn-back triggers (lightning, heat index, fatigue).

**Closing (5 min)**
Thank guest, hand out hike info sheet (map, time, what to wear/bring, emergency contact). Explain Req 7 reflection will happen right after the hike. Collect permission and transportation forms.

## Auxiliary Day — The Two-Mile Walk + Reflection (90–120 min)

*Off-site trail or park. Den, pack, or family — buddy system and active adult supervision required. This day is mandatory to complete the adventure.*

**Logistics (leader pre-check morning of):** Final weather check, AHMR/first-aid kit/EpiPen on hand, attendance + buddy tags, Six Essentials per Scout, water and sun protection verified, trail permission and parking confirmed, emergency plan shared with all adults.

**Program:**
- **The Two-Mile Walk (60–90 min) — Req 6:** Hike planned 2-mile route at Scout pace with rest stops. Practice Leave No Trace Principles for Kids (stay on trail, pack out trash, respect wildlife) and Outdoor Code (be clean, careful with fire, considerate, conservation-minded). Leader narrates application at stops. Buddy system enforced; no Scout alone. Adults provide active supervision.
- **Stop-Start-Continue Reflection (15 min) — Req 7:** Immediately after hike, circle up with water/snack: What went well? What would we do differently next time? (gear, pace, weather, LNT). Capture notes on poster to share with den record.

If a Scout misses the auxiliary hike, they may complete Req 6 with family on the same approved route and return with parent verification, then join a later den reflection to complete Req 7 — do not sign off Req 6 without the walk.

## Materials Summary

Six Essentials poster/cards and sample kit (water bottle, flashlight, whistle, first-aid kit, sunscreen, trail food), weather forecast printouts, local trail maps (paper + digital), SAFE Checklist handout, Leave No Trace and Outdoor Code cards, first-aid station supplies (moleskin, gauze, elastic wrap, sunscreen, water bottles, cold pack), whistles, buddy tags, attendance sheet, route-risk worksheet, first-aid kit, trash bags, hike info sheet.

## Safety Notes

Use SAFE Checklist at every meeting and on the hike. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines. Complete *Hazardous Weather* training and *Weather Related Safety* moment before the walk; check Scouting America Annual Health and Medical Record for all Cub Scouts and adults and note EpiPen/ allergy plans — ensure at least one other adult can administer. Use buddy system, active adult supervision, and no solo walking. Verify weather forecast and have contingency date; know heat- and cold-related illness signs. Review Digital Safety for any online mapping. Carry first-aid kit, trash bags, and ample water; follow rest, shade, and turn-back triggers.
