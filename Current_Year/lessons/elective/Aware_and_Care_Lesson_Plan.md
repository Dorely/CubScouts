# Aware and Care Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Do an activity that shows the challenges of a being visually impaired.
- [ ] **2.** Do an activity that shows the challenges of being hearing impaired.
- [ ] **3.** Explore barriers to access.
- [ ] **4.** Meet someone who has a disability or someone who works with people with disabilities about what obstacles they must overcome and how they do it.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Awareness and Access

**Approximate time:** 60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–3

Combine vision and hearing activities with an exploration of access barriers.

**Prep and materials:** Choose an activity for each requirement. Gather drawing materials, familiar objects, vision-obscuring materials, headphones or animal prompts, and objects for reach activities; arrange outdoor access if needed.

### Day 2 — Guest or Visit

**Approximate time:** 45–60 minutes plus travel for a visit · **Requirements:** 4

Meet someone with a disability or someone who works with people with disabilities.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm the guest or rehabilitation-facility visit, preferred format, access needs, and Scout questions. A short guest conversation can also join Day 1 in a 75–90-minute meeting.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Aware and Care Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Aware_and_Care_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/aware-and-care/)

### Requirement 1

- **Can We Get There?**: Navigate an obstacle course with both eyes covered one eye covered, and blurred vision.
- **Can You Draw It?**: Draw a picture while vision is obscured.
- **I Can Do It**: Pour a cup of water and eat a snack without using vision.
- **What Is This?**: Identify common household items without using vision.

### Requirement 2

- **Listen Carefully**: Play a game of Simon Says wearing noise canceling headphones.
- **Story Time**: Tell a story using American Sign Language.
- **What Animal**: Cub Scout guess the animal without talking.

### Requirement 3

- **Can You Reach It?**: Cub Scouts experience what it is like to have an object out of reach.
- **Outdoors Barriers to Access**: Cub Scouts go outdoors looking for barriers to access.

### Requirement 4

- **How Do People Help?**: Visit a rehabilitation facility.
- **How Do You Do It?**: Invite a guest to speak about disabilities.
- **Jobs For Dogs**: Have a person who trains service dogs or has a service dog visit the den.
