# Champions for Nature Webelos Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Discover the four components that make up a habitat: food, water, shelter, space.
- [ ] **2.** Pick an animal that is currently threatened or endangered to complete requirements 3, 4, and 5.
- [ ] **3.** Identify the characteristics that classify an animal as a threatened or endangered species.
- [ ] **4.** Explore what caused this animal to be threatened or endangered.
- [ ] **5.** Research what is currently being done to protect the animal.
- [ ] **6.** Participate in a conservation service project.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Habitats and an Animal Study

**Approximate time:** 60–75 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–4

Explore habitats, choose a threatened or endangered animal, and learn why it is at risk.

**Prep and materials:** Gather habitat-observation materials and current information on potential animals. Prepare the habitat game and charades materials.

### Day 2 — Protection and Service

**Approximate time:** 60–90 minutes · **Requirements:** 5, 6

Research protection work, make the animal-protection comic, and complete a small conservation project.

**Prep and materials:** Bring paper, pencils, and current information on the selected animal. Confirm the project partner and gather the requested building, planting, or cleanup supplies.

### Separate Conservation Activity

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus travel · **Requirements:** 6

Join a larger conservation event, installation, or waterway cleanup.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm the partner, site, supplies, transport, and project arrangements.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Champions for Nature Webelos Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Champions_for_Nature_Webelos_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/champions-for-nature-webelos/)

### Requirement 1

- **Local Habitat Hunt Game**: Cub Scouts learn about habitats by playing the Habitat Hunt game.
- **Local Microhabitat Study**: Cub Scouts learn about microhabitats.

### Requirement 2

- **Pick an Animal**: Cub Scouts pick a threatened or endangered animal to study.

### Requirement 3

- **Think Like a Conservationist**: Cub Scouts learn about animals that are threatened or endangered.

### Requirement 4

- **Endangered Animal Charades**: Cub Scouts learn why animals became endangered by playing charades.

### Requirement 5

- **Animal Protection Comic Strip**: Cub Scouts create a comic strip.

### Requirement 6

- **Air Bee n' Bee**: Cub Scouts build a bee hotel that will attract solitary bees and give them a safe home.
- **Build a Bat House**: Cub Scouts build bat houses.
- **Clean Waterways**: Participate in a waterway cleanup to remove aquatic trash and improve waterway health.
- **Endangered Species Service Project**: Cub Scouts participate in a service project to benefit a locally endangered species.
- **WOSM Earth Tribe**: Explore the World Organization for the Scouting Movement's conservation efforts.
