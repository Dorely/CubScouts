# Yo-yo Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** Learn the safety rules of using a yo-yo and always follow them.
- [ ] **2.** Discover how to find the proper yo-yo string length for you.
- [ ] **3.** Explain why it is important to have the correct string length and to be in the right location before throwing a yo-yo.
- [ ] **4.** Demonstrate how to properly string a yo-yo and how to create a slip knot.
- [ ] **5.** Conduct the pendulum experiment with a yo-yo. Explain what happens to the yo-yo when the string is longer.
- [ ] **6.** Show that you can properly wind a yo-yo.
- [ ] **7.** Attempt each of the following: gravity pull, sleeper, breakaway.

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Setup and Pendulum

**Approximate time:** 60 minutes · **Requirements:** 1–6

Review rules, adjust string length, practice stringing and winding, and conduct the pendulum experiment.

**Prep and materials:** Preview the official tutorials and try the experiment. Bring one yo-yo and string per Scout, spare strings, scissors, a marker, stopwatch, pencils, and the worksheet or handbook plus experiment visual.

### Day 2 — Trick Practice

**Approximate time:** 45–60 minutes · **Requirements:** 7; practice 2, 4, 6

Attempt gravity pull, sleeper, and breakaway, with time to revisit setup and winding.

**Prep and materials:** Preview the trick tutorials, bring yo-yos and spare strings, and arrange space and individual help.

### Family Practice

**Approximate time:** 15–20 minutes between meetings · **Requirements:** 2, 4, 6, 7

Practice stringing, winding, and tricks at home.

**Prep and materials:** Share tutorial links and arrange for Scouts to take their yo-yos home.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Yo-yo Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Yo_Yo_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/yo-yo/)

### Requirement 1

- **Yo... Yo... Be Safe**: Cub Scouts review the basic safety rules for using a yo-yo.

### Requirement 2

- **Aye String**: Cub Scouts find the proper length of string for a yo-yo.

### Requirement 3

- **Right Length**: Cub Scouts learn the importance of having the correct string length and how to select a safe location before throwing a yo-yo.

### Requirement 4

- **Oh Knots**: Cub Scouts learn how to properly string a yo-yo and how to create a slip knot.

### Requirement 5

- **Swinging**: Cub Scouts conduct the pendulum experiment with a yo-yo.

### Requirement 6

- **All Wound Up**: Cub Scouts show how to properly wind a yo-yo.

### Requirement 7

- **Yo-Yo Ninja**: Cub Scouts attempt each of the following yo-yo tricks: the gravity pull, the sleeper, and the breakaway.
