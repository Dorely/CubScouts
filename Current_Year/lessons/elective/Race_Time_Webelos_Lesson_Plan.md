## Requirements Checklist

- [ ] **Req 1:** With an adult, build either a Pinewood Derby(R) car or a Raingutter Regatta(TM) boat.
- [ ] **Req 2:** Learn the rules of the race for the vehicle chosen in requirement 1.
- [ ] **Req 3:** Explore the properties of friction and how it impacts your chosen vehicle.
- [ ] **Req 4:** Before the race, discuss with your den how you will demonstrate good sportsmanship during the race.
- [ ] **Req 5:** Participate in a Pinewood Derby(R) or a Raingutter Regatta(TM).

## Day 1 — Rules, Friction & Sportsmanship (60 min) — low-coordination Reqs only

*Classroom. No cutting/painting. Groups of 3.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Flag, Scout Oath & Law (focus on *cheerful* and *clean* — enjoy the race, keep the workspace clean). Preview Race Time as the propulsion and friction elective. Explain structure: Day 1 = rules + friction science + sportsmanship in the classroom; Day 2 = build day with adult help (bulk kits, tools, paint); Auxiliary = REQUIRED pack/district race on a real track/trough. Confirm den's single vehicle choice (car OR boat) for all Scouts.

**Activity 1 — Learn the Rules (15 min) — Req 2**
- *Rules Walk (8 min):* Present official rules on poster — Pinewood: 5-oz max, length/width/ground-clearance limits, wheel/axle must be kit-supplied, dry graphite only, no liquid lube, no moving weights; or Regatta: hull from kit, sail size/position limits, power by blowing through straw only (no touching boat). Emphasize check-in and disqualification traps (overweight, wet paint). Quiz with Jeopardy cards in threes — "What happens if you use oil instead of graphite?"
- *Apply (7 min):* Teams of 3 review a sample car/boat photo and spot 2 rule violations. Each Scout names one rule they will follow on their build. Completes Req 2 — each Scout has learned the race rules for the chosen vehicle.

**Activity 2 — Friction Lab (20 min) — Req 3**
- *Concept (5 min):* Define friction — force that opposes motion; propulsion (gravity on a sloped track, air from lungs on water, push) vs. friction (wheel/axle, track surface, water drag, air). Less friction = farther/faster if propulsion stays the same.
- *Tests (15 min):* Three stations rotating every 5 min — (A) Smooth vs. sandpaper ramp: roll same car/boat shape down each, measure distance/time; (B) Axle lube vs. dry: spin wheels or slide hull on felt vs. wax paper; (C) Weight/shape: compare heavy vs. light car or ballasted vs. unballasted hull. Each group records results and names which surface/lube reduces friction and why graphite/polish and smooth hulls help. Leader connects findings to build choices (sand/polish axles, smooth hull, weight at limit). Completes Req 3.

**Activity 3 — Sportsmanship Before the Race (15 min) — Req 4**
- *Discussion (8 min):* In groups of 3, list what good sportsmanship looks like — cheer for everyone, congratulate winners, help with repairs, accept results, thank volunteers, win/lose with grace (A Scout is Kind, Friendly, Courteous). Role-play one scenario per group: your car loses, friend's car breaks, you win — what do you say/do?
- *Den Pledge (7 min):* Den drafts a 4-line sportsmanship pledge to read at the auxiliary race; each Scout signs. Cover handling frustration and watching language. Completes Req 4 — each Scout has discussed and committed to good sportsmanship before the race.

**Closing (5 min)**
Recap rules, friction findings, and pledge. Preview Day 2 build: bring old shirt/smock, safety glasses required, adults handle saws — Scouts sand, assemble, and paint. Remind families: do not pre-build at home — Req 1 is built with an adult at the den build day — and the REQUIRED auxiliary race date/location will be announced Day 2.

## Day 2 — Build Day with an Adult (60 min) — Advanced / Coordinated Reqs HERE

*Workshop classroom with drop cloths, ventilation, and adult-supervised tool stations. Groups of 3, 1 adult per group.*

**Opening (5 min)**
Tool-safety briefing: safety glasses on, tie back hair, no loose sleeves, cut away from body, adult operates saw, Scouts sand/file/glue/paint, graphite outdoors or with cover, wash hands after. Confirm vehicle choice and that every build is with an adult partner — leader initials adult pairing.

**Activity 1 — Shape & Prep (20 min) — Req 1 part 1**
- *Design (5 min):* Each Scout sketches top/side view within official dimensions, marks weight target (Pinewood: 5 oz; Regatta: kit hull) and notes friction choices from Day 1 (smooth surfaces, polished axles/hull).
- *Cut & Sand (15 min):* Adults cut blanks at the saw station per Scout sketch; Scouts sand to shape with progressive grit, file edges, and dry-fit wheels/mast. Rotate through sanding while adults cut. Emphasize sanding direction and dust control (wipe, not blow). Leader checks each Scout has hands-on sanding/fitting.

**Activity 2 — Assemble & Finish (30 min) — Req 1 part 2**
- *Assembly (12 min):* Scouts glue axles/hull parts and attach wheels/sail with adult clamping help — no bare-hand saw work. For cars: insert axles straight, test roll on short board; for boats: true the mast/sail and ballast. Add graphite to axles (cars) with adult dispensing — or trim/set sails (boats).
- *Paint & Detail (18 min):* Scouts paint/decorate with 2–3 colors plus decals/numbers; adults manage ventilation and drying rack. While drying, each Scout completes a build card — who helped, what rule they followed, one friction-reducing step they took. Leader verifies each Scout personally built with adult help; wet builds go to rack — not raced today. Completes Req 1 for those with finished assembly (paint may finish at home with adult, but bulk build is credited today).

**Closing (5 min)**
Gallery of builds on the drying rack — each Scout names their vehicle and one rule they obeyed. Hand out auxiliary race packet — date/time, weigh-in window, pit-crew rules, sportsmanship pledge, what to bring (vehicle, safety glasses, repair kit). Remind: final paint/touch-up at home with parent only if needed; no weight cheating or added motors. Collect attendance and build cards.

## Auxiliary Day — REQUIRED Pack/District Race (60–90 min)

*Council/pack/district-coordinated Pinewood track (32-ft aluminum + electronic timer) or Raingutter trough (paired 10-ft gutters + water/hose). Check-in table, weigh-in scale, pit area, brackets, announcer, awards. Adult supervision continuous, SAFE Checklist in effect. This day is mandatory — Req 5 cannot be signed off without it.*

**Logistics (leader pre-check morning-of):** Confirm track/trough reservation, timer/power or water source, level tables, weigh-in scale calibrated, extension cord/hose, pit supplies (extra graphite, glue, tape, sandpaper, straws for Regatta), brackets seeded by den/age, volunteer roles (starter, judge, pit boss, announcer), first-aid kit, and AHMRs/permissions. Set go/no-go on weather if outdoors/trough. Bring den pledge poster and camera.

**Program:**
- **Check-In & Weigh-In (15 min):** Each Scout checks in, weighs/measures vehicle with official, and makes legal adjustments (add/remove weight, sand burr). Vehicles impounded after pass.
- **Race — Req 5 (45–60 min):** Run brackets/heats — Pinewood: gravity on sloped track; Regatta: blow through straw down water trough. Every Scout races at least 2–3 heats. Den recites sportsmanship pledge before first heat; Scouts practice cheering, congratulating, and helping. Leader logs participation for each Scout — finishing place does not matter, participation does.
- **Awards & Wrap (10 min):** Recognize finishers, best craftsmanship, and sportsmanship; thank volunteers; photos. Discuss one friction lesson visible in results (polished/smooth vs. rough).

**Make-up:** No classroom substitute for the race. If a Scout misses the pack/district auxiliary, they may complete Req 5 at a council/pack make-up race or scheduled time-trial event within the program year on the same official track/trough with brackets — do not sign off Req 5 on a hallway roll or at-home run.

## Safety Notes

Use Scouting America SAFE Checklist at every meeting and at the race. Review Guide to Safe Scouting and Age-Appropriate Guidelines — continuous qualified adult supervision, tool-safety training before use, safety glasses for all during cutting/sanding/painting, adults operate saws and dispense graphite, ensure ventilation for paint and no open flames, keep small parts and graphite away from mouths/eyes and wash hands after. Check AHMRs for allergy/medical needs and have first-aid kit accessible. Provide adequate adult coverage (1 per 3 Scouts on build day), enforce safe tool handling and clean workspace, and follow the most restrictive local regulation and site rules. At the auxiliary, control track/trough access, keep hands clear of moving cars/water, and manage crowd flow at start/finish lines.
