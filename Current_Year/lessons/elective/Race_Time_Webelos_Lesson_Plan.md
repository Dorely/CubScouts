# Race Time Webelos Lesson Plan

## Requirements

- [ ] **1.** With an adult, build either a Pinewood Derby(R) car or a Raingutter Regatta(TM) boat.
- [ ] **2.** Learn the rules of the race for the vehicle chosen in requirement 1.
- [ ] **3.** Explore the properties of friction and how it impacts your chosen vehicle.
- [ ] **4.** Before the race, discuss with your den how you will demonstrate good sportsmanship during the race.
- [ ] **5.** Participate in a Pinewood Derby(R) or a Raingutter Regatta(TM).

## Suggested Lesson Days

Start each den meeting with a **denner-led game** and involve the denner in setup, activities, and closing. Allow 5–10 minutes for the game within the meeting times below.

### Day 1 — Rules, Friction, and Sportsmanship

**Approximate time:** 60 minutes · **Requirements:** 2–4

Learn the chosen race’s rules, explore friction, and discuss sportsmanship with the den.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm the race and vehicle type. Bring the event rules, matching Jeopardy materials, and car/wheel or boat friction examples.

### Build Day or Family Build

**Approximate time:** 90–120 minutes plus finishing time · **Requirements:** 1

Build the car or boat with an adult.

**Prep and materials:** Gather kits, project tools, work surfaces, and finishing supplies. Share the event rules with families before building.

### Race Day

**Approximate time:** Allow 2–3 hours; use the event schedule · **Requirements:** 5

Participate in the Pinewood Derby or Raingutter Regatta.

**Prep and materials:** Confirm registration, check-in, transport, and track or regatta access. Bring the completed vehicle.

## Suggested Activities by Requirement

**Resources:** [Race Time Webelos Adventure](../../../reference/Webelos/elective/Race_Time_Webelos_Adventure.md) · [Official adventure and activity directions](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/race-time-webelos/)

### Requirement 1

- **Pinewood Derby(R) Car Build Day Webelos**: Den or pack provides a build day for Cub Scouts.
- **Raingutter Regatta(TM) Build Day Webelos**: Den or pack provides a build day for Cub Scouts.

### Requirement 2

- **Pinewood Derby(R) Rules Jeopardy**: Learn the rules of the Pinewood Derby(R) with a game of Jeopardy.
- **Raingutter Regatta(TM) Rules Jeopardy**: Learn the rules of the Raingutter Regatta(TM) with a game of Jeopardy.

### Requirement 3

- **Boat Friction**: Cub Scouts learn how friction impacts their boat.
- **Wheel Friction**: Cub Scouts learn how friction impacts their race car.

### Requirement 4

- **A Scout is Kind Webelos**: Cub Scouts discuss good sportsmanship and how to demonstrate it during their racing event.

### Requirement 5

- **Pinewood Derby(R) Race Webelos**: Conduct a Pinewood Derby(R) car race.
- **Raingutter Regatta(TM) Webelos**: Conduct a Raingutter Regatta(TM).
