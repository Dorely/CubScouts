# Webelos Parent Leader Selection Guide

Choose an adventure below. Each plan starts with the requirements, followed by suggested lesson days with approximate times and prep, then activities for each requirement. Bobcat and Stronger, Faster, Higher are completed.

## Calendar for Review

Print the current Google Doc linked from the [calendar source pointer](../calendar.google-doc.md). Use it for meeting dates, leads, and denners.

## Remaining Required Adventures

- [My Community](required/My_Community_Lesson_Plan.md): Voting and a federal-law timeline work well together. Arrange an elected official for a conversation; service may fit another meeting or a separate event.
- [My Family](required/My_Family_Lesson_Plan.md): Family activities using the official passport. A parent volunteer can coordinate distribution and returns.
- [My Safety](required/My_Safety_Lesson_Plan.md): Meeting-space review and natural-events worksheets fit den time. Household work belongs at home; the video activity includes parent/guardian permission and co-viewing.
- Webelos Walkabout — [Lesson 1: planning and first aid](required/Webelos_Walkabout_Lesson_1_Hike_Planning_and_First_Aid.md) and [Lesson 2: Oak Glen pack hike](required/Webelos_Walkabout_Lesson_2_Oak_Glen_Pack_Hike.md): Route planning, forecast, SAFE review, and first-aid practice at a den meeting, then the two-mile walk and reflection at the Oak Glen Adventure on Oct 10.

## Four Electives to Consider First

- [Aware and Care](elective/Aware_and_Care_Lesson_Plan.md): Hands-on awareness activities and exploration of access barriers. Arrange the required guest or visit; this is the main advance coordination need. Already assigned in the live calendar, so coordinate with that lead before changing plans.
- [Art Explosion](elective/Art_Explosion_Lesson_Plan.md): Pencil drawing, digital filters, painting, and mixed media. Best for a parent comfortable gathering art supplies; allow device turns, drying, and cleanup, possibly across two meetings.
- [Earth Rocks](elective/Earth_Rocks_Lesson_Plan.md): Rock examination, mineral testing, and crystal growing. Needs specific lab supplies and a trial run; crystals grow overnight or over about a week, depending on the chosen activity.
- [Modular Design](elective/Modular_Design_Lesson_Plan.md): Build, make instructions, have someone follow them, and rebuild. A practical choice when building pieces are available; add the video with parent/guardian permission. Larger models may need two meetings.

## Before Leading

- Confirm your date and denner against the live calendar. Coordinate the denner-led opening game and give the denner a role in setup, activities, and closing.
- Choose activities, read their official directions, and gather the materials listed for your lesson day.
- Tell families about home requirements, any parent participation or permission, supplies to bring, and how to report completion.
- Record which requirements each Scout completes and what remains for home or another activity.
- For Markdown printing, use the workspace-wide stylesheet setting `workspace.resource/styles/markdown.print.css`.
